Draft an employment agreement that works for any employer

Every business that hires needs an employment agreement. In the community you draft one in full, covering probation, notice and confidentiality, plus a remote work monitoring policy, so it suits a team in any city.

Clause 6. Termination and notice6.1 After probation, either party may end this Agreement by giving the other 30 days' written notice, or salary in lieu of notice. 6.2 The Company may end this Agreement without notice for gross misconduct, as defined in Schedule 1. 6.3 On leaving, the Employee shall return all Company property and documents.
This clause reads the same in Pune or Patna. Write it once, and write it well, and it serves employers wherever you live.
