Waiting for the firm to promote you

Your first fix: an NDA with its red flags found by AI

In the live sessions you use AI to find missing clauses and red flags in real agreements. On NDA day you apply that to a one sided NDA, then write the clause it lacks. Here is one an AI review often flags as missing.

Clause 8. Return and destruction of information8.1 Within ten days of a written request, or on termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and every copy of it. 8.2 An authorised officer of the Receiving Party shall confirm in writing that this has been done. 8.3 This Clause 8 survives termination of this Agreement.
AI flagged this as missing. Knowing why it matters, and writing it cleanly, is the part a business pays a drafter for.
