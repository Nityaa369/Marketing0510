11

Your first business document: a vendor service agreement

Most small businesses hire vendors: a cleaning agency, a software developer, a delivery partner. You learn to draft the agreement that says what the vendor must deliver, and what the business can do when the vendor falls short.

Clause 5. Service levels and remedies5.1 The Vendor shall perform the Services to the standards set out in Schedule B. 5.2 If the Vendor misses a Service Level in any calendar month, the Client may deduct the service credit shown in Schedule B from the next invoice. 5.3 Three such misses within six months entitle the Client to end this Agreement on fifteen days' written notice.
Shop owners understand a deduction from an invoice. Write it so they can follow it at a glance, and they will trust the whole contract.
