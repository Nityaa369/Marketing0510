Monitoring of work devices3.1 The Company may monitor activity on Company-issued laptops during working hours only, to protect its systems and client data. 3.2 The Company will not access personal devices, private messages or cameras. 3.3 Employees will be told in writing what is monitored, why, and for how long records are kept.
