Your first lesson is the parts of a contract

The first day covers how a contract is built: parties, recitals, definitions, operative clauses, boilerplate and signatures. Once you see the structure, every later document is easier. Here is a recitals and definitions block of the kind you write first.

Recitals and Clause 1. DefinitionsWHEREAS the Company develops mobile software and wishes to engage the Consultant to provide design services; NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows. 1.1 "Deliverables" means the designs listed in Schedule A. 1.2 "Effective Date" means the date on which both parties sign this Agreement.
In exams you define terms inside answers. In a contract, a clear definition stops an argument years later. Practise these first.
