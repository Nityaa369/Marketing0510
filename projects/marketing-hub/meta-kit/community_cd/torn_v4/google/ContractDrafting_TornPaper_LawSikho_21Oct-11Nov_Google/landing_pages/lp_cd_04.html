What junior advocates in litigation already bring to contracts

Court work trains you in exactly what a careful drafter needs. You have watched agreements argued over line by line, and you have learnt to read a document the way the other side will. That experience is an asset. These three habits carry straight to the desk.

11

Your desk document: a commercial lease clause

A restaurant signs a nine year lease for its first outlet. The rent rises every three years, and neither side wants to walk away early. You learn to draft the escalation and lock in clauses so both sides know the numbers from the first day.

Clause 4. Rent escalation and lock in4.1 The monthly Rent shall increase by fifteen percent at the end of every three years from the Rent Commencement Date. 4.2 Neither party may terminate this Lease during the first thirty-six months (the Lock In Period), except for a material breach not cured within thirty days of written notice.
You have seen escalation fights reach court. Writing the percentage and the date plainly is how you keep this one out of it.
