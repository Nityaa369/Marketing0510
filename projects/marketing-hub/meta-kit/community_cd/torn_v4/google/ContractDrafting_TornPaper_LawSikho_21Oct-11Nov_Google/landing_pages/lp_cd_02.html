11

Your first investor document: a SAFE agreement clause

A startup takes money today and promises the investor shares later. The SAFE records when that happens and at what price. You learn to draft the clause that decides it, and to explain it to a founder in two minutes.

Clause 3. Conversion on equity financing3.1 If the Company issues preferred shares in an Equity Financing before this Agreement ends, the Purchase Amount shall convert into preferred shares. 3.2 The number of shares issued shall equal the Purchase Amount divided by the lower of (a) the Valuation Cap Price and (b) the price per share paid by new investors, less the Discount Rate.
Founders often skim the valuation cap. Practise explaining it to a friend in two plain sentences before you show anyone this draft.
