What tech law work looks like for a lawyer who left a firm job for family

Software development agreements are close to the commercial contracts you drafted. The camp's mnemonic covers appointment, milestones, specifications, payment, acceptance testing, IP assignment and handover. You will recognise the bones, and only the tech details are new.

SaaS deals add the vocabulary. SLAs with uptime, downtime and credits, source code versus object code, and white labelling all turn up in the clauses. Day 1 explains each, and a firm-trained eye knows where they belong in a contract.

Review work suits your instincts. On Day 3 the camp reviews a startup's terms for gaps such as uncapped liability or unclear IP ownership. You have done this kind of markup on bigger deals, so it should feel like familiar ground. Day 3 then shows how a lawyer offers the fix to the founder.
