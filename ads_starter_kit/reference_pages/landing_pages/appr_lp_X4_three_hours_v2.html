THE WORK YOU CAN GET

10 kinds of simple work to start with

Nobody starts specialised. You start with simple work that is in high demand, collect the first happy clients and reviews, and climb from there as your confidence builds. Every task below is simple, done from home, and a place to begin, not where you will stay. On Day 1 you pick the three that fit you.

1. Inbox and calendar keeping

A founder abroad wakes up to two hundred emails. You sort them, answer the routine ones and keep the calendar honest. They are buying their mornings back.

2. Customer replies by email and chat

Small online businesses get questions all day: where is my order, does this fit, can I return it. You answer from a script at first, then from experience.

3. Online store upkeep

New products listed, prices corrected, photos placed, stock counts updated. Every small store has this pile, and nobody in the company wants to own it.

4. Books and invoices kept tidy

Receipts entered, invoices sent, payments matched to bills. A business that cannot afford an accounts person still needs the accounts done every week.

5. Research and lead lists

A company that sells to dentists needs a list of dentists: names, cities, emails. You build it carefully, and careful is the whole job.

6. Writing work

Product descriptions, how-to guides, blog posts, newsletters. Written in plain English, checked before it goes out. The most common first task for former teachers.

7. Social media upkeep

Posts scheduled for the week, comments answered, messages passed on. The owner knows it matters and cannot sit on it daily. You can.

8. Meeting notes and transcription

A one-hour recording becomes one page of clean notes with decisions and next steps. Quiet work, done on your own hours, valued far above its difficulty.

9. Hiring support

Applications sorted against a checklist, shortlists prepared, interviews scheduled across time zones. HR women recognise this work immediately.

10. Spreadsheets and customer lists cleaned

Duplicates removed, missing fields filled, formats fixed. Every growing company has a messy list it is embarrassed about, and it pays to have it fixed.

WHAT THIS WORK PAYS

How the money builds

Nobody starts at the top. This is the order it usually goes in, and what moves you up a step.

First, small paid work

Short, well-defined tasks for one client. Small on purpose. The point is a finished job and a person willing to say you did it well.

Then, repeat work from the same client

The second job from a client you already have is the cheapest work you will ever win. Most steady income starts here, not with new clients.

Then, specialised work

Rates climb with depth, not with hours. Once you handle something most people cannot, you set the price instead of accepting one.
