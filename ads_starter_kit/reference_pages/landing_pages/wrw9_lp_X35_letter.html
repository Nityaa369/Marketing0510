A letter from the lead trainer of the Women Remote Work Bootcamp

Dear friend,

My name is Nidhi Dayani. I am a lawyer and a Company Secretary. In the past three years, I have trained more than 5,000 professionals. I work remotely with Skill Arbitrage from my home in Nagpur, so the working life I teach in this camp is the same working life I live every single day. I am not describing something I read about. I am describing my own routine.

Virtual assistant work means keeping a small business owner's inbox, calendar, customer replies and social posts in order, from home. Paid in dollars. I want to tell you plainly why I run this camp, and exactly what will happen inside it, so that you can decide for yourself whether it is worth three of your afternoons and ten rupees.

Small business owners in America need help with everyday work. Answering emails. Keeping records tidy. Preparing simple documents. Following up with customers. Managing calendars and bookings. These owners are glad to pay a reliable person who does this work for them from far away, on a laptop or even a phone. Most women in India have never been shown this work. Not because it is a secret, but because nobody around them does it, so nobody could point to it. That is the gap this camp closes.

In my batches, I have taught school teachers, homemakers, women who stopped working when their children were born and now want to restart, and women past fifty who believed their working years were behind them. Over sixteen thousand women registered for one recent batch. I have watched women who were nervous on the first afternoon build a profile, complete a work sample, and write their first message to a real business owner by the third. When a woman sees her own finished work sample on her screen, something changes in the way she carries herself. I have seen it too many times to call it luck.

P.S. before you read further: you do not have to finish this letter to hold your place. The camp costs ₹10 for all three days, and if it is not useful to you, that ₹10 comes straight back. You can book now and keep reading.

Here is exactly what we will do together across the three days.

On the first day, I will show you the kinds of remote work that US small business owners actually pay for, with real examples, so you can see clearly which of them suits your situation. On the second day, we will build your profile together, step by step, and begin your first work sample. On the third day, you will finish that work sample, and I will give you a simple first-client plan: where to look, what to write, and how to follow up. You will walk out holding three things: a clear picture of the work, a ready profile with a work sample inside it, and a plan for reaching your first client.

Now let me be honest with you, because I would rather lose a registration than mislead a woman who trusts me.

This is training. We are not placing you in a job. What we teach you is how to find this work yourself, and finding it costs nothing. You will never need to pay anyone for leads, listings, or introductions. If anyone ever asks you for money to "give" you remote work, walk away from them.

Everything we use in this camp is a free tool. You do not need to buy software, and you do not need an expensive laptop. The phone in your hand is enough to attend and enough to begin.

There are no night shifts here. This is not call centre work and it is not BPO work. This is skilled support work for small business owners, done in hours that you choose, from your own home.

On money, I will say only this much: the work is paid in dollars, and it grows as your track record grows. I will not put a number in your head. Anyone who promises you a fixed income before you have done a single piece of work is not being straight with you.

Abhyuday Agarwal, COO and co-founder of Skill Arbitrage, co-hosts this camp with me. Between the two of us, you will hear both sides of the story: how the work is actually done, and how the industry behind it runs.

Now the practical part. The camp runs on 5, 6 and 7 September 2026, Saturday to Monday, from 2 PM to 5 PM IST each day. That is nine hours of live teaching. It is fully online, so you join from home on your phone or laptop. The full fee for all three days is ₹10. Yes, ten rupees. We keep a fee because a small payment keeps the batch serious, and we keep it tiny so that money is never the reason a woman stays out.

And here is my promise on that ten rupees: if you attend the three days and feel they were not useful to you, tell us, and your ₹10 comes back to you. No questions, no argument, no forms. You are risking nothing except three afternoons.

Once you pay, you will receive the class joining link and an invite to the batch WhatsApp group, both before Day 1. Keep an eye on your WhatsApp and your email after you book, and everything you need will reach you there.

If you have read this far, some part of you is already curious about what your afternoons could become. Bring that part of you to class on Saturday. I will be there live on all three days, and I will speak to you the same way I have spoken to you in this letter.
