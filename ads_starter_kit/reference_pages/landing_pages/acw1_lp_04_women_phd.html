The work

7 kinds of research work women with a PhD get paid for abroad

Each one uses a skill your doctorate already trained.

  1. Research gap analysisRead a professor's body of work and set out the questions it has not answered yet.
  2. Grant proposalsBuild a funding application for a professor or a think tank, from the research question to the plan of work.
  3. Literature reviewsMap what is known on a topic, the way you did for your thesis, for a team starting a new study.
  4. Manuscript draftingHelp turn a team's findings into a paper, work that can come with co-authorship.
  5. Policy briefs and white papersShorter reports for think tanks and NGOs, built on the research.
  6. Book projectsHelp an academic prepare a book and get it placed with a publisher.
  7. Course materialWrite e-learning content for edtech companies and for experts building courses.
The buyers

6 kinds of clients who pay PhD holders for research work

  1. Professors in the US, UK, Canada and EuropeTheir funding depends on proposals and papers, and they need trained help to produce them.
  2. Think tanks and policy institutesTheir project managers bring in researchers for proposals, reviews and reports.
  3. Corporate R&D teams and labsThey need someone who can survey the research before they commit to a project.
  4. Deep-tech startupsSmall teams that apply for grants and need the research case written well.
  5. Edtech companiesIn London and elsewhere, they commission course material from subject experts.
  6. PublishersThey work with academics on books and need people who can shape a manuscript.
Why you

Why clients abroad value a PhD

  • You have done the hardest training already. Years of reading, framing questions and defending your answers are what clients pay for.
  • You know how to find a gap. Every thesis starts with one, and every grant proposal needs one.
  • You can write at length and stay precise. A proposal or a review is a long document that has to hold together, and your thesis taught you how.
  • The title opens conversations. A doctorate tells a professor abroad that you understand how research works before you say anything else.

The work also fits around the rest of your life. It is done from home, on your own hours, one project at a time. A PhD holder who is teaching, between posts or raising a family can take on a proposal or a review and keep everything else going.

The route

How the first client usually comes, in 4 steps

  1. Start from your own subjectSearch Google Scholar for researchers abroad who work close to your thesis topic.
  2. Read their latest papersNote the question their work leaves open.
  3. Offer to help answer itPitch a gap analysis, a literature review or the first draft of a grant proposal.
  4. Build on the first projectA professor who liked one proposal from you will have another funding round to prepare. Proposals, reviews and papers tend to come from the same few clients.
