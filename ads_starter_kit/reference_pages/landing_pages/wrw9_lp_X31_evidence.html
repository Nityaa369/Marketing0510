Exhibit 01 · The size of the market
36.2 million
small businesses in the United States
US Small Business Administration, official government data

These are not giant corporations with big HR departments. They are dentists, accountants, online shops, real estate agents, small law firms. Most have fewer than ten people. The owner does the admin work at night because there is nobody else to do it. That owner is the person who pays for remote help.

And the pool keeps growing. US Census data shows 578,926 new business applications were filed in July 2026 alone. That is more than five lakh new businesses in one month, each one a possible future client for someone who can work online.

Exhibit 02 · The problem those businesses have
36%
of US small businesses cannot fill their open jobs
NFIB Small Business Jobs Report, July 2026

More than one in three small businesses in America has work waiting and nobody to do it. This is why remote work for Indians exists at all. It is not charity and it is not a trick. It is a simple shortage: they have tasks, they cannot find local people to do them, and the work can be done from anywhere with an internet connection.

Exhibit 03 · What they do about it
75%
of US small businesses with international staff plan to hire even more abroad
Gusto survey of US small businesses

The same Gusto survey found that 58% of the US small businesses hiring internationally do it because they simply cannot find workers at home. And when they look abroad, India is one of their top three source countries. So this is not a door that is closing. The businesses that already hire across borders want to do more of it, and India is already on their shortlist.

Exhibit 04 · The trend, measured
+63.7%
growth in one year in UK companies hiring Indians who stay in India
Deel, State of Global Hiring 2025

Deel is a company that handles payroll when businesses hire across countries, so it sees the real hiring numbers. Its 2025 report shows full time hiring of Indians grew 63.7% among UK companies and 24.1% among US companies in a single year. The important part: these workers did not migrate. They stayed in India, in their own homes, and the salary came to them.

Exhibit 05 · What happens when work comes home
3x
women offered work from home were more than three times as likely to start working
A randomized study in India by J-PAL researchers

This is the strongest evidence on this page. Researchers ran a proper randomized study in India, the same method used to test medicines. When women were offered jobs they could do from home, they took up work at more than three times the rate. The conclusion is simple and hopeful: crores of Indian women are not unwilling to work. They are willing and able the moment the work comes to where they are.

Exhibit 06 · Why the old route is unfair
49%
fewer interview callbacks for women with a career break, on identical resumes
Ashoka University research, 2024

Researchers sent out resumes that were exactly the same except for one thing: a career break. The women with a break got about half as many callbacks. If you took years out for your children or your family and the job portals went silent, the problem was never your ability. It was a biased filter. And you are not alone: the Udaiti Foundation estimates around 7 million Indian women are looking to return to work after a break. Remote clients abroad care about one thing, whether the work gets done. They do not ask about gap years.

Exhibit 07 · What the work pays
$22/hour
average rate earned by Indian women freelancers
Payoneer research on global freelancers

Payoneer, a payments company that moves freelancer earnings across borders, found Indian women freelancers averaging 22 dollars per hour, up 42% since 2020, and at par with their global peers. Even better: in freelancing worldwide, women earn 92 cents for every dollar men earn, a far smaller gap than in regular salaried jobs. Online work does not know your age, your city, or your gap years. To be clear, these are market averages, not a promise: your results will vary with your hours, your skill, and your consistency.

Exhibit 08 · The money is already flowing
$4.03 billion
paid out to freelancers through just one platform in 2025
Upwork, published 2025 results

Upwork is one single website where businesses hire online workers. In 2025 it paid freelancers over 4 billion dollars, from 785,000 client businesses. And if you worry that AI will take this work away, note this from the same report: AI related work on Upwork crossed 300 million dollars a year and grew 50% in one year. AI is creating paid online work faster than it removes it, for people who learn to use it.
