The route: this weekend to month six

D1 Station 1 · Saturday, 5 Sep

Day 1: The map of work that pays

We walk through the online work that clients around the world actually pay for right now, and which of it suits women working from home in India. Writing, research, admin support, social media help, data work and more, with real examples of who hires and what they pay.

In your hands after this: your own shortlist of 3 work types matched to what you already know. Time cost: 3 hours live class (2 to 5 PM)
D2 Station 2 · Sunday, 6 Sep

Day 2: Your profile and your first work sample

We build with you, live, the two things a client checks before hiring anyone: a clean profile that describes you honestly, and a one page work sample that proves you can do the job. You do not watch this happen. You make yours during the class, with guidance at every step.

In your hands after this: a finished profile and a one page work sample, ready to show a client. Time cost: 3 hours live class (2 to 5 PM)
D3 Station 3 · Monday, 7 Sep

Day 3: Your outreach plan

Work does not arrive on its own, so the last day is about going to where the clients are. We show you the platforms and places where small businesses look for help, how to write a short message that gets a reply, and how to keep going politely when there is silence.

In your hands after this: a written outreach plan plus your first list of 10 names to contact. Time cost: 3 hours live class (2 to 5 PM)
W1 Station 4 · The week after camp

Week 1: First messages go out

You send your first messages using the scripts from Day 3. Here is the honest rule we teach: expect to send about 10 messages before the first response comes. That is normal, not failure. Knowing the number in advance is what keeps you calm at message six.

In your hands after this: your first replies, and a habit of daily outreach. Time cost: about 1 hour a day
M1 Station 5 · Months 1 and 2

Months 1 to 2: First small paid work

Replies turn into small paid jobs. Starting rates for new remote workers are usually 5 to 7 dollars an hour. The money matters, but the bigger prize at this station is the testimonial. Every finished job where you ask the client for two honest lines of feedback becomes proof for the next client.

In your hands after this: your first earnings and your first written testimonials. Time cost: about 2 hours a day
M3 Station 6 · Months 3 to 6

Months 3 to 6: Rates rise, work steadies

With testimonials on your profile, you stop being a stranger. Rates step up to 10 dollars an hour and beyond, clients come back with repeat work, and your income stops depending on one lucky message. This is the station where home work becomes a steady part of the family income.

In your hands after this: repeat clients, rising rates, steady monthly work. Time cost: the hours you choose to give it

Simple math for station six: 3 hours a day at 10 dollars an hour is roughly fifty thousand rupees a month.
