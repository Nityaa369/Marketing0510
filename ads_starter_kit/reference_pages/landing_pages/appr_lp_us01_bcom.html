Why US firms are hiring in India

01
The USA is short of 300,000 accountants in 2026, and the US accountant headcount is down roughly 10% since 2019.
02
1 in 3 US firms already offshore accounting work to India, heading to 1 in 2 by 2028.
03
The Enrolled Agent licence, the US IRS's own credential, is open from India: three papers, exam in Indian cities, no US degree and no age bar. Only 5,216 EAs exist in all of India.

Figures from published US industry and IRS data compiled in our research brief, July 2026.
