The work

7 kinds of data and report work medical researchers get paid for

All of it starts with the data skills you already use at work.

  1. Reports from public dataTake raw public data, such as health survey figures, and turn it into a clear report a client can use.
  2. Survey studies in IndiaTest a foreign professor's findings with Indian patients or settings, from the questionnaire to the write-up.
  3. Data analysisClean a dataset, run the analysis and explain the results in plain words.
  4. Transcription and codingTurn interviews and focus groups into coded data that a research team can analyse.
  5. Research gap analysisRead a professor's published work, find the question it leaves open and propose the study that would answer it.
  6. Grant proposalsPut the data and the study plan into a funding application, in the format US funders use.
  7. Policy briefs and white papersShort reports for think tanks and NGOs that turn research findings into recommendations.
The buyers

6 kinds of clients who pay for research data and reports

  1. Professors abroad who want an Indian settingA study done in the US or Europe raises a question: does it hold in India? You are placed to find out.
  2. Think tanks and policy institutesTheir project managers hire researchers to build reports and briefs from data.
  3. International NGOsHealth programmes need reports written from field data.
  4. Corporate R&D teams and labsThey need technical reports and research summaries without adding staff.
  5. Biotech and deep-tech startupsLean teams that need their data turned into reports for funders and partners.
  6. Pharma, medical-device firms and hospitalsMedical writing, including reports on studies and their results.
Why you

Why clients abroad need a medical researcher in India

  • You can reach Indian settings. Hospitals, clinics and communities here are places a researcher abroad cannot easily study.
  • You have run studies before. Consent, sampling, questionnaires and data cleaning are already part of your work.
  • You can read the numbers. A report is only as good as the person who checks that the analysis says what the text claims.
  • You can write it up. Clients prefer to get the data and the report from one person.

There is also the question of credit. When you extend a professor's study into India, you add something new to their research. That kind of work can lead to a shared paper and a long working relationship, instead of a one-off job.

The route

How the first client usually comes, in 4 steps

  1. Read one professor's recent studyChoose a study from the last few years that was done outside India.
  2. Write a one-page gap analysisSet out what the study leaves open, and how a survey in India would test it.
  3. Send it as your pitchA proposal with a real research idea in it gets read. A general request for work does not.
  4. Deliver the first reportOnce a professor has one solid report from you, the next study is an easy conversation. Professors also recommend people they trust to their colleagues.
