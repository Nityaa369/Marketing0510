Why they hire from India

This is not one or two lucky stories. The shortage on their side is measured and published.

  • There are 36.2 million small businesses in the US, as per the SBA.
  • 36% of US small businesses cannot fill open jobs right now, as per NFIB.
  • In July 2026 alone, 578,926 new business applications were filed in the US, as per Census data. New businesses mean new work to hand over.
  • A Gusto survey found 58% of US small businesses that hire internationally do it because they cannot find workers at home, and 75% plan to hire more abroad. India is a top-3 source country.
  • Deel reported in 2025 that hiring of Indians grew 63.7% among UK companies and 24.1% among US companies in one year, with those workers staying in India.
  • Businesses paid freelancers over 4 billion dollars on Upwork alone in 2025.

Every one of those numbers is a door. The buyers exist, the budgets exist, and the shortage on their side is real. What is missing is the person on this side who knows how to show up, present herself and do the work.
