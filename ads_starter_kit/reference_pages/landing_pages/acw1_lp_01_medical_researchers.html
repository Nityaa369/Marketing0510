The work

8 kinds of research work medical researchers get paid for abroad

None of this is new work to you. What is new is who pays for it.

  1. Literature reviewsFind the studies on a topic, summarise what they found, and name the gaps worth studying next.
  2. Systematic reviewsThe same, done to a set method, for teams preparing a publication.
  3. Medical writingDocuments for pharma companies, medical-device firms and hospitals. The script is plain about it: you do not need to be a doctor.
  4. Grant proposalsHelp a professor or a think tank in the US, UK or Europe put together a funding application.
  5. Journal formatting and submissionReferences, style-guide compliance, cover letters and the back-and-forth with the journal.
  6. Pre-submission reviewCheck a paper's structure, citations, methods and language before it goes to a journal, with AI agents doing the first pass.
  7. English editingPolish papers for researchers in Europe, Japan, Korea and China who publish in English journals.
  8. Survey and field researchRun a survey or collect data that tests a foreign study in an Indian setting.
The buyers

6 kinds of clients who pay for medical research writing

  1. Professors and researchers abroadIn the US, UK, Canada and Europe, where promotions and funding depend on how much they publish.
  2. Pharma and medical-device companiesThey need clinical and regulatory documents written by someone who can read the science.
  3. Hospitals and research institutesThey publish, apply for funding and report results, and rarely have enough writers.
  4. Think tanks and policy institutesHealth policy reports and briefs need writers who understand the research behind them.
  5. Biotech and deep-tech startupsFunded but lean: they need grant proposals, investor reports and technical documents without a full-time writer.
  6. Research and editing agenciesFirms that serve researchers worldwide and keep a bench of freelance medical and academic writers.
Why you

Why professors abroad pay medical researchers in India

  • They have to publish. Tenure, promotion and research funding abroad all depend on papers, and there are never enough hours to write them.
  • Teaching and administration eat their week. Their research assistants work limited hours and are often not yet trained researchers.
  • English is a barrier for many of them. Researchers in non-English-speaking countries need their papers ready for English-language journals.
  • Your domain knowledge is the part a general writer cannot supply. A medical paper needs someone who can read a methods section and a results table, which you already do every week.

There is a second reason too. Researchers abroad want their findings tested in other settings. A medical researcher in India can offer to run the survey or collect the data that shows whether a study holds here, which is work a writer abroad cannot do, and it often comes with credit on the paper.

The route

How the first client usually comes, in 4 steps

  1. Pick recent papers in your fieldPapers from the last three to five years, whose authors are still actively researching.
  2. Send a specific pitchOffer a literature review, a pre-submission review or a field study in India. Never just "please give me work".
  3. Deliver one small project wellA review or a formatting job is enough to start.
  4. Let the work travelProfessors recommend people to other professors, so one good project leads to the next.

Questions

Straight answers

Do I need a PhD?

No. Master's graduates, teachers and working researchers do this work. What matters is that you can read research and write clearly about it.

Do I need to be a doctor for medical writing?

No. The bootcamp covers how people from pharmacy, life sciences and even the humanities do medical writing.

Can I do this alongside my job?

Yes. Most people start with projects on the side and keep their current research or clinical role.

Won't AI take this work?

AI speeds up the research: searching papers, sorting findings, checking citations. The writing and the judgement stay yours, and journals ask authors to disclose AI use. The bootcamp shows you how to use AI tools properly for both.
