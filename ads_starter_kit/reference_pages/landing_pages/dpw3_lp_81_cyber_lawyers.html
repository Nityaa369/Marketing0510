Why now

When businesses start buying this work

  1. 2009 India's first data-protection rule, section 43A of the IT Act, applied only to a narrow list of sensitive data.
  2. 11 August 2023 the Digital Personal Data Protection Act was passed, covering every business that handles personal data in India.
  3. 13 November 2025 the Rules under the Act were notified, with an eighteen month phase-in announced by the Press Information Bureau.
  4. November 2026 consent manager registration begins under that phase-in.
  5. May 2027 the core duties under the Act take effect for businesses.

Dates as notified and as set out in the Press Information Bureau's release on the phase-in. The point here is when businesses start buying this work, not what happens to anybody who is late.
