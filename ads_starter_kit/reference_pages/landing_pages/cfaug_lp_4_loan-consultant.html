What corporate finance work actually is

Not a degree. A defined set of jobs that companies across India and abroad pay outsiders to do.

Startup valuation

Put a defensible number on a company using DCF and revenue or profit multiples.

Financial models & projections

Build the 3–5 year forecasts investors ask to see before they invest.

Pitch decks

Turn a company’s numbers into an investor-ready story.

Investor outreach systems

A repeatable way to find, contact and track the right investors.

Periodic investor updates

The monthly and quarterly reporting investors expect after they invest.

Budgeting, forecasting & cash flow

Plan the money so the business knows what it can spend.

Financial MIS & dashboards

Put a company’s key numbers at its fingertips, updated every month.

Unit economics

Work out whether each customer actually makes the business money.

Cap tables, term sheets & SAFEs

Track who owns what, and read the terms before anything is signed.

Debt & alternative funding

Invoice discounting, revenue-based and inventory-backed funding.

Financial controls & ESOP plans

Put the guardrails and the employee equity plan in place.

Virtual CFO work

Be the part-time finance brain for a company that can’t yet hire one.
