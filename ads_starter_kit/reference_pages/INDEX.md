# Reference landing pages

60 pages copied out of the main repo, nothing else from their kits. Two jobs:

1. **Donors.** A page that already converted is a better starting point than a blank
   file. Keep the 21-step spine, change the argument inside it (Rule 4b).
2. **They are the population `closing_reader.py --selftest` fits its fold finder
   against.** That selftest needs both markup shapes - pages carrying `div.close` and
   pages without - or the gate is blind to half the corpus, which is the `ad_lint` C8
   failure all over again. This set is balanced on that: 30 with, 30 without.

Filenames are prefixed with a short kit tag because six basenames collided across
kits. The `cheapest face` column is the lowest live cost recorded for a FACE in the
same kit - it says that kit earned its money, not that this page did.
