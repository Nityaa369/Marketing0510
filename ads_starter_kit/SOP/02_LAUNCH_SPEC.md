## Campaign settings

- `OUTCOME_SALES`, CBO, `HIGHEST_VALUE`.
- Optimise **PURCHASE**. Never registration, never lead.
- 1 to 2 ad sets. India. 25 to 45 (HR GrowthCamp: 25 to 50). Advantage+ audience.
- Meta only at launch.

## Ad set rules

- **Max 5 ads per ad set.** Twelve in one ad set is two to four times past the breaking
  point — Meta puts the budget into the top two or three and leaves the rest on a few
  dollars a day. *(Settled 7 Aug 2026.)*
- **Launch in a fresh ad set, or a fresh CBO.** Never drop new creative into an ad set an
  earlier wave owns. The older ads' delivery history starves the new cells beside them.
- **Never let two of our own campaigns target the same audience.** They bid against each
  other. Put them in one campaign. *(Settled 10 Aug 2026.)* When two batches share an ICP,
  split across ad sets or hold one back.
- **Do not raise the campaign daily cap on launch day.** Judge the new cells at flat spend
  first. A drift to ₹300+ is saturation, not a signal about the creative.
- Creative that names a specific client market (US small business, dollar billing, European
  D2C) goes in its own ad set, or the broader cells absorb its budget.
- A carousel's card order is fixed: card 1 is the hook, card 2 carries the offer.

## Money

- **Target CPP ₹300 or better including GST.** Comparable ₹10 camps: ACW ₹256 to ₹277,
  USCL ₹336, Corporate Litigation ₹315. HR GrowthCamp beats ₹331.
- **Circuit breaker: pause any ad above ₹450 CPP.** Where no baseline exists for a batch,
  pause at roughly 2x its own opening cost per buyer.
- **Scale at ₹250 or better.**
- **At 100 purchases, reconcile the payment bridge against Meta against the CRM before
  scaling.** Cashfree on content writing, Razorpay on HR and the senior batches.
- Re-baseline CPP when a kit is a genuinely different creative treatment or a much wider
  ICP mix. A carried-over number is a starting assumption, not a result.

## Page speed is a launch criterion

Campaigns get flagged for page speed independently of the copy. TechLaw BC ran at only 33%
