# 04 · Compliance

Not up for renegotiation per pack. A kit README may be stricter, never looser.

## Claims

- **No placement or job guarantee, ever.** Saying "no guaranteed jobs" in a *what we will
  not promise* block is correct and expected. Indian edtech buyers distrust that claim more
  than any other: in a survey of 90,000 prospective students, 50% worry about fraud in
  online education and 20% specifically doubt placement claims.
- **No income promise.** Market data must be labelled as market data, with source and date.
- **No earning, salary or per-word rupee figures on any ad face.** Rate and salary ladders
  are landing page only.
- **No AI-fear framing.** The argument is a ladder, not a threat: AI took the production
  floor, which raised the price of the judgment that decides what gets produced.

## The offer

- **Live only, no recordings.** Never promise a recording. The substitutes are session
  notes, templates, the WhatsApp group and a certificate.
- **Price labelled including GST**, refundable anytime, said plainly wherever price appears.

## Brand

- **SkillArbitrage and the legal-products brand are never co-branded.**
- SkillArbitrage credentials appear on every page: NSE Emerge-listed, Recognised NSDC
  Training Partner, IIT Roorkee iHUB Training Partner.

## Copy

- **No em-dashes** anywhere in shipped copy.
- **No "this is not for you if…" block.** Banned on this account after it appeared directly
  above the price. Qualify positively instead.
- **Never "freelancer", never "adapt", never "content marketing" as the audience label**
  on the content writing family.
- The **D2C** acronym does not appear on an ad face. Say "brands selling straight from their
  own website". *(Section A ad 15 ships with it by explicit instruction — the one exception,
  recorded so nobody discovers it by accident.)*

## The two judgment calls

These are not mechanical and `preflight.py` cannot see them. Full detail in `../CLAUDE.md`.

- **Rule 1 — offer a better career, never threaten.** No regulator or liability framing, no
  more work at the same desk, no "get named the person who fixed it".
- **Rule 2 — no assumed knowledge.** One sentence defining the field, in ordinary words, on
  every ad face. Skipping it is what "cryptic" means.
