For the Remote Women / VA bootcamp, an ICP is only worth an ad when four things are true
at once: **D**emand (a large, countable, growing US practitioner population), **S**olo
(they run a one-person business, so they ARE the admin team), **G**ap (they must market
and administrate and cannot), and **N**eighbour (a trained Indian professional who
already speaks that field's vocabulary).

**Why:** the "113,000+ credentialed RDNs" stat that produced the approved dietitian ads
worked because of all four, not because the number was big. Miss N and the ad sells
generic offshore admin into the most price-competitive market on earth — a weak offer
and it reads as one.

**How to apply:** score candidates against D/S/G/N before writing `value_props.md`. The
researched market list, VA route taxonomy and Indian-credential mapping are in
[docs/RemoteWomen_ICP_MARKET_RESEARCH_26Aug.md](docs/RemoteWomen_ICP_MARKET_RESEARCH_26Aug.md)
(26 Aug 2026), with a 12-row recommended shortlist and a caveats section on which numbers
are soft. Related: [[feedback_house_ad_formula]].
