Every blind-reader run asks five questions, not three. Ramanuj, 26 Aug 2026:
**"always always check if the value prop is coming through."**

1. Who is this addressed to?
2. What job would I be doing?
3. What can I do on the last day that I cannot do today?
4. **In one sentence, what is this ad offering you that you do not have now?**
5. **Would this feel like a step UP or a step DOWN from the career you trained for?**

**Why:** Q4 and Q5 catch what Q1 to Q3 cannot. Six folds once answered Q1 to Q3 cleanly
and still came back "step DOWN, and it stings" on Q5 — the ad was legible and still
unsellable. Q3 answers were simultaneously vague across all six ("what I could actually
*do* differently is unclear"), which no mechanical gate sees.

**How to apply:** context-isolated subagent, first-fold text only, no brief and no ICP
list, and never predict the answers yourself. Run it at spec/copy stage, before any face
is rendered, not just before handover. Related: [[feedback_credential_icp_cannot_work]],
[[feedback_same_craft_better_payer]].
