Pulled 1 Oct 2026, campaign `CorpFinance_bootcamp_17-19Oct_CBO`, Skill Arbitrage, INR incl. GST,
website purchases (Rs 10 seat buyers).

**The cost is climbing and it is creative wear, not auction cost.** Per-buyer cost by day:
24 Sep 192, 25 Sep 213, 26 Sep 297, 27 Sep 254, 28 Sep 365, 29 Sep 295, 30 Sep 278. Over the same
days CPM fell 177 to 133 and CTR fell 1.01% to 0.64%. August par was Rs 194.

**Individual faces decay inside a week.** `cf_cma` went Rs 139 per buyer (24 Sep) to Rs 319
(30 Sep) on 239 buyers. `cf_equity_research` went Rs 73 to Rs 788 on 140.

**The wave 3 collage face is the cheapest static in the account.** `cfw3_loan_consultant`
Rs 136 on 117 buyers (band Rs 111 to 161), two full days both at Rs ~136. Same reader, same week,
same campaign: the August face Rs 209, the wave 2 face Rs 248. **The design moved the number, not
the reader** - the cleanest controlled design result in the account.

Ad set level, last 3 days: W3_Collage Rs 156, W4_NewReaders Rs 251, W2_DealsMkt Rs 254,
W2_CommFin2 Rs 305, LPfix_SetA Rs 315, LPfix_SetB Rs 443, W2_CommFin1 Rs 469.

**The standing failure is budget routing, not creative.** Waves 3 and 4 launched 15 faces on
30 Sep. One took Rs 15,767 and proved the design; the other fourteen got Rs 100 to Rs 2,400 each
and cannot be read at all - including the collage faces for CMA and equity research, the two
readers whose old creative is dying. Inside a CBO, Meta feeds the face with history. **A new wave
needs its own ad set with a floor or it is not tested.**

Readers under par in BOTH August and October: loan consultants/DSAs, cost accountants (CMA),
equity research analysts, aspiring investment bankers. Losers in both: commerce graduates (1.93x),
final-year students (3.4x), career break, retired bankers (1.50x).

Wave 5 (`CorporateFinance_Wave5_Worklist_MediaBuyer_17-19Oct`, built 1 Oct) gives those four
readers a second device, the `launchlist` layout - see [[feedback_launchlist_is_the_measured_design]].
