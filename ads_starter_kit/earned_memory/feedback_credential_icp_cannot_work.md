For the women's remote-work camp, **an ICP whose identity is a practising professional
credential does not work, and copy cannot fix it.** Proven three ways on 26 Aug 2026:
the `WomensRemoteWork_USRoles_MediaBuyer_5-7Sep` kit shipped 5 ads and 0 approved;
`ICPs_20_Wave2.md` had already cut BDS and PhD scholar on self-image; and six blind
readers returned step DOWN 6 of 6, with a full rewrite of the worst returning step DOWN
again.

**Why:** making the credential load-bearing sharpens the contrast between what she
trained for and what she is handed. A reader on the rewrite: *"the ad opens by naming my
clinical training and then hands me a phone and a scheduling queue."* The defect is the
distance between ICP and offer, not the wording.

**How to apply:**
- Score a credential on **practising identity, not prestige**. A nurse who left the ward
  still says "I trained in clinical care"; a B.Com graduate who never worked in finance
  has no bruisable identity.
- **Ownership beats assistance.** Approved cells give her a book: "an income that is
  yours", "your own list". "Assistant to a US advisor" reads as a demotion however the
  title is worded.
- Keep the ad's band a SITUATION (all 28 approved Wave 4 cells do). Put credential
  research into targeting and the landing page, not the identity band.
