**Women AI = "Remote work revolution for women", SkillArbitrage.** The Oct 3-5 camp is
**FREE**, so the unit is cost per REGISTRATION. Never blend it with the 5-7 Sep Rs 10 camp
(par Rs 108/buyer) or the June Community camp (Rs 429/buyer).

**Live campaign `WomenAI_FREE_3-5Oct-2026_BC_CBO_v1`,** 30 days to 1 Oct 2026: Rs 5.88L,
16,467 registrations, **par Rs 36**. Cost rose Rs 27 (19 Sep) to Rs 40 (1 Oct).

**The diagnosis, which is the useful part:** CPM +17%, CTR **+9%**, registrations per click
**-11%**. CTR did not fall, so this is not mainly creative fatigue. The decay is on the
landing page. And the three live pages carrying **10,700 of the 16,467 registrations run an
identical H2 sequence**, changing only the H1 - the Rule 4b defect, live on two thirds of
the spend. See [[feedback_lps_in_a_kit_must_differ]].

**The designs, read off the live creatives (not off repo look-alikes) and indexed to par:**

| design | index | results | band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **struck list** (3 things she is waiting for, struck, then the answer) | **0.88** | 5,327 | +/-3% |
| **clock band** (the hour her time opens to the hour it closes) | **0.93** | 856 | +/-7% |
| pay dot-plot | 0.96 | 320 | +/-11% |
| label rows | 1.01 | 5,211 | +/-3% |
| day-part cards | 1.07 | 3,445 | +/-3% |

Both are now adkit layouts (`strucklist`, `clockband`) with provenance in
`adkit/layouts_custom.py`. **Palette is not decoration here:** the same struck-list copy ran
as two palette variants and scored 0.82 and 0.97, far outside both bands.

**The readers that work:** single mothers on one income **0.83x** (n=3,175) and mothers free
during school hours **0.86x** (n=360) are the only two under par with a clean band. The axis
they share is **a named block of free hours plus a money reason**, and that is what the
fifteen untried readers in wave 10 were built on.

**Standing trap:** gender targeting leaks on this product - 22.5% of spend reached men on
ad sets with `genders=[2]`. See [[feedback_gender_expansion_leak]].
