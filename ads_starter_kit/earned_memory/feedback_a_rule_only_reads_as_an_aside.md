*"What is this weird line next to the paragraph"* - the 8px accent bar on the delivery block.
The Rs 82 approved face carries the same bar and it works there, so the bar is not the defect.
On that face it marks a small closing note sitting under a coloured tension line and a grey
question paragraph: it has something to be an aside FROM, and it is the least prominent thing
on the face.

**Why:** once the field definition came off the face
([[feedback-definition-belongs-on-the-page-not-the-face]]) and the tension line was dropped,
the delivery sentence was the ONLY body copy left. A quote bar on the only paragraph is
decoration with no function, and at 8px against 37px bold serif it reads as a stray stroke.

**How to apply:** a device that marks subordination needs a hierarchy to be subordinate
within. Before copying a treatment off an approved face, count how many text blocks that face
has and where the treated one sits among them. The Rs 82 winner runs five distinct sizes;
the face this was reported on ran band 64 / head 66 / barrier 56 / body 37, which is why it
also read as flat.
