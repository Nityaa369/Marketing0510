**Read `SOP/05_LANDING_PAGES.md` before building any LP.** It specifies an eleven-section
spine and 2,800-3,000 words. The actual approved pages
(`APPROVED/ContentWriting_Ready13_29-31Aug/landing_pages/lp_cw_10e_women.html` and
siblings) run **1,550-1,670 words across seventeen headings** — so treat ~1,600-1,800 as
the real target and the SOP number as the ceiling.

**Why:** on 25 Aug 2026 a wave of ten LPs shipped at 808 words across five sections
(what you get / the 3 days / 5 signs / FAQ / dates). Every page had the same body and only
the first fold changed. Feedback: "super short, deliver no value."

**What the approved pages do that a feature list does not:**
- They ARGUE in numbered steps: how the work is priced, who buys it, where to find them,
  what to send, what that puts you in a position to do.
- They give value BEFORE the ask. "Client kaise milega. Here is one answer, free, before
  you pay anything" names a buyer, a trigger, a place to look and a first message.
- They reframe the price: "The Rs 10 is not the cost. Three evenings is the cost."
- They carry a named human with a real quote, not adjectives.
- Their FAQs answer that reader's specific objection.
- Statistics are sourced (Unbounce; Edelman/LinkedIn) or absent.

**Testimonials.** The only real ones in the repo are three unnamed "Past attendee" quotes in
`WomenFreelance_MediaBuyer_5-7Sep/landing_pages/`. `APPROVED/LawSikho-Arbitration-.../README.md`
sets the house position: "inventing one is not an option", and a real quote goes "below the
money section, never in the prime scroll".

**Watch for cross-product claims.** "Eleven cohorts since 2024" belongs to the content-writing
bootcamp, not every bootcamp. Verifiable across SkillArbitrage: NSE-listed, approved NSDC
training partner.
