Women AI wave 10, 2 Oct 2026. Twenty faces named the reader, her free hours, and the money
plainly. Every mechanical gate passed. Then **all twenty blind readers flagged the same
thing** at question 6 — *"client work is never explained, what kind of work?"* — and the
salon face answered question 2, *what job would I be doing*, with **CANNOT ANSWER**.

The two blinded controls, both US Accounting faces, did not have the problem: they say
"US accounting work" and "close their books".

**Why:** "client work", "remote work" and "paid work from home" are category labels, not
tasks. A reader can follow the whole argument — your mornings are free, somebody pays for
them — and still not know what she would actually be doing. That is cryptic by this repo's
own definition, and it is invisible to every gate, because the sentence is clear English.

**How to apply:** name two or three concrete tasks on the face, in the reader's own words,
drawn from what the camp actually teaches. "Answer a business's customer emails, keep its
invoices tidy, clean up its customer lists" instead of "client work abroad". Pick the tasks
for THAT reader: the former BPO agent gets orders and returns, the teacher gets guides and
newsletters, the branch clerk gets receipts and invoices. After the fix every face answered
question 2 concretely and the salon face went from CANNOT ANSWER to a full description.
