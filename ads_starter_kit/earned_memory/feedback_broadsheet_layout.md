On 26 Sep 2026, for Tech Law wave 2, the broadsheet sample came first at 99 words ("isn't the
broadsheet too text heavy?"). The trimmed one she liked has 43 words: "i like the 43 words one".
Then "where is the register button?" A text line inside the price box does not count as a button.

**The standard, top to bottom:**
1. Brand line and a serif masthead with the camp name.
2. A strip with "live online · 3 evenings" and the dates.
3. A black reader bar (the call-out).
4. The serif head, about 84px.
5. A black-and-white photo on the left. On the right, "What you learn" with three ticked lines.
6. A price box: ₹10, incl. GST, refundable.
7. A full-width filled button, "Register at ₹10 →".

Cut from the reference: the edition and price corner boxes, the italic deck, the prose
paragraphs with a drop cap, and the footer.

**Why:** the reference is the DPDP newspaper face (oct_01 and siblings, Rs 157 per paid on
GrowthX in the week of 18-24 Sep). It was dense at about 105 words. The newspaper look is the
asset; the reading is not. Her standard is a face a half-asleep reader can accept or reject.

**How to apply:** keep a broadsheet face near 45 words, and never drop "incl. GST" when trimming a
price. Every face needs a real filled button. adkit's `broadsheet` layout was still a clone of
notice on 26 Sep; the sample is hand-built in
`TechLaw_Wave2_MediaBuyer_24-26Oct/samples/tl41_broadsheet_trademark_trim.html`, so port that
into adkit before building a wave on it.
