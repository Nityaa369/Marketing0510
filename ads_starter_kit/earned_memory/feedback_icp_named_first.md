Every creative names its ICP first, before the hook or the angle. Restated by the user on
27 Aug 2026 as feedback previously received on the ads: *"the icp comes first so that it
targets the people it is meant for."*

**Why:** it is step 1 of the approved first-fold structure and it is what the winning ads
actually did. The eight lowest-CPL Independent Director angle statics all carried a fixed
slab, `FOR PROFESSIONALS AT OR AFTER RETIREMENT`, sitting *above* the big statement headline.
So even the ads that look like pure mechanism reveals ("You cannot apply for a board seat",
₹40 CPL) named the audience first. Nothing is in tension here: ICP first, then the angle.

**How to apply:** on a static, the ICP slab sits above the headline at full size, never grey
or small. On video there is no glance order and nothing stays on screen, so the audience has
to be **named out loud in the first spoken line** and held on screen while it is said. One
clause only; two clauses spends the hook. See [[feedback_ad_clarity_rules]] and
[[feedback_price_hook_then_value_fast]].
