On 21 Sep 2026 Ramanuj rejected all 19 Corporate Finance notify faces with: "where does it say build a career in corporate finance", "join what", "the prize is - get into corporate finance, earn more, get a career upgrade". The offer lines named a *task* ("Advise the same clients on what their business is worth"). The field name appeared only in the small eyebrow, and "bootcamp" was nowhere on the face, so "Join for Rs 10" had no object. The approved August faces had both: "Do corporate finance work..." and "Rs 10 3-day bootcamp".

**Why:** The reader buys a career move, not a task. And a CTA with no noun reads as "join what".

**How to apply:** The big offer line leads with the move into the named field ("Move into corporate finance, ...", "Start your career in X"), then the reader's own bridge to it. The definition line defines the field itself, not a sub-discipline. The face names the product ("3-day live bootcamp"), and the CTA carries that noun ("Join the Rs 10 bootcamp"). The LP closing line carries it too. Keep "earn more" off the face unless a sourced figure exists (no income promise). Relates to [[headline-names-job-not-buying]] and [[icp-named-first]]. No gate catches this yet: hook_rules, blind_reader and closing_reader all passed the rejected faces.
