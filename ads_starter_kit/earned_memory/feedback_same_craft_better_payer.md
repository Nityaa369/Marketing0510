For a credentialed ICP, **the role name must be one the reader already knows** (virtual
assistant, social media manager) and **the specialism goes in the duties**. Ramanuj,
26 Aug 2026: *"the routes are roles people will not recognise - generalise the roles."*

**Why:** I had used the real US titles (intake coordinator, prior-authorisation
coordinator, client services coordinator) reasoning that "assistant" reads as a demotion
to a credentialed reader. That is Rule 2 backwards: a title nobody recognises reads as
invented, which is the recorded rejection *"those jobs don't really seem real, I doubt
anyone is hiring."* The dietitian-VA cell failed on its **duties** (one of four used her
training), never on its title.

**How to apply:** generic title, domain-loaded duties, so a generalist would do every
duty worse. The same test applies to the buyer: "med spa", "ABA practice", "RIA",
"prior authorisation" are US trade words; write "a US skin and cosmetic clinic", "a US
practice that treats children", "getting a treatment approved by the insurance company".

`womens_ai_bootcamp_5-7Sep/ICPs_20_Wave2.md` (non-practising BDS and PhD scholar cut on
self-image); a cut credential needs a different *situation* to survive.
