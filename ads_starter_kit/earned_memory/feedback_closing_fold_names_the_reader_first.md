Measured 1 Oct 2026 on Corporate Finance wave 6. Twenty closing folds were written in the shape
**"You already <do X>. These three evenings show you how to <do Y>."** Blind closing readers could
not answer "who is this addressed to" on **six** of them and only inferred it on four more:
"You already prepare the property file", "You already know why a file is declined", "You already
understand these businesses better than their paperwork does".

Rewriting every one to **open with the job title** fixed all twenty in a single pass:
"Mortgage and property loan officers already prepare the property file. These three evenings show
you how to write the business case that goes beside it." Score went from 6 failures to 23/23 at a
3/3 control floor.

**Why:** second person alone ("You already...") reads as addressed to nobody when the fold is seen
without the ad above it, which is exactly how the closing reader sees it, and how a reader who
scrolled past the hero sees it.

**How to apply:**
- The first words of `close_h2` are the reader, named as an occupation, plural: "Cost accountants
  already...", "Credit analysts at banks and NBFCs already...".
- Where the reader has no job title yet, use "As a <role> you already..." or state the situation
  in full: "You want investment banking work and have no bank seat yet."
- **Keep one voice.** "Relationship managers at banks already know ... These three evenings show
  YOU" was flagged for mixing third and second person; "As a relationship manager at a bank you
  already know..." passed.
- **Expand every acronym in the fold.** "GCCs" was read as the Gulf Cooperation Council; "a global
  capability centre" passed.

This supersedes nothing in [[feedback_closing_fold_names_person]]; it is the same defect found
again and the concrete repair that fixed it at scale.
