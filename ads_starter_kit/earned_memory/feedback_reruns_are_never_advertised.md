**Told by Ruchika, 9 Sep 2026:** *"none of the RE bootcamps have any ad campaigns EVER."*

`BOOTCAMP_CALENDAR.md` marks each camp `MAIN` (first run) or `RE` (rerun). **Only MAIN
camps are advertised.** A rerun with no live campaign is the normal state, not a gap.

**Why this matters:** the daily report's coverage check - every future camp must have a
live campaign behind it - fired on five reruns on its first run, including a **CRITICAL**
on Corporate finance (RE, 12-14 Sep) that was reported as the single most urgent item in
the account. It was not a problem at all. Five of six "dark camp" findings were false, and
the false one was louder than everything true in the report.

The wider lesson: a coverage check invents urgency out of an absence, so it needs to know
which absences are expected before it is allowed to shout. An absence is only a finding
where presence was the rule.

**How to apply:** `daily_report.py` now skips `RE` rows in the coverage finding entirely,
and the Camps ahead table shows them as *"rerun, not advertised"* rather than a red
*"no campaign"*. This also closes SOP/10's open question about rerun launch lead time -
there is no lead time to settle, because reruns are not advertised.

See [[project-meta-data-pull-path]] and [[feedback-never-judge-an-ad-on-a-part-day]].

**29 Sep 2026, stated as a rule by the user:** "we do not launch any ads for reruns". A kit built for an RE
camp (SeniorAI_Reach_31Oct-2Nov) was retargeted to the next MAIN (3 to 5 Oct) instead. Before building any
kit, check the camp row is MAIN on the dates sheet. When retargeting, re-read THAT camp's own Day 1-3 script:
the 3-5 Oct script put the "first 50 connections" list in the paid programme only, so a line copied from the
RE kit would have promised something the bootcamp does not give.
