A visual refresh has to carry the winning ad's CONTENT, not replace it. Built 21 Sep 2026 for
`CorporateFinance_Notify_MediaBuyer_17-19Oct` from a DTC sunscreen static, after two rejections:

- `venn` — rejected on logic. "How does the Venn diagram make absolutely any sense?" A Venn's
  overlap means membership of both sets, and a camp is neither a debt file nor an equity
  valuation. See [[feedback-venn-layout]].
- `alert` — the notification look with a short message. Rejected: "so much information is missing
  from the ad, button is not visible or highlighted."

Then the opposite failure: rebuilding the information put the poster back and lost the device.
"Now it's lost the notification UI element. I need it to be a visual refresh, not slight
modifications to the same design." The fix was to keep the notification as the *grammar* and pour
the whole first fold into it: app row, ICP as a question, qualifier row as chips, offer, the
one-sentence definition, the take-aways as an attachment card, meta line, one filled action.

**Why:** three separate pushbacks, and the question behind all of them was "is it based on good
past performing ads?" It was not, until I rendered the ₹164 and ₹170 August faces and copied their
element order. A refresh that cannot name the priced face it inherits from is decoration.

**How to apply:**
- Ask for the live cost of the faces the structure comes from BEFORE designing, and say so in the
  build docstring. [[feedback-icp-is-the-biggest-lever]]
- One action only. A second cell ("What's in it") competes for the click with the CTA and was cut
  on the user's own note.
- Accent button on a light ground: "the button and the background is the same color" killed the
  first version, which put a white card on a full accent field.
- Never let a face read as a downgrade. The offer is the same client at a higher level, or an
  engagement instead of a task. Never piecework, never "start again". [[feedback-same-craft-better-payer]]
- The offer line names the work; it never defines the work by contrast with the reader's job.
- Meta disallows creative that imitates platform UI misleadingly: no status bar, no platform
  glyphs, no fake close control.
