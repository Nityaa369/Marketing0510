28 Sep 2026, Ruchika: *"we will not switch off any ads, only swap landing pages"*, and build the
alternates from each bootcamp's best-performing pages. Output: `LP_Swaps_28Sep/` and
`LP_Swaps_28Sep_SEND_THIS.zip` (README is the upload guide).

**Method that worked.** Flag a page on IG+FB traffic only (WhatsApp inflates LPV), 7 full days,
results/LPV vs its own campaign (Poisson p<0.01 and at most 75% of the rate) or vs its own previous
3 weeks. Donors = highest conversion that still clears a 90% lower bound. Keep the current page's
shell and first fold (ad match), rebuild the body on the donor argument, no new facts.
`LP_Swaps_28Sep/check_swap.py` gates checkout labels, injected-code leftovers, dashes, banned
blocks, logistics unchanged, length, reader h2, and <65% overlap with donors and the old page.

**What the build uncovered on live pages** (all unsourced or wrong, left to the team):
- All 81 Senior Consulting 3-5 Oct pages and 53 ads say 6 to 9 PM; the dates sheet says 2 to 5 PM
  (days 1-2) and 7 to 10 PM (day 3). Unresolved as of 28 Sep.
- SQE community pages still sell 9 / 27 Sep batches; no later date exists on any page.
- DP transcription-rcm's day outputs and faculty name are not in the October script.
- SC ERP/teacher pages carried unsourced US/EU/Indian rate bands; removed on the swaps.

**Judge each swap at 7 full days, 20+ results**, against the before-numbers in each notes file.
Arbitration ad01 had no statistically better donor: it is a refresh, weaker evidence.
