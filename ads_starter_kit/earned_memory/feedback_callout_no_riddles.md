On 21 Sep 2026 Ramanuj flagged the CF wealth manager call-out "whose client's largest asset is not in the portfolio?" with "what does this mean... sounds like a riddle". Four other call-outs had the same shape: "prices the product but never the company", "covering only what the filings cover", "reporting the numbers somebody else prices" and "whose files stop at repayment". All five were rewritten as plain statements, for example "whose clients own a business nobody has valued?". The rewritten faces scored 3/3 on the blind read.

**Why:** A call-out is meant to be recognised in two seconds. Withholding the referent turns recognition into a puzzle. It is Rule 2, applied to the ICP line.

**How to apply:** Name the thing directly (the business, the loan, the valuation) instead of describing it by what it is not or where it is missing. The blind read passed every riddle at 2/3, so it can't be relied on here: read every call-out out loud and ask "what is 'it'?". Related to [[feedback-offer-line-names-the-prize]] and [[demand-clause-must-take-the-object]]. When Ramanuj supplies copy, use it word for word even if a gate would object, and make room by cutting chrome, not his words. His CFA line kept "opportunity funnel / capital funnel" undefined; the blind reader could not name the job (2/3).
