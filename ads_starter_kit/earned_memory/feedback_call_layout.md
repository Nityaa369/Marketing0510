Built 21 Sep 2026 for the LawSikho Data Protection 17-19 Oct wave from an AG1 static
Ruchika supplied ("Your immune system / rings ..." with one Accept slider). Her idea:
"they are getting a call from a better opportunity". Layout `call` in
`adkit/layouts_custom.py`; catalogued in `adkit/styles.py`.

**Why it needed adapting, not copying:** the AG1 slider is iOS's answer control - a
green phone glyph and a fake "Accept". SOP/10 forbids imitating system UI misleadingly
("no status bar, no platform glyphs, no fake close control, brand colours throughout, and
the only control-looking element is the real CTA"). So: brand colours, an ARROW knob,
no Decline, and the pill's label IS the CTA. And AG1 carries two lines because its brand
is known; the alert layout died that same day for "so much information is missing", so
the call face carries the priced face's full content (ICP question, chips, the winning
headline as the caller, a caller card with definition and take-aways).

**How to apply:**
- On a DARK ground the highlight rule inverts. A light-tint `<em>` on the reader's title
  read as de-emphasis on the first render; the title goes full white and the rest of the
  question takes the tint. The gates did not catch this - it is a judgement.
- Centre everything. simplicity L1 only fires on competing LEFT edges when most blocks
  are not centred, so a centred call screen passes by construction.
- The field must be on the large type (Ramanuj, CF wave, same day: "where does it say
  build a career in corporate finance" / "join what"). The caller name reads as
  "Data protection, the practice area with no seniors / is calling..." and the pill says
  "Join the Rs 10 bootcamp".
- First face (01 law firm associates) blind-read 3/3 against a 2/3 control floor; fit,
  gaps and simplicity clean. No live cost yet.

**Caller lines, learned the same day:** a caller line must be a NOUN PHRASE (it is the
thing that "is calling..."), but it must still carry the READER and a VERB. creative_judge
C1 read seven bare fragments ("The data-protection side of every deal, under the new rules")
as riddles, on Ramanuj's own evidence ("ONE PREPARATION. FIVE BOARD ROLES." rejected as
cryptic). Adding "...which you can lead" / "...you already advise" took the judge from 13
to 20 straight accepts. Write dates as "17 to 19 Oct": C7 counts an en dash as an em-dash.
On a dark gradient, palettes in one hue family (gold, olive, maroon) read close at
thumbnail size even above dE 12, because every accent fades into near-black.
