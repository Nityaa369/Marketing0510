Across every Sep/Oct bootcamp ad with 20+ buyers (175 ads, 11 campaigns, each indexed to its
own campaign par, faces classified by eye from the live creative):

| design family | ads | buyers | index |
|---|---|---|---|
| collage (CF wave 3) | 1 | 117 | 0.55 |
| dark object photo | 10 | 808 | 0.76 |
| **named-work list (`launchlist`)** | **29** | **5,568** | **0.79** |
| video | 5 | 637 | 0.90 |
| translate | 4 | 217 | 0.92 |
| unclassified | 92 | 17,608 | 0.99 |
| toon / meme / quiz | 13 | 2,142 | 1.08 |
| verb headline + middle widget | 18 | 1,380 | 1.50 |

**The controlled read is inside one campaign.** Senior Consulting 3-5 Oct, same weeks, same
product, par Rs 140: the `SC_Oct3|w9_*` named-work-list faces ran **0.67x on 2,060 buyers**; the
same campaign's `sr*`/`sc*`/`ap*`/`flm_*` faces ran **1.50x on 1,380**. 2.2x apart, nothing else
different. The same structure carries Global Patent's cheapest faces (veterinary 0.59,
microbiologists 0.75), US Corporate Law's (cs_inhouse_mnc 0.75) and Academic Writing's
(medical_researchers 0.78): four campaigns, two brands.

**Why:** this supersedes [[project_style_index_sep23]], which found no repeatable design win.
That pull classified by ad name and left most ads unclassified; this one pulled the live
creative images and looked at them.

**What separates the winners from the losers, on the faces themselves:**
1. The headline is a **destination noun phrase**. "Launch your own hospital consulting business."
   beats "Get paid to cut their cloud bill." Every 1.50x face led with a verb or a clever turn.
   This matches the approved-headline corpus ([[feedback_checkers_refit_basis]]).
2. **No middle widget.** Every loser carried an extra table, bar chart or two-column panel
   between the headline and the list. Not one winner did. This is "wayyy too much text" and
   "too clever with the image and the hook", measured.
3. The work is **named in a numbered list**, label plus one ordinary-words line.
4. A small positive qualifier pill removes the barrier ("No capital. No need to quit.").

**It is Corporate Finance's own structure.** adkit built `brief` from
`CorporateFinance_MediaBuyer_15-17Aug` faces 4 and 1 (Rs 164, Rs 170), the batch approved 7 of 7.
`launchlist` (adkit/layouts_custom.py, built 1 Oct 2026) is that order with the device panel
removed. Other camps borrowed it and ran it up to 5,568 buyers.

**Honest limit:** 43 of 175 faces were verified by eye, so this reads the top and bottom bands,
not a census. The collage row is one ad.
