**Diagnosed 16 Sep 2026** when Ruchika asked for a visual refresh, then a landing page refresh,
of the Arbitration community ads.

- The three June 2026 pages (`11june-main/-roi/-merged`, URLs unchanged) fell from **13-17% of
  page visitors buying in weeks 1-2 to 4-9% by weeks 8-10**. It was gradual and hit all three
  pages equally, so the pages did not break. The audience wore out (lifetime frequency 4.8 on
  Rs 8.67L). Campaign cost per buyer went Rs 228 to Rs 600-800.
- New pages launched into that audience converted *worse* than the tired ones.
- The June winners had already been re-skinned into `[Community]_14Day_Arbitration_12Sep`.
  Click-through held (0.57% vs 0.55%) but the ads ran at Rs 518-2,099, because the same people
  were clicking and not buying.
- Every Arbitration ad ever run named "lawyers" cut by seniority or situation. **None named the
  practice the lawyer already runs**, which `SOP/08` §2 measures as the cheapest cut.

**Why:** a face or page refresh aimed at the same readers was the instinctive fix and the
evidence ruled it out. Recording it so the next rising-cost Arbitration call starts from reach,
not redesign.

**How to apply:** new ICP list at `docs/ICPs_Arbitration_Community_16Sep2026.md`, spec at
`Arbitration_Community14Day_Wave1_MediaBuyer_16Sep/value_props.md` (11 practice-split ICPs, not
yet approved as of 16 Sep). One check was never run: GrowthX paid counts against Meta purchases
for Jul-Aug, which would rule out a tracking fault. Women advocates (Rs 371, n=294, July
bootcamp) is the recommended relaunch control cell. Related: [[feedback-icp-is-the-biggest-lever]],
[[feedback-same-copy-different-face-10x]], [[feedback-load-rate-is-not-traffic]].
