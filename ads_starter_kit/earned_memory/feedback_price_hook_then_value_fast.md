On a price-led creative, opening on the ₹10 is right, but the value prop has to follow
immediately. Approved 27 Aug 2026 on the Women AI Bootcamp UGC video script: *"i like that
you start with 10 rs but then quickly move to the value prop."*

**Why:** the price stops the scroll but says nothing about what she gets. The first draft
spent 0:10 to 0:42 answering "what is the catch" and only reached the field definition at
0:42. Raising an objection before she wants anything just gives her a reason to leave.

**How to apply:** price alone on screen for ~4 seconds, then a hinge line ("here is what ten
rupees buys you"), then the better job and the field-definition sentence. Value fully
delivered by ~0:40 of a 90s cut. The catch, the live-only rule and the refund come after,
once she already wants it. Live-only stops being fine print there and becomes the reason the
price is ₹10, which is cheap trust. See [[feedback_blind_reader_value_prop]] and
[[feedback_house_ad_formula]].
