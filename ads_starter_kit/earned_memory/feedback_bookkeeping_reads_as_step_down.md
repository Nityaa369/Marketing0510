25 Sep 2026, US Accounting wave 2 (`USAccounting_Wave2_MediaBuyer_10-12Oct`). The first blind
read of 12 faces, with the five questions, came back **step DOWN on 7 of 12**. Every reason was
the same word: "bookkeeping is below a trained accountant's level", "same routine work with a
different client", "extra bookkeeping hours". All 3 priced controls read UP. They sell "build a
US accounting practice" (us22), "a dollar second income" (us05) and "US accounting work" (us25).
The 17 Sep search wave had put "US bookkeeping in QuickBooks and Xero" on every face, because
naming the tool fixed question 3. That fix is right, but the tool must not become the job.

**Why:** Rule 1 says offer a better job. For an accountant, "bookkeeping" names a smaller
version of the job they already have. Questions 1 to 3 pass on these faces, so only question 5
catches it.

**How to apply:** the head and the delivery line name the step up: US accounting work, your own
US clients (who pay in dollars; an Indian firm's employee does not), a practice of your own, and
the Enrolled Agent licence. QuickBooks and Xero stay, as the tools. Bookkeeping, payroll and
reconciliations appear as tasks inside the work, on the page, never as the headline. Always ask
question 5.
