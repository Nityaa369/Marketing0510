Senior AI (SkillArbitrage, senior professionals 45+, consulting camp) has run **four
campaign generations** across three ad accounts. A 1,100-day Meta pull on 23 Sep 2026
covers all of them: `ad-analysis-shared/reports/senior_2026-09-23.json`, rendered by
`senior_alltime_board.py` -> `senior_alltime_board.html`.

| Generation | Campaign | Account | Judged on | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 2026 | `VT_SA_2_SeniorAI_14-Mar-2026_BC_LP1..LP5` | Skill Arbitrage_2 | lead (free camp) | Rs 356/lead |
| Jun 2026 | `SR_SA_V_senior_ai_27-29June` | SkillArbitrage_V | Rs 10 buyer | Rs 581 |
| Aug 2026 | `Senior ai bootcamp 22 nd aug` | Skill Arbitrage | Rs 10 buyer | Rs 220 |
| Oct 2026 | `SeniorConsulting_BC_3-5Oct2026_CBO_v1` | Skill Arbitrage | Rs 10 buyer | Rs 215 (live) |

**March is a different funnel type** - free camp, 1,794 leads and 81 later purchases - so
its per-buyer numbers (Rs 5,000-12,000) are not a Rs 10 CPP and must never be blended with
the other three. See [[feedback_ads_report_columns]].

All-time cheapest Rs 10 buyers (20+ buyers only): `SC_Oct3|sc2_05_banker_panels` Rs 167,
`SC_Oct3|sr10_erp_direct` Rs 169, `SC_Oct3|sr13_trusted_numbers` Rs 173,
`Senior45|Rented_Exec|sc_01` Rs 190 (Aug), `Senior45|US_SmallBiz|us01` Rs 203 on the
largest spend in the product (Rs 1.59L, 781 buyers).

What repeats across generations: **name the reader's own trained discipline** (ERP
implementer, credit officer, QA owner, security head) - see [[feedback_icp_is_the_biggest_lever]];
**sell consulting, not AI** (the AI-led ads are the dearest in every camp); the **rented
executive / fractional** angle and the **banker-to-small-business** pairing price well every
time they run; the **side-of-the-9-to-5** angle is the only one that priced well in all four.
CTR runs backwards here as usual - `si11_defence_discipline` has the best CTR on the product
(2.26%) and is 22nd on cost per buyer - see [[feedback_paychart_and_ctr_traps]].

Lead-level detail for Aug and Jun (funnel-platform leads, not Meta) is in
`senior_board.html` / `senior_board.py`: ranking on leads reorders the winners, and the
defence ad is the cheapest lead in the account at Rs 119 while being 7th of 8 on buyers.
