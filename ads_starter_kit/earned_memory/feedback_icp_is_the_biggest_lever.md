Measured 31 Aug 2026 over 128 ads / Rs 2,591,070, Skill Arbitrage account. Written up in
`SOP/08_ICP_SELECTION.md`; LP half in `SOP/05_LANDING_PAGES.md`.

**Five ICP archetypes, normalised to each ad's own campaign median (1.00 = par):**
B life situation 0.96 | A occupation adding a line 0.98 | C aspiration/abstract 1.05 |
E broad population 1.15 | **D device-led with no ICP named 1.22**. Only A and B are worth
building. Every ad over Rs 280 in the corpus is C or D.

**The strongest single rule: name the discipline the reader already trained in.** Own
discipline indexes 0.95, generic/distant 1.18, replicated in 4 of 4 campaigns where both ran
(1.10x to 2.17x penalty). Patent camp: biomedical Rs 91, semicon Rs 121, agri-food Rs 131,
ayurveda Rs 135 vs Exam-window Rs 203, Exam-2027 Rs 251, Calendar Rs 285. Manufactured
urgency is the expensive way to sell; a named discipline is the cheap way.

**The landing page template is already solved - stop redesigning it.** All seven Corporate
Finance LPs are structurally identical (same 10 h2 sections, 1712-1737 words) yet convert
9.3% to 20.7%. Within-kit spread is 2.1x to 2.9x in every kit measured, and it tracks the
ICP, not the page. Benchmarks: free bootcamp LPs ~30.5% of landing-page views, paid Rs 10
camps ~9.6%. About 22% of clicks never reach the page at all (median click-to-LPV 78%), and
that gap does not explain conversion (r=+0.09) - chase it in the deploy pipeline.

**The worked pair, same kit, same week, same skeleton, 2.2x apart:** "A loan consultant whose
every conversation ends at a loan?" 20.7% vs "A retired banker who still wants to work, from
home?" 9.3%. The first names a CEILING, the second an ASPIRATION. That is the test.

**Why:** we kept treating ICP selection as upstream admin and creative as the lever. It is the
other way round.

**How to apply:** get the ICP list and value_props.md approved before any face exists; score
each candidate on Identity / Pressure / Adjacency / Ceiling. Related:
