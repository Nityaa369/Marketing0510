Measured 30 Sep 2026, `TechLaw_BC_24-26Oct2026_CBO` (LawSikho Ad Account 4), 25-30 Sep:
Rs 1,44,236, 340 buyers, **Rs 424/buyer**. August's `Techlaw_BC_15AUG`: Rs 288 on 1,033.

- **Buyers per click went UP**: 6.6% Oct vs 5.4% Aug. Page and offer are not the problem.
- **CPM went up**: Rs 255 vs Rs 162 (incl. GST). CTR 0.91% vs 1.04%.
- **Not the market**: IP law (Main Bootcamp), same account, same week, identical targeting
  (broad, Advantage+ audience, 25-65, no interests), ran CPM Rs 119 and Rs 282/buyer.
- Per-ad CPM follows the reader the face attracts: law-college rerun 168, women returning 174,
  vs US paralegal 296, startup clients 465. Beginner / career-break faces buy cheap impressions.
- August's volume engine `hottest` (451 buyers, Rs 272, "no IT degree, law school or metro")
  got only Rs 1,473 as `TechLaw_Rerun_tr01_hottest` in the October CBO.
- Only October face clearly under the campaign: `TechLaw_20T_04_lawfirm_tmt` Rs 285, n=44.

**Meme evidence, corrects the 23 Sep style index:** `iplaw_sep26_meme_mm02_lawgrads_2016`
(2016/2026 then-vs-now panel, call-out "LAW GRADUATES WHO NEVER PRACTISED") took Rs 58k,
**211 buyers at Rs 276 vs campaign Rs 282** (0.98x). A meme can carry volume at par; it is
still not a discount. The other four IP memes got under Rs 700 each.

**How to apply:** for Tech Law October, new faces aimed at the cheap-impression readers
(never-practised graduates, small-town lawyers, women returning), in their own ad set so the
36-ad CBO does not starve them. Not new ICPs: non-lawyers are struck
([[project-techlaw-nonlawyer-icps-rejected]]) and niche practice readers pull toward pricier
audiences. Related: [[project-style-index-sep23]], [[feedback-icp-is-the-biggest-lever]].
