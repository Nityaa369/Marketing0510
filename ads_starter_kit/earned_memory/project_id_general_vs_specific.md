Measured 28 Sep 2026 over every free ID bootcamp ad since July (Jul Rs 62, Aug 69, Sep 77, Oct 63
per lead), each ad against its own camp's average, ads with 20+ leads:

- **General retired, no occupation:** 10 ads, 2 beat par, median 1.34x. The two that beat it are
  the cheapest ID ads ever run: rid_quote_ 0.69x on 4,180, Boardroom_Retirement_ 0.78x on 2,708.
- **Named occupation:** 16 ads, 7 beat par, median 1.06x. The winners also carry a retirement frame
  (V3_educator_retired, retired banker, vid6_educator, vid2_infra).
- **"15+ years experience", no retirement, no occupation:** 4 ads, 0 beat par, median 1.90x.
- **Women:** 6 ads, 0 beat par, median 1.61x. Do not propose a women cell for ID again without new evidence.
- May 2026 ID campaigns (VT_SA_, SR_SA_) ran Rs 270-548 per lead, a different funnel; never blend.

**Why:** answers "does specificity win" for this line: neither outright. Retirement is the frame
that works; occupation adds reliability, not ceiling. Confounded by format (several specific
winners are videos, the general winners statics) and by CBO starving early losers.

**How to apply:** for ID, keep a general "recently retired" control face beside any split, and
split retirement by situation (this year, VRS, age 60, hometown, ex-KMP) rather than stripping
the retirement frame. Calendar wave built this way: IndependentDirector_Calendar_MediaBuyer_10-12Oct.

Also found: the IICA test exemption for ex-directors/KMP is **3 years** (rule 6(4)), not 10; the
10-year limb is for practising advocates, CAs, cost accountants and CSs. 
**The October ID script (checked 28 Sep 2026) teaches a 6-month plan, not a 90-day one.** Day 2:
"the 6-month plan to build your personal brand, crack the IICA exam, learn corporate governance";
registration ("How to register on the MCA and IICA website") is Day 3; no WhatsApp group is promised.
The Oct chat/search kits and the Sep donor page all say "90-day" - untraceable. The Day 1 script
names real learners usable as proof: Golock Chandra Sahoo (retired Senior Audit Officer, Ganesh
Infra), Sanjay Roye (Navy 36 yrs, Sachee Rome), Santanu Sengupta (banker, Harmony Lifestyles).
