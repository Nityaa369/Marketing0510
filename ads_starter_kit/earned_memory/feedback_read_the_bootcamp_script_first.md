22 Sep 2026, Ruchika: "review all the ad and lp content once using bootcamp scripts". The
LawSikho Data Protection 17-19 Oct kit had passed every gate, yet all 20 ads and pages promised
four take-aways drafted from the August camp: a real company's data mapped live, a
data-processing agreement, a 72-hour breach note, a board note. The October scripts
(`docs/October_2026_bootcamp_scripts.md` index; summary in
`docs/DataProtection_17-19Oct2026_curriculum.md`) teach none of them as written. They teach a
DPIA live on a sample startup, privacy policy gap review, data subject requests, breach response,
cross-border transfer, and finding clients in India and abroad, plus 23 giveaways.

**Why:** no gate checks content against the curriculum; blind and closing readers score
comprehension, not truth. Same failure as Ramanuj's CF rejection ("corporate finance work is not
valuation - please read the bootcamp script").

**How to apply:** before writing any face row, DAYS/KEEP section or close_line, read that camp's
prep doc and day scripts (Claude in Chrome, `/mobilebasic`), write a curriculum file with a
"NOT taught" list, and grep the pages against it. Never import the scripts' income, job-count or
"no competition" claims. The scripts brand Day 2 as SkillArbitrage; confirm the brand.
