**1 Oct 2026. Ruchika, looking at one Corporate Finance wave 6 face: "is this not a riddle?"**
She was right, and it was three riddles stacked across all twenty faces.

1. **The headline named a chore, not a career.** "Write the loan proposal, not just fill the
   form." A loan consultant reads that as *different paperwork at the same desk*. No face in the
   wave said the reader ends up in corporate finance. That is the 21 Sep Notify rejection
   repeated: *"where does it say build a career in corporate finance" / "join what"*
   ([[feedback_offer_line_names_the_prize]]), and it is Rule 1's "more work at the same desk".
2. **The qualifier compared to an unstated baseline.** "The business pays you on delivery, not
   when the deal closes" points at a success-fee arrangement the face never mentions.
3. **Ellipsis and vague agents.** "chosen before the lender is", "a document somebody is paid to
   write", "not just wait for a good one", "from the side that builds it", and "a raise" as a
   bare noun on 11 faces and all 20 pages.

**The repair that worked, in one pass:** head = **"Start a corporate finance practice, on the
loan files you already place."** A destination noun phrase naming the career, tied to the asset
the reader already owns. Qualifier = two plain facts, no comparison: "Paid for each document. No
CFA or MBA needed." Every row a complete clause with a named agent. The judge went 13 accept / 7
with-changes to **20 accept / 0 with-changes**.

**THE IMPORTANT PART, and the limit of the gate.** Twenty blind readers scored the original faces
**3/3** and read them as a step UP. They passed because they answer "what job would I be doing"
*with effort*. Ruchika saw it in one glance.

> `blind_reader.py` measures whether a face CAN be decoded. It cannot measure whether a face
> reads without decoding, and it cannot see that a headline sells a chore instead of a career.
> A 3/3 blind read is not evidence that a hook is not a riddle.

Before trusting a blind read, read every headline alone and ask: **does this name a career, or a
task?** If a reader could do the thing in the headline tomorrow at their current desk, it is a
chore and the face will be rejected however the gate scores it.
