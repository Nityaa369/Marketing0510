17 Sep 2026, on the US Accounting 10-12 Oct kit: "nothing should be factually inaccurate...
make sure all statements are factually accurate". Also: keep the ad consistent with the page
(the Tally ad named Wave and Zoho Books; the camp teaches QuickBooks and Xero, so the AD changed).

What a source check found in copy that had been live on September pages and reused:
- "USA short of 300,000 accountants in 2026" misquotes BLS data: 300,000+ accountants and
  auditors LEFT their jobs over two years (WBUR Feb 2023).
- "1 in 3 US firms send work to India": no source. AICPA 2023 MAP survey: 25% of firms use
  offshore workers (Journal of Accountancy, Nov 2024), not India-specific.
- "5,216 Enrolled Agents in India": no source found anywhere.
- Rupee pay ladder (Rs 50-70k bookkeeping, Rs 1L+ EA): contradicted by salary sites. Replaced
  with the BLS US median for bookkeeping clerks, labelled "not what you will earn".
- "EA exam taken in Indian cities": false. IRS FAQ: international testing is remote-proctored
  only, and international scheduling was delayed with no new dates (Sept 2026).
- "Live on Zoom" and "certificate of participation": invented; September pages say "live
  online" and "certificate of completion".
- Headlines that generalise about how all US firms or clients hire ("US firms hire on skill,
  not the CA title", "US clients check the books, not the gap", "Skip the entry-level desk")
  and "the same audit work" (only a US CPA signs a US audit) were rewritten.

**Why:** copy was trusted because it had shipped; nobody had opened a source.
**How to apply:** before handing over any ad or page, list every statement about the world and
open a primary source for each (irs.gov, bls.gov, icai.org, vendor product pages). Unsourced
generalisations get "some"/"can" or are cut. Camp logistics come from a live page, never
assumed. Stories stay but are attributed "as published". Logs for this kit:
session scratchpad `factcheck/`. Related: [[reference-usaccounting-testimonials]],
[[feedback-contradiction-means-failed-test]].
