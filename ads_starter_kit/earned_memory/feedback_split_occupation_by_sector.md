Ruchika, 17 Sep 2026, reviewing a US Accounting ICP list: *"for the HR ad campaigns, the HR
in hospital did super well, it was not obvious but it unlocked a whole new demographic"*,
and *"can't these be at least two separate ICPs"* about "accountants doing entries,
reconciliations and payroll".

Measured: `hospital` in `Hr_bootcamp_22nd_august` ran Rs 135 a buyer on 64, **0.77x** the
camp's Rs 175 average, on only Rs 8,658 of budget. The same camp's hospitality cells were
weak (`hospitality_qsr` 1.12x on 8, `w10_52_hospitality` 1 buyer).

**Why:** SOP/08's degree split (the Patent lesson) is one axis. Two more exist, and I was not
using them: **the sector the person works in** and **the job titles hiding inside a combined
description**. A reader who ticks "hospital accounts" or "AP executive" on a form is a
different box from "accountant".

**How to apply:** when building an ICP list, (1) break any "X doing A, B and C" into the
separate job titles people actually hold; (2) try the occupation inside two or three sectors
where the work is genuinely specialist (hospital billing, contractor job costing), and hold
sectors where it is not (hospitality). Related: [[feedback-icp-is-the-biggest-lever]],
[[project-usaccounting-icp-history]].
