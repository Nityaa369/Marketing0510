Camp: 29, 30, 31 Aug 2026, ₹10, SkillArbitrage.

**21 Aug.** Ad work was narrowed to **19 named ICPs** drawn from the 37 in
`D2C_VALUEPROP_AND_ICPS_HANDOFF.md`, with format inspiration images supplied one at a
time and matched to an ICP.

**22 Aug: scope widened.** Ruchika supplied a **40 candidate ICP list** (families A to F,
built on the reworked 21 Aug value proposition, not the D2C salary-ladder memo) and asked
for ads covering every candidate with no ad yet. That list, not the 19 ICP shortlist, is
now the target. **All 40 now have at least one creative.**

Coverage as of 22 Aug: the approved cells in `new ad types/_ALL_ADS_22Aug/` plus the 23
statics in `new ad types/wave6_contentwriting_29-31Aug/`. That wave has its own
`README.md` recording which cell covers which ICP number and what was wrong with each of
the 13 rebuilds, so do not re-derive that here.

Two decisions from that wave worth carrying forward:

- **`cw_47` was repointed**, not just fixed. It duplicated `cw_32`'s ICP, so it moved to
  ICP 21 `appraisal-no-raise`, which had no ad. When two cells collide on an ICP, prefer
  repointing one to an uncovered ICP over building a second creative.
- **`cw_51` is deliberately a second creative** for ICP 32, which `cw_35` already covers
  as an approved cell. Route them as separate ads rather than treating one as a
  replacement.

**Landing pages remain the standing blocker for the whole pack**, and it is not one that
can be closed from the creative side: Rule 5 needs a real GrowthX funnel slug per cell
and a browser test buy, and no slugs exist for these cells. `preflight.py` reports a
no-landing-page blocker on every kit; that is expected, and it gets flagged rather than
hidden. Ask for the slugs before offering to build LPs.
