- v2 (stacked card, clean ground): "now the translate layout is gone". The side-by-side pair IS

  right = the paid version), so the left pane has no dead corner. Approved direction.
- Right tab: "After the bootcamp" (her idea). Payoff line: "You offer paid research writing to
  teams abroad." - she removed "can"; she prefers the direct "You offer".
- She wants the value prop explicit (more money from skills they already have) and every ICP a
  step up; "No PhD needed" only in the terms strip, never a hook.

inside that structure. **How to apply:** keep tabs + ⇄ + two panes; fill space with matched
rows, not by changing the skeleton. Related: [[feedback-callout-no-riddles]],
