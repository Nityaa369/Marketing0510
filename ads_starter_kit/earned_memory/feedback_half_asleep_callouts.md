**Why:** I had packed a pain clause into every callout ("whose fees move only when matters
move") and a truth-hedge tail into every caller line ("as India's new rules phase in"): 9 to 15
words each. The August winners that were actually priced read "Lawyers:" + "Start a privacy law
practice on the side". The pain clause was my invention and was never tested.

**How to apply:** callout is the reader only ("A litigator?"). Headline states the gain with a
verb and names the field: "Better-paid data privacy retainers you can add to your practice",
"A better-paid Data Privacy Analyst role you can step up to". Use step up / better-paid /
higher-value / early start / new line of cases; no income figures. One shared plain definition
across a wave. A soft-true hedge is only needed when a line claims newness; if it does not, drop
the tail. Reference LP Ramanuj liked: `reference_lps/RAMANUJ_LIKED_cf_loan_consultant_17-19Oct.html`
(short hero: reader question, elig strip, one prop line, one definition, "no CFA needed" badge;
body built from the bootcamp script: what the work is as 12 named jobs, key takeaways, three
evenings in detail, 5-step first client, ten things worth knowing, FAQ accordions). Do not copy
its en dashes or its "Is this for you?" heading.
