Ramanuj, 24 Sep 2026: *"non-lawyer ICPs do not work for tech law."*

That strikes the **whole wave**, not a reader in it: company secretaries, software vendor
managers, NBFC compliance officers, chartered accountants, data scientists and AI engineers,
software consultants, startup MBAs, cyber security professionals, content writers, virtual
assistants (ICPs 6, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 35, 38, 39, 40 in
`docs/ICPs_TechLaw_24-26Oct2026.md`). **Do not rebuild them for Tech Law.**

What this cost, and the lesson: twenty faces and ten landing pages were built across a Meta
carousel wave and a Google single-face wave, and every one passed all eleven gates and
blind-read 3/3 before the verdict arrived. The gates measure clarity and compliance; they
cannot tell you an audience is wrong for a product. That is round one of approval, and the
ICP table should have gone to Ramanuj on its own — see [[feedback-only-ramanuj-approval-counts]].

The camp's own script does invite non-lawyers in as many words ("if you are a professional
who is not a lawyer... you are most welcome as well"), so a welcome line in a script is not
evidence that an ICP will buy. See [[feedback-read-the-bootcamp-script-first]].

What survives for the 24-26 Oct camp: the 10 lawyer ICPs, in two formats pointing at the
same 10 pages - `TechLaw_Carousels_24-26Oct_SEND_THIS` (Meta) and
`TechLaw_Google_Lawyers_24-26Oct_SEND_THIS` (Google, see
[[feedback-google-wave-is-one-search-face]]).

The verdict is about **Tech Law only**. Several of these readers have their own evidence on
other camps, and nothing here transfers to Data Privacy or the SkillArbitrage products.
