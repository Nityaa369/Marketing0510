On 22 Sep 2026 Ramanuj rejected all 19 CF notify landing pages: "who said valuing the business is what you charge for", "corporate finance work is not valuation", "please read the bootcamp script to understand the work". The kit had inherited valuation as the definition of the field from the August faces, and the pages were argued around being paid for a valuation. The script says the work is raising money for businesses, as debt or equity: find the owners who need money (the opportunity funnel) and the people who have it (the capital funnel), prepare the pitch deck, projections, information memorandum or loan proposal, and close the deal. It is paid as a success fee on the money raised or sanctioned, plus fixed fees for the documents. Valuation is one skill taught on Day 1.

**Why:** A definition copied from an earlier kit passed every gate. The gates test clarity, not truth, so a wrong model of the work reaches Ramanuj untouched.

**How to apply:** Before writing the definition line, the deliverables or the LP body, read that camp's own script ([[reference-october-bootcamp-scripts]]) and write a one-page brief from it (the CF one is `CorporateFinance_Notify_MediaBuyer_17-19Oct/SCRIPT_BRIEF_22Sep.md`). Say what the fee is paid on. Never write "X is what you charge for" unless the script says so. Related: [[feedback-offer-line-names-the-prize]].
