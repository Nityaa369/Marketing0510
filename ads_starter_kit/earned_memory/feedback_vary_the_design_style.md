A wave must carry different design styles, not one shape repeated.

**Why:** asked for 31 Aug 2026 — *"all of the different design styles that have gone
live, what have performed well so that there is a variety in the ads designed."* It is
the visual twin of [[feedback-lps-in-a-kit-must-differ]]: a reader sees several ads from
one campaign. `IndependentDirector_Wave1` is 7 of 10 photo-led.

**How to apply:** pick from `SOP/10_DESIGN_STYLES.md` (generated by `adkit/styles.py`
from the device names the kit generators write in their own docstrings, the six approved
hook types, and the explored formats in `new ad types/`). Check the mix with
`python -m adkit.styles --kit <folder>`, which warns at 60% one style.

**Only two styles have a measured cost index and both are worse than par:** the pay chart
at **2.34** (replicated across three product lines, and banned from the face on
compliance grounds anyway) and toon/chat at **1.22**. Everything else prints `-` — about
a dozen live ads can be matched to a classifiable face, so there is no basis for a
per-style cost table. **An unpriced style is not a safe style.** Do not let the catalogue
be quoted as if it ranked styles by performance.

Also honest: only 157 of 644 faces carry a device signal in their name or markup. The
counts are not a census, and the value of the file is the catalogue and the examples.
