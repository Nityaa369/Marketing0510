# GOLDEN notes — the hand-written WHY IT PASSED entries

Written by hand in August 2026 and **kept verbatim**. `GOLDEN.md` is now
generated (`python -m adkit.golden`); this file is not, because these are
judgements no script can re-derive. Add to it by hand when a new face is
worth explaining rather than just listing.

### GOLD-01 · `mh01_corridor.png` · reader's-own-voice speech bubble · approved 22 Aug 2026

**Kicker:** COURT LITIGATORS
**On the image:** "Sir, I will call you back. I think I will just get into arbitration instead
of waiting in the corridor all day for matters to be called."
**Caption:** How to create mass hysteria in a court corridor.
**Offer block:** Join our 21 day arbitration training for ₹200.
**Explainer:** We give you the 21 skills, a drafting template for every one, and the method to
run an arbitration from notice to award, in 21 days.

**WHY IT PASSED**
- The line in the bubble is something this exact person would say out loud, first person,
  present tense — zero decoding required.
- The "mass hysteria" framing is only the caption sitting on top of a literal, plain statement.
  The cleverness decorates the hook; it is never the hook itself.
- The reader recognises the moment (a call, a decision made mid-corridor) before they've read a
  single word of body copy.
- Scored zero findings on every mechanical check run against it — the cleanest ad in the whole
  approved set.

### GOLD-04 · `usmb_05_agency_copy.png` · quoted-self mirror + direct headline · approved

**ICP band:** AGENCY COPYWRITERS ON INDIAN RETAINERS
**Quote box ("YOUR WEEK, PROBABLY"):** Client ka budget kam hai · Revision rounds khatam nahi
hote · Retainer renew nahi hua.
**Headline:** Write the same ads for clients who pay more.
**Body:** US small businesses and startups cannot hire world class marketing talent locally. They
hire it remotely, and India is where they look.

**WHY IT PASSED**
- The quote box uses the reader's actual code-switched vocabulary — not an English paraphrase of
  a Hindi complaint. Recognition is instant because the words are theirs.
- The headline states the exact trade (same work, better-paying clients) with zero metaphor
  between the reader and the claim — this is Section 4's "grievance-as-headline" type, paired
  with the quote-box hook.
- No pronoun stands in for a noun anywhere in the headline or payoff.

### GOLD-06 · `hoc_01_gap.png` · interrogated-list / self-audit device · approved

**ICP band:** WRITERS AIMING AT HEAD OF CONTENT
**Headline:** Build the skills the top content job asks for.
**Device:** two-part checklist — "YOU ALREADY HAVE" (writing and editing / research and
interviewing / managing a calendar) against "THE JOB TURNS ON THESE" (calculating ROI / mapping
content to funnel stage / reporting ROI in finance language).

**WHY IT PASSED**
- No hero image at all — the checklist itself is the entire visual, and it does the defining work
  Rule 2 requires without a separate sentence, because the second column names the gap in plain,
  nameable skills.
- Every row in "you already have" is indisputably true of the reader; every row in "the job turns
  on" is a nameable skill, not a vague aspiration ("leadership," "vision").
- One argument (you're halfway there, ROI is the other half), not four separate benefits, even
  though the device shows six rows.

### GOLD-07 · `hoc_02_ladder.png` · progression-ladder device · approved

**ICP band:** SENIOR WRITERS WHO WANT TO LEAD
**Headline:** Get past the rung where writers stall.
**Device:** a five-row ladder — Writer: you are given the brief / Senior Writer: you write the
brief / Most stop here: every rung above is measured in ROI / Content Lead: you own the ROI of
the content / Head of Content: you own the plan and its ROI.

**WHY IT PASSED**
- A different device shape from GOLD-06 (a progression, not a checklist) proving Section 4's
  device system isn't limited to one table format — it's a family of shapes, each still capped at
  ~5 rows with one flat gloss line.
- "MOST STOP HERE" mid-ladder is the one line doing the emotional work (this is where you are
  stuck), and it's placed at the exact rung where the reader would recognise themselves — proving
  the identity/situation split (Section 2) applies inside a device, not just in a callout band.
- The headline is a grievance-as-headline type layered on top of the device — showing hook types
  from Section 4 can be combined with device types from Section 5 rather than picked exclusively.

### GOLD-08 · `hoc_03_question.png` · overheard-dialogue / interview exchange · approved

**ICP band:** WRITERS WHO WANT TO RUN CONTENT
**Headline:** One question decides who gets the job.
**Device:** three-line chat exchange — "Tell me about your best piece of content." / "It ranked,
and it got shared a lot." / "And what was the ROI on it?"

**WHY IT PASSED**
- The gap (not knowing the ROI) is never explained inside the bubbles — it's just the third,
  unanswered question. The reader supplies their own dread; the ad doesn't narrate it.
- Exactly 3 lines, as the type constraint requires — a fourth line (an answer, or an explanation)
  would have killed the tension the exchange is built on.
- The paragraph underneath is the only place the gap gets resolved into an offer — one resolution,
  stated once, not repeated across the device and the body copy.

### GOLD-09 · `wrw_04_talk.png` · overheard-dialogue, the domain-knowledge moat · approved 26 Aug 2026

**ICP band:** DIETITIAN OR HOME SCIENCE?
**Qualifier row:** DIETITIANS &middot; NUTRITIONISTS &middot; HOME SCIENCE GRADUATES
**Bubbles:**
&nbsp;&nbsp;"Tu abhi bhi ghar pe hai?"
&nbsp;&nbsp;"Kaam kar rahi hoon. I run a US dietitian's social media."
&nbsp;&nbsp;"But won't a practice want a social media expert?"
&nbsp;&nbsp;"Wahi to. Anyone can post 'eat clean'. I know what is actually true."
**Headline:** Get hired as a social media *virtual assistant.*
**Promise block:** WHAT WE DO IN 3 DAYS &mdash; "Social media assistant work means planning
the posts a practice puts out each week. In 3 days we teach you over 9 hours how women like
you land this work and earn in dollars."

**WHY IT PASSED**
- **The third bubble is the reader's own objection, said out loud by someone else.** "But
  won't a practice want a social media expert?" is exactly what she is thinking, and it is
  voiced by the friend rather than by the brand, so it does not read as a brand handling an
  objection. The fourth bubble answers it in her voice.
- **The moat is stated concretely, not claimed.** "Anyone can post 'eat clean'. I know what
  is actually true." Two real posts, one worthless and one only she can write. This is the
  status reversal the whole campaign turns on, and it lands in twelve words without an
  adjective.
- **An earlier draft of that third bubble read "But you are not a social media person?" and
  `simplicity` S2 BLOCKED it** as deficiency framing. Moving the doubt onto the employer
  ("won't a practice want...") kept the tension and cleared Rule 1. Worth copying: when an
  objection has to appear, put it on the buyer, never on the reader.
- **The band widened from one job title to a credential family** after a narrow "TRAINED AS
  A DIETITIAN?" excluded most of the reachable audience. Director section 2's "widen
  laterally" rule, applied: sibling identities at the same rung, in the qualifier row.
- **The role is named in the largest line.** An earlier version buried "virtual assistant"
  in a card footnote and the reviewer's question was "how do people know what job it is".
- Clean on the full `check_all` gate: hooks, C8, `hook_rules`, both halves of `simplicity`
  at the declared 130-word cap, and the creative judge.

## How to use this file

1. **Paste this whole file into context before generating any new hook or device.** Don't
   summarize it first — the point is the model pattern-matching against real examples, not a
   description of them.
2. **When picking a hook type (`AD_CREATION_DIRECTOR.md` Section 4), find its GOLD- entry above
   and read the actual copy before writing your own.** Match the *word count and directness*, not
   the literal words.
3. **Before showing any new ad, run the blind-reader test (Director Section 12.5) and ask: would
   this sit comfortably in this file, or does Ramanuj see something none of these 8 have?** If the
   new ad needs more setup, more decoding, or more words than any entry here to land, it's not
   ready.
4. **Retire and re-date entries.** These are frozen at 22–29 Aug 2026 judgment. When a new batch
   is approved, add it here with the same WHY-IT-PASSED discipline, and if an old entry's pattern
   stops getting approved, mark it superseded rather than silently deleting it — that disagreement
   is itself information about how the bar moved.
