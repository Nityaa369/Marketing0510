# New ad type candidates — 2026-08-18 (revised)

**This replaces the first pass from earlier today**, which searched meme-template sites
almost exclusively and leaned on evergreen "iconic Bollywood dialogues" listicles that
weren't actually current. Two fixes here: (1) the list now covers the full range this
folder already represents, not just meme stills, and (2) every entry below is checked for
an actual recent trigger (a release date, a named recent event) rather than a blog post
that just happens to have "2026" in its URL — flagged explicitly where that check came up
short.

## What this folder already has — not re-suggested below

The seven reference images cover six distinct format categories already: movie-still +
speech-bubble meme (x2), a fake WhatsApp/DM chat mockup, a portrait-with-caption card, a
script/screenplay dialogue with a reaction shot, a stick-figure comic strip, and a
New-Yorker-style single-panel cartoon with caption. Candidates below are either a genuinely
different format category, or a specific fresh instance (a currently-peaking dialogue)
worth calling out separately from the generic version of its category already in the
folder.

## 1. Poll/quiz interactive Story card
**Mockup:** new ad types/candidates_2026-08-18_mockups/01_poll_quiz_story.html
**Track:** ui-layout
**Source:** https://www.gethookd.ai/learn/5-effective-instagram-poll-ads-examples-to-try-in-2026/ ;
https://www.balistro.com/instagram-ad-creative-ideas-convert-2026/
**Format category:** Native/interactive — not represented in this folder at all.
**Why it fits:** A yes/no or two-option poll sticker doubles as a qualifying question
("Still doing X the hard way?") — a natural fit for the "who you are" recognition beat
(Rule 2 step 1), and it captures first-party engagement data as a side benefit.
**Pairs with:** Any ICP — this is a Story/Reel wrapper, works as a top-of-funnel format
ahead of any specific kit.
**Caution:** Needs Meta's native poll/quiz sticker on Stories/Reels specifically, not a
static feed image — check the placement fits the media plan before building.

## 2. "Unposed screenshot" confession format
**Mockup:** new ad types/candidates_2026-08-18_mockups/02_unposed_screenshot.html
**Track:** native
**Source:** search aggregation on the current Instagram trend tied to Timothée Chalamet's
2026 Critics Choice speech (screenshot a small real thing you rely on + deadpan subtitle
caption naming it)
**Format category:** Native/screenshot — genuinely new, confirmed fresh (tied to a specific
named 2026 event, not a generic listicle).
**Why it fits:** The format's whole appeal is sincerity disguised as deadpan humor — maps
well onto "the domain knowledge you already have is the part nobody can buy" (the approved
pharmacovigilance angle in CLAUDE.md) without needing a joke structure at all.
**Pairs with:** Retired-banker or second-innings ICPs (`s`-series pages) — the deadpan,
low-key tone suits an audience that would find a loud meme off-putting.
**Caution:** Trend is very new — verify it's still circulating before committing build
time, fast-moving formats like this can fade in weeks.

## 3. Star-rating / review-screenshot mockup card
**Mockup:** new ad types/candidates_2026-08-18_mockups/03_review_screenshot.html
**Track:** ui-layout
**Source:** general native-ad-format search pass (testimonial/review mockup tools and use
cases)
**Format category:** Native/testimonial — not represented in this folder.
**Why it fits:** Reads as social proof rather than a claim, which sidesteps the
income/placement-guarantee trap entirely (standing compliance rule) since it's framed as
someone else's rating, not the brand's promise.
**Pairs with:** Any kit with a strong named-learner story already in its evidence bank,
e.g. `ContentWriting_29-31Aug/evidence_bank.html`.
**Caution:** Must be a real review, not a fabricated one — same bar as the "named proof"
blocker already flagged in `ContentWriting_29-31Aug/README_MEDIA_BUYER.md`.

**Track:** ui-layout

## 6. Two Buttons / "Daily Struggle" binary-choice graphic
**Mockup:** new ad types/candidates_2026-08-18_mockups/06_two_buttons.html
**Track:** meme
**Source:** https://bolao.belgo.com.br/two-choice-meme-understanding-psychology-z491.html
**Format category:** Meme/graphic — not a dialogue or movie-still, so distinct from what's
