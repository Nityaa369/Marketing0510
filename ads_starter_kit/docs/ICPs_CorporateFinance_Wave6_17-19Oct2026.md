# Corporate Finance, wave 6 — reader list and evidence (17 to 19 Oct 2026, Rs 10)

Drafted 1 Oct 2026. Twenty readers, every one tied to what it has actually cost, from an
ad-level Meta pull across every account the reporting token can read.

## Why this wave exists

The live Oct campaign is fatiguing: cost per Rs 10 buyer went Rs 192 on 24 Sep to Rs 365 on
28 Sep, against an August par of Rs 194, while CPM fell 177 to 133 and CTR fell 1.01% to 0.64%.
Creative wear, not auction cost.

## The design

The `launchlist` named-work list. Measured 1 Oct across every Sep/Oct bootcamp ad with 20+
buyers (175 ads, 11 campaigns, each indexed to its own campaign par, faces classified by eye
from the live creative): **0.79 on 29 ads and 5,568 buyers, across four campaigns and two
brands.** Controlled inside Senior Consulting 3-5 Oct, same weeks and product: **0.67x on 2,060
buyers** against **1.50x on 1,380** for that campaign's verb-headline faces.

Held constant across all twenty faces on purpose, so a result reads the reader and the prize
rather than the layout.

## The value proposition, and why it is new

Every Corporate Finance page currently live ends in the same place, measured by reading the fold
line of all nineteen: *"a written 6-month plan to your first client, a business owner who pays you
to raise money."*

This wave sells the other half of the same camp: **the documents.** From `SCRIPT_BRIEF_22Sep.md`
section 3, read off the 17 to 19 Oct scripts: *"Fixed fees for the documents: projections, pitch
deck, project report, information memorandum, loan proposal. These are paid even if the deal does
not close."*

A success fee needs a closed deal and a capital network. A document fee needs neither. It also
agrees with the script's own order of work, which says to build the opportunity funnel first.

Face line: **"The business pays you on delivery, not when the deal closes."**

## Deliberately not in this wave, with the evidence

- "No CA needed" as a standalone angle: 1.05x while taking Rs 2 lakh in August. It dilutes.
