## What the camp actually teaches (from the script, not from memory)

The script is the **6th NCA bootcamp, 26-28 April 2024** (Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 and the
preparatory doc). It is the latest script in the folder. Its argument is a three-step
roadmap, and every ICP below is written against it:

**Step one.** **Clear the NCA exams from India.** The NCA (National Committee on Accreditation, part
   of the Federation of Law Societies of Canada) assesses an Indian LLB and usually
   prescribes 5 subjects: Foundations of Canadian Law, Constitutional, Administrative,
   Criminal, Professional Responsibility. Online, proctored, open book, about 6 months at
   2 hours a day. Clearing all of them makes you equivalent to a Canadian law graduate.
**Step two.** **Do Canadian legal work remotely while preparing** - contracts valid in Canada,
   privacy policies for Canadian startups, company incorporation in Canada, litigation
   support for Canadian solo lawyers - found on Upwork, LinkedIn and law-society
   directories. This is the track record that turns into a job offer, a work permit or a
   business visa.
**Step three.** **Pass the Ontario Barrister and Solicitor exams** and practise in Canada, or stay in
   India and bill Canadian clients as a Canada-qualified lawyer.

Contrarian spine the script repeats: **do not buy a Canadian LLM (Rs 25-40 lakh) and do
not land on a PR with no Canadian track record.** Qualify first, work for Canadian clients
from India, move last, with a job in hand.

Day-by-day demos: NCA question types and IRAC answers (Day 1-2), the 12-month plan and
finding Canadian work on Upwork (Day 2), a Canadian employment agreement (Day 2),
reviewing a non-compete under Canadian law, the top 20 questions of a CEO expanding into
Canada, incorporating a Canadian company, a privacy policy for a Canadian startup
founder (Day 3). Certificate needs 2.5 hours each day.

Three lessons:

   relatives or interests. Naming the reader's own practice is the untested lever

   Lead with the career (Canadian clients, a Canadian law job), not the exam.

## The spine

Every ICP is **a trained Indian lawyer adding a second qualification, Canada's**, and
the better job it opens: Canadian clients billed from India, or a Canadian law job taken
up with the offer already in hand. Never "Canada is closing", never "you will be stuck
driving Uber" (the script uses that; it is Rule 1 threat framing), never the LLM as a
mistake the reader made. The reader's Indian training is the asset: common law, a
Charter that reads like Part III, a Criminal Code that reads like the IPC.

## Part A - backed by evidence (8)

1. **Lawyers taking IELTS for Canada** - Indian lawyers who have taken or are preparing for IELTS because a move abroad is already planned; the qualification that lets them practise when they land. SQE IELTS-interest ad sets 0.73x on n=260, English 0.68x; the one migration signal that has run cheap for a foreign-qualification camp.
2. **Lawyers whose spouse is moving to Canada** - women and men advocates whose husband or wife has a Canadian job offer or work permit; their own licence instead of a spouse's visa. Script case studies Hezal Shah (in-house, Mumbai to Vancouver) and Yashika Malhotra (litigator, to Montreal with a CAD job in hand). Pressure is real and dated: since 21 Jan 2025 a spouse only gets an open work permit if the partner works in a TEER 0/1 or listed TEER 2/3 job ([canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/special-instructions/spouses-dependent-children/eligibility.html)). Targeting uses the "long-distance relationship / family abroad" signals, not a Canada geo.
3. **Young law graduates, 22 to 34** - LLBs in the first years after university, choosing where their career is built; Canada-qualified before 35, when age points in the PR system are highest. LawSikho law-university audiences 0.82x on n=372.
4. **Corporate and contract lawyers** - lawyers who draft commercial contracts for Indian companies, drafting contracts valid in Canada for Canadian startups and small firms. California Bar commerce-law ad sets 0.72-0.76x on n=221; the script's Day 3 demo (non-compete under Canadian law, incorporating in Canada, privacy policy for a Canadian founder) is exactly this work.
5. **Law firm associates** - associates at Indian firms, adding a Canadian qualification to cross-border work, then Canadian or international firms. LawSikho law-firm audiences 0.85x on n=484.
6. **In-house counsel at companies with Canadian business** - legal teams at MNCs, IT services and SaaS companies selling into Canada, becoming the Canada-qualified lawyer on the team, or taking that role in Canada. LawSikho in-house 0.80x on n=389 (thin: 6 ads). Script: Hezal Shah, in-house at Conde Nast.
7. **Women lawyers returning after a break** - qualified advocates back from a career break, returning into Canadian client work done from home, and a qualification that travels. LawSikho legal campaigns 0.90x on n=8,586.
8. **Lawyers with close family in Canada** - advocates whose parents, siblings or in-laws are settled in Canada; a law career there, not a visit. NCA "relatives" ad sets 0.97x (n=1,173) and 1.11x (n=202): at par, never cheap. Kept because the script builds its sell around it and a named-occupation face has never been run on it.

13. **LPO and legal-process lawyers** - lawyers at LPOs and legal-support firms already doing document review and research for North American firms, moving from vendor to Canada-qualified lawyer. Never targeted.
