# Corporate Finance, wave 5 - reader list for round-one approval (17 to 19 Oct 2026, Rs 10)

Drafted 1 Oct 2026 from a fresh ad-level Meta pull (35 days, every account the token can read),
cost per Rs 10 buyer incl. GST, each ad indexed against its own campaign's average.

## Why this wave exists

The live Oct campaign's cost per buyer is climbing: Rs 192 on 24 Sep to Rs 365 on 28 Sep,
against an August par of Rs 194. CPM is flat to down (177 to 133) and CTR fell 1.01% to 0.64%,
so this is creative fatigue, not auction cost.

Wave 3's collage faces launched 30 Sep and `cfw3_loan_consultant` is now the cheapest static in
the account at **Rs 136 on 117 buyers** (band Rs 111 to 161). The same reader's older faces ran
Rs 209 and Rs 248 in the same days, so the design, not the reader, moved the number.

**This wave gives each proven reader a SECOND face on a different device**, so the campaign has
something to rotate to when the collage tires the way the August faces did.

## The design, and why this one

Measured 1 Oct 2026 across all Sep/Oct bootcamp ads with 20+ buyers (175 ads, 11 campaigns),
each indexed to its own campaign par. Faces classified by eye from the live creative, not by
name: 43 of 175 verified, so this is a reading of the top and bottom, not a census.

| design family | ads | buyers | index |
|---|---|---|---|
| collage (wave 3) | 1 | 117 | 0.55 |
| dark object photo | 10 | 808 | 0.76 |
| **named-work list (ICP band, destination head, 01/02/03)** | **29** | **5,568** | **0.79** |
| video | 5 | 637 | 0.90 |
| translate | 4 | 217 | 0.92 |
| unclassified | 92 | 17,608 | 0.99 |
| toon / meme / quiz | 13 | 2,142 | 1.08 |
| verb headline + middle widget | 18 | 1,380 | 1.50 |

The controlled read is inside **one campaign** (Senior Consulting 3-5 Oct, same weeks, same
product, par Rs 140):

| design | ads | buyers | cost | index |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| named-work list | 11 | 2,060 | Rs 93 | **0.67x** |
| dark object photo | 10 | 808 | Rs 106 | 0.76x |
| verb headline + middle widget | 18 | 1,380 | Rs 210 | 1.50x |

Same campaign, same weeks, 2.2x apart. The named-work list is the only static design with a
repeatable win across more than one campaign: it also carries Global Patent's cheapest faces
(veterinary 0.59, microbiologists 0.75), US Corporate Law's cheapest (cs_inhouse_mnc 0.75) and
Academic Writing's (medical_researchers 0.78). Four campaigns, two brands, 5,568 buyers.

**It is Corporate Finance's own structure.** `adkit` built the layout from
`CorporateFinance_MediaBuyer_15-17Aug` faces 4 and 1 (Rs 164 and Rs 170 per buyer) - the batch
Ramanuj approved 7 of 7 and the one CLAUDE.md Rule 2 quotes. Other camps borrowed it and ran it
up to 5,568 buyers. This wave brings it home.

What separates the winners from the losers, on the faces themselves:
- the headline is a **destination noun phrase**, not a verb ("Launch your own hospital
  consulting business." beats "Get paid to cut their cloud bill")
- there is **no middle widget**. Every loser carries an extra table, bar chart or two-column
  panel between the headline and the list. Every winner goes straight to the named work.
- the work is **named in a numbered list**, each row a label plus one ordinary-words line

## The readers

Only readers that beat their campaign par in BOTH August and October. Nothing unproven in this
wave: it is a device test, so the reader must be a constant.

### 1. Loan consultants and DSAs
Aug 0.85x on 396 buyers. Oct: Rs 209 (0.84x) on the August face, Rs 136 (0.55x) on the collage.
The strongest reader in the product's history.

### 2. Cost accountants (CMA)
Aug 0.88x on 281. Oct 0.85x on 239, but decaying inside the week: Rs 139 per buyer on 24 Sep to
Rs 319 on 30 Sep. The highest-volume face in the campaign and the one most in need of relief.

### 3. Equity research analysts
Aug 0.94x on 169. Oct 0.99x on 140, decaying from Rs 73 to Rs 788 across the same week.

### 4. Aspiring investment bankers
Aug 0.91x on 50 (`cf_aspiring_ib`). Oct Rs 197 (0.79x) on 18 buyers (`cf_aspiring_banker`).
Below par both times; the thinnest evidence here, and the only cell under 20 buyers in October.

## Not in this wave, with the evidence
- Credit analysts (1.14x Oct on 90) and FP&A (1.12x on 39): above par both times. Wave 3 is
  already testing whether a plain hook fixes them; adding a second device would confound that.
- Commerce graduates 1.93x on 41, final-year students 3.4x, career break 1.84x on 5,
  retired bankers 1.50x on 23: lose in both months.
- Company secretaries 0.98x Aug then 1.13x Oct: not proven in both.
