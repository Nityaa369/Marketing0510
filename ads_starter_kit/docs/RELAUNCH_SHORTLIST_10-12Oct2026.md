# Relaunch shortlist - US Accounting and Independent Director, 10-12 Oct 2026

Written 17 Sep 2026. Both camps are 23 days out; their 12-14 Sep predecessors first spent 22 days
before the camp, so both are already late.

**Sources.** Lifetime ad-level pull of the two 12-14 Sep campaigns through the System User token
(`US Accounting_AND_Bookkeeping_Sep_12th`, `Retired_Independent_Director_BC_12-Sep-2026_LEAD`,
both on Skill Arbitrage `954464642377027`), every 2026 Independent Director campaign across all 19
accounts, and GrowthX registrations per funnel for 20 Aug-19 Sep.

**Meta's lead counts are real.** GrowthX registrations match Meta within about 3% on every
funnel checked (e.g. `usacc-not-evening` 3,532 vs 3,464; `meta-retired-id-sep-banker` 3,137 vs
2,498 + 461 across the two ads sharing it). Registrations are form fills only; attendance is not
in this data.

**How to read the tables.** Cost is per lead, incl. GST. Margin is +/-196/sqrt(n). "vs median"
is against the campaign's median across ads with 20+ leads (US Accounting Rs 48, ID Rs 86).
"Drift" compares the cost of the ad's second half of spend with its first half; a large positive
number means it was tiring.

**Rules used.** KEEP: 100+ leads, at or below the campaign median, and not tiring badly. BENCH:
at par or thin data, use if a kept ad fails. DROP: above median with enough data to say so.

## Decide these three before anything is built

1. **Free or paid?** Both September camps ran as free lead funnels. The dates sheet leaves the
   PAID/FREE column blank for both 10-12 Oct camps. Every shortlisted ad says "free" on its face,
   so this decides whether they can be reused at all.
2. **The Independent Director camp is now wider.** The sheet calls it "ALL ID together:
   experienced + post-retirement". Every September winner speaks to retired people. The
   experienced half has no September winner (the `i1_cxo`, `i2_cfo`, `i3_inhouse`, `i5_corplaw`
   ads were starved at under Rs 1,100 each), but July has one (see ID section).
3. **Three proven faces break standing rules.** They ran, and cheaply, but the rules say
   otherwise, so it is Ramanuj's call whether they go out again as they are:
   - `banker` puts **"Rs 25,000-1,00,000 sitting fees per meeting"** on the face (salary figures
     are LP-only) and uses an em dash.
   - `RID_i7_forces` says **"Land your first board appointment in the next 6 months"**, which
     reads as an outcome promise.
   - `rid_quote_` (August) uses two em dashes on the face.

## What every relaunched ad needs

- **New dates on the face.** Every kept static prints "12 to 14 September" (or "8-10 Aug",
  "18-20 July") in the image, not just the primary text. The videos say "12-14 Sep" in their copy
  and need their end cards checked.
- **New funnels.** Every ad points at a September GrowthX funnel (`usacc-not-*`,
  `meta-retired-id-sep-*`). Each October ad needs its own October funnel slug (Rule 4: one ad,
  one page).
- **Source files.**
  - US Accounting: finished PNGs and landing pages are in
    `APPROVED/US_Accounting_MissedICP_Notice_MediaBuyer_12-14Sep/` and
    `APPROVED/US_Accounting_Similarities_MediaBuyer_12-14Sep (1)/`, but **no render source**. The
    faces must be rebuilt on adkit's `notice` layout with the same copy, or the date line edited
    in the image.
  - Independent Director: **none of the September winners, videos included, are in this repo.**
    `banker` and `educator` exist only as August PNGs in `RID_BC_8Aug_Ads (1)/`. Whoever built
    the September set needs to supply sources or re-render.

## Launch structure (SOP/02)

- A new campaign per camp, never the dormant September one. Fresh ad sets, **at most 5 ads per
  ad set**.
- US Accounting: 7 ads in two ad sets, with the two "evenings" faces split between them.
- Independent Director: 6 kept + 2 brought back = 8 ads in two ad sets, with `rid_quote_` and
  `business-boardseat` in their own set.
- Do not raise the daily cap on launch day. Judge the first 7 full days on 20+ leads per ad.
- **Audience reach is already high.** September US Accounting reached 21.7 lakh people 3.8 times
  each, and Independent Director 11.1 lakh people 2.7 times each, three weeks ago. Expect October
  to open dearer than September closed, and add fresh faces early rather than waiting for these
  to tire.
- Decide whether September registrants (at least 14,900 US Accounting and 8,700 Independent
  Director on GrowthX, counting only the funnels checked here) should be excluded from these prospecting campaigns. The reporting token
  is read-only, so the exclusion audience has to be built in Ads Manager.
