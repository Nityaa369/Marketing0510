### ICP decides the rest

Every face under Rs 125 names a **trained operational discipline**. Every face over
Rs 240 sells a concept (`factory_labour` Rs 352, `ca_startup` Rs 324, `banker_farewell`
Rs 300, `no_capital` Rs 261, `govt_panels` Rs 258, `subject_pays` Rs 246, `ex_founder`
Rs 243, `canteen` Rs 243).

Across all four generations: **sell consulting, not AI.** The AI-led face is the dearest
in the product every time it runs (`India_Pros|AI_Growth` Rs 834/lead, March). The
side-of-the-9-to-5 angle is the only one that priced well in all four generations
(`9to5_Side_Start` Rs 151/lead, the cheapest March face).

## 4. What this wave must not do

- **No middle widget** — no chip row, bar chart or two-column panel between the headline
  and the list. Every 1.53x face in this campaign has one; no 0.68x face does.
- **No verb headline.** "Launch your own <X> consulting business." is the measured shape.
  "Take your ERP work direct to clients" is the 1.53x shape.
- **No AI framing.** Dearest angle in all four generations.
- **No concept ICPs.** A trained discipline, named as a job title.
