## The spine

Every ICP is framed as **a practice line the reader can add**, never as an obligation that
now lands on their desk. That is the shape all three lawyer winners share, and the opposite
of what got four DPDP ads rejected on 12 Aug 2026 (CLAUDE.md Rule 1).

## Part B — added to reach 20 (15)

### Backed: cheaper than their LawSikho campaigns (6)

6. **Company secretaries** — CS professionals whose board and governance work already touches how a company handles data. The strongest named segment in LawSikho's legal history: `cs` ad sets 0.69x on n=529 across 3 CA/CS bootcamps, 0.88x on n=2,280 broadly. At par on DPDP (n=209).
7. **Labour and employment lawyers** — lawyers advising employers, who hold every employee's personal records. LawSikho `Employment` and `HR_Labour_Law` ad sets 0.94x on n=1,242, but across only 2 campaigns. (An `LL` ad-set group at 0.86x is NOT labour law: it spans CA/CS, SQE and US Corporate Law campaigns and is almost certainly Meta's lookalike-audience shorthand.)
8. **Banking, fintech and SEBI lawyers** — lawyers advising lenders, payment businesses and listed companies, all built on customer financial data. LawSikho `banking` ad sets 0.88x (n=1,682, all on IBBI insolvency bootcamps) and SEBI ad sets, 0.95x on n=5,340 combined. The audience in those camps may be bankers as much as lawyers, so the lawyer share is uncertain.
9. **Women lawyers restarting after a break** — qualified advocates returning after time away, choosing a newer practice area to return into. LawSikho legal campaigns 0.90x on n=8,586, measured with the non-legal women-remote funnels excluded.
10. **In-house counsel** — lawyers inside companies who become the natural owner of the data-protection question. LawSikho 0.80x on n=389, but only 6 ads across 2 campaigns: promising and thin.
11. **Young law graduates, 22 to 34** — law graduates in the first years after university, choosing the area they will be known for. Replaces law students. LawSikho law-university audiences 0.82x (n=372, SQE and IP Law bootcamps) and 0.93x (n=971, Contract Drafting), while law STUDENTS came in dearer at 1.10x on n=1,973.

### Backed: at par with their LawSikho campaigns (3)

12. **Corporate and M&A lawyers** — deal lawyers for whom data handling is now part of every diligence. LawSikho M&A and corporate-lawyer ad sets 0.99x on n=712.
13. **Technology and IP lawyers** — lawyers advising the platforms and software businesses that hold the most personal data. LawSikho 0.99x on n=10,689; the pool includes science-graduate patent audiences, so the lawyer share is less certain.
14. **High court advocates** — advocates practising before high courts, where writ practice already turns on individual rights. LawSikho `high_court` ad sets 0.97x on n=1,226, all on AOR bootcamps, so one product. Written on the forum, not the cause list, so it does not repeat the litigators refresh.

### Deliberate tests: no usable evidence (6)

Never targeted under LawSikho or DPDP, too few ads to judge, or the only numbers found
belong to a different audience. Each is a claim that could be wrong, and the kit should be
read that way.

15. **Tax lawyers** — lawyers advising businesses whose tax work already runs through their financial and customer records. A `tax_law` ad-set group sits at 0.98x on n=1,596, but it ran almost entirely on a Corporate Finance / Investment bootcamp and a CA/CS bootcamp: a tax-interest audience on finance products, not tax lawyers. So it does not back this reader.
16. **Healthcare and medical lawyers** — lawyers advising hospitals, labs and health businesses, where patient data is the most sensitive category. One LawSikho campaign only (4 ads, 0.93x), so no verdict. Health data was the strongest non-lawyer cluster on DPDP.
17. **Consumer protection lawyers** — lawyers already representing consumers, where personal-data rights are a newer claim type. Never targeted.
18. **Solo advocates in tier-2 and tier-3 cities** — independent practitioners outside the metros adding a practice line local businesses need. Never targeted as "solo"; tier-2 geo targeting ran only on non-legal products.
19. **Cyber law practitioners** — lawyers already handling cybercrime and IT Act matters, the closest existing practice to data breaches. Never targeted.
20. **Paralegals and legal assistants** — legal support staff who can step up into privacy work in their own right. Never targeted as an audience ("US Paralegal" in LawSikho accounts is a product, not this reader).
