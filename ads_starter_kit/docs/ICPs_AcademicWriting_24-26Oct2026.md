SkillArbitrage, Rs 10. Written 22 Sep 2026.

## Two rules from Ruchika, 22 Sep 2026

1. **Simple.** The ICP and the hook must be understood by someone scrolling half asleep. The
   reader is named the way they name themselves ("PhD scholar", "school teacher"), with no
   second clause describing their frustration.
2. **Always a step up.** Every reader must see this as more money, more recognition or a
   bigger career than they have now. Any reader who could see it as a step down is out.

  pre-submission review, English editing for non-English-speaking professors, grant

  says outright that a PhD is not needed and that teachers and non-science graduates can
  do it. Its three goals: a full-time remote job, part-time freelance work on the side, or
  an office job at a writing agency or corporate R&D team.

## Dropped, and why

- **Bankers and finance staff** (the script's Anujvrat case): a senior banker would read
  research support as a step down.
- **Freelance writers worried about AI:** the angle is fear (Rule 1). The step-up version
  survives as ICP 11, content writers.
- **Office workers who loved research as students:** not a label anyone recognises in two
  seconds.
- **Lab researchers:** the script's own example (a lab researcher at a writing agency on
  Rs 60k) reads as a sideways move, not up.
- **Professors, law academics, engineering faculty, postgrad board, AI detectors, pay
  chart:** measured failures (0 buyers, or 1.5x to 4.4x par). Professors are also the
  reader most likely to see the work as beneath them.
- **Science media:** the 2025 face ran at 0.60x, but its ad name does not say who it
  targeted, so there is no ICP to rebuild.
