# Data Protection bootcamp, 17 to 19 October 2026: what the scripts actually teach

Read 22 Sep 2026 from the October script index (`docs/October_2026_bootcamp_scripts.md`, row
"Data Privacy, 17-19 Oct"): the prep doc (1KjZBYNf...), Day 1 (1WLfoVtK...) and Day 2
(1AbO37oh...) in full; Day 3 from the prep doc's agenda. This is the source of record for every
claim an ad or page makes about the camp. Anything not listed here is not taught.

**Title in the scripts:** "International Career in Data Protection and Privacy Management".
The "13th data protection bootcamp". Hosts: Roshan Mariyam (lawyer and CS), Sampark Kumar Setia,
Kshamita Patil, "executive office of LawSikho & Skill Arbitrage". Day 2 is branded SkillArbitrage
throughout (placement office, Upwork agency, "SkillArbitrage students").

**Format (script, Day 1):** 9 hours over 3 days; Sat and Sun 6 to 9 PM, Mon 7 to 10 PM. "This is a
live event; there will be no recordings, and no presentation will be shared." Community groups and
a resource page with notes. Certificate instructions shared as a PDF. Giveaways require attending
at least 2.5 hours on that day.

## Day 1
- Why the work exists; the 7 kinds of privacy work (software and UI, privacy management and
  administration, compliance, training, routine drafting and negotiation such as data-processing
  addendums, legal work); job roles and the hierarchy (privacy analyst to DPO).
- What an organisation needs implemented now under the DPDP Act and Rules.
- **Skill 1, live:** a data privacy impact assessment (DPIA) under the DPDP Act or GDPR, on a
  simulated Indian edtech startup selling courses into Europe: the 10 questions (what data, who has
  access, third parties, where stored, server location, control, access conditions, restrictions,
  adequacy, further action), starting with the lifecycle and flow of user data. Report drafting.
- How to help existing clients or your own employer; how to build a track record in a job.
- **Skill 2:** outreach, domestic and international: finding projects on Upwork and Fiverr,
  reaching startups (Y Combinator), how to pitch.
- The CIPP exam: overview, sample questions, applicability of GDPR and the Indian law.

## Day 2
- The 6-month plan: 20 skills, 2 hours a day, track record and outreach from month one.
- Networking with foreign privacy consultants (LinkedIn demo) and Indian startups (Shark Tank demo),
  with message scripts.
- How to decide on international data transfer (US, UK, Canada, EU, Singapore, Japan, India).
- Networking with Big Four partners; 10 differences between the DPDP Act and GDPR.
- How to draft, review and find gaps in a privacy policy under the DPDP Act and GDPR (prep doc).
- 5 CIPP concepts. Case studies. The scholarship offer (after 8 PM).

## Day 3 (prep doc agenda)
- Earning and building a track record from scratch; examples of early work.
- Upwork profile and website as a privacy consultant; proposals written with AI.
- International data transfer decisions.
- Drafting or reviewing a privacy policy under California and Canadian law, and pitching for it.
- Negotiating your own contract as a privacy consultant or DPO.
- Handling data subject requests (access, correction, deletion).
- How to respond to a data breach.

## Giveaways (23; attend at least 2.5 hours that day)
Day 1: controller or processor; cross-border transfer; consequences of GDPR violations; does GDPR
apply to you; how to create a personal data asset inventory list; drafting a cookie policy;
rights of customers, employees and other data subjects.
Day 2: workplace privacy in the US; GDPR-compliant privacy policy template; Canadian privacy policy
template; right to erasure request form; data access request form; privacy notice template.
Day 3: when and how to do a DPIA; transfer impact assessment checklist; how to succeed as a DPO;
CCPA compliance checklist; data subject request form; personal data flow map; US-law privacy
policy template; GDPR impact assessment report template; privacy policy template; DPO agreement
template.

## NOT taught (do not claim)
Mapping "a real company's" data (the live exercise is a simulated startup); drafting a
data-processing agreement (only named as a kind of work; the template given is a DPO agreement);
a "72-hour breach note written with you" (breach response is taught; no note is written);
a board note (absent); due diligence; complaints before the Data Protection Board; writ practice.

## Do not import from the scripts
The scripts' market and income claims ("5 lakh DPO jobs", "earn at least 2x", "USD 2,000 a
month", fee bands, salary figures, "hardly anyone trained", "everyone starts as a beginner",
placement numbers). House rules stand: no income promise, no unsourced numbers, no "no one ahead"
claims ([[feedback_newness_claims_must_be_true]]).
