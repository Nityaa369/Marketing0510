## The spine

Every reader is offered **a tech law line or move built on what they already do**, never
a rule that now applies to them. No "no seniors / no head start" (feedback_newness_claims):
the true newness facts are the DPDP Rules (notified 13 Nov 2025, deadlines Nov 2026 and
May 2027, per the script) and that law college does not teach this work (the script's
own claim, and the Mentorship-gap winner's line).

## Part A — refresh the August faces that held (5)

1. **Fresh law graduates** — LLB graduates in their first year out, with no first job yet. The topchoice face (Rs 184, n=56, 0.66x) spoke to exactly this reader, and law-university audiences 22-34 ran 0.82x under LawSikho. The strongest evidence in the list.
2. **Lawyers in their first three years** — junior lawyers learning on their own in a chamber or office that does no tech work. The Mentorship-gap face (Rs 232, n=47, 0.83x) and the script's "no training from seniors" reality check.
3. **Lawyers outside the metros** — advocates in tier-2 and tier-3 towns who want work that is not tied to the local court. The hottest face's "no metro city" line (Rs 267, n=541, at par, the volume engine).
4. **Lawyers aiming at foreign clients** — Indian lawyers who want to work for US and UK startups and firms from India. The International-career face (Rs 302, n=125, at par) and the camp's own title.
5. **Litigators, 2 to 10 years** — courtroom lawyers adding tech disputes and tech contracts. lit_02 (Rs 271, n=36, at par). Already the October search kit's one reader: one refresh here, on a different layout, not three.

## Part B — backed by LawSikho segment evidence (5)
