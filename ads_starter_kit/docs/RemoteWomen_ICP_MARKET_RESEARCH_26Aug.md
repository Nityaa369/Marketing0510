# Remote Women bootcamp - where the next 30 ICPs come from

Research run 26 Aug 2026. Purpose: find more ICPs of the same shape as the one that
already worked - "113,000+ credentialed RDNs" led to the dietitian / home-science /
nutritionist ads in `WomensRemoteWork_USRoles_MediaBuyer_5-7Sep`.

This file is **research input for `value_props.md`**, not a kit. Nothing here is ad copy.
Every ICP still has to survive the value-props round before a face is built.

---

## 1. What actually made the RDN stat work

The dietitian angle did not work because 113,000 is a big number. It worked because four
things were true at once. That is the test every candidate below is scored against.

| # | Test | Why it matters |
|---|------|----------------|
| **D** | **Demand: a large, growing US population of practitioners** | The reader has to believe the client exists. A named, countable profession does that. |
| **S** | **Solo: they run their own tiny business** | A salaried employee at a hospital has an admin team. A solo practitioner IS the admin team. That is the hiring pressure. |
| **G** | **Gap: they must market and administrate, and cannot** | The practitioner trained in the craft, not in Instagram, intake forms or invoicing. |
| **N** | **Neighbour: a trained Indian professional who speaks the vocabulary** | This is the differentiator. The pitch is not "I will do your admin cheaply", it is "I already know what a macro split / a lien waiver / a discharge note is." |

Miss any one and the ad falls over. Miss **N** and you are selling generic offshore VA
work into the most price-competitive market on earth, which is a bad offer for a woman in
India and reads as one.

**The N test is the whole product.** Everything below is organised around it.

## 2. Demand side - US markets that pass the test

Sourced, most-defensible numbers first. Each row: the stat, why they are solo, and the
vocabulary an Indian neighbour already owns.

### Tier 1 - strongest fits (big, growing, overwhelmingly solo, obvious Indian neighbour)

**1. Mental health therapists in private practice**
~600,000 therapists in the US; over 130,000 LPCs actively practising; ~382,000 counsellors
in NPPES data; workforce growth projected at 20% through 2033, with a projected shortage of
128,200 mental health counsellors by 2037.
*Solo because:* private practice is the default exit from agency work; a solo therapist does
her own intake calls, insurance verification, no-show chasing and waitlist.
*Indian neighbour:* MA/MSc Psychology graduates, MSW, counselling-diploma holders, clinical
psychology students who did not get an M.Phil seat. India produces psychology graduates far
in excess of the licensed clinical seats available.
*Route:* mental-health VA / intake coordinator / insurance-verification VA / practice SMM.
*Note:* HIPAA vocabulary is a real skill and a real gate - it is teachable in a bootcamp and
it is exactly the kind of thing that makes a pitch credible.

**2. BCBAs and ABA therapy practices**
83,586 BCBAs held active credentials in the US in 2025, up from 44,025 in 2020. In 2025
employers posted 132,307 positions requiring BCBA certification against those 83,586
credential holders. BLS projects ~17% growth for behavioural health roles through 2034.
*Solo because:* enormous numbers of small independent ABA practices and solo BCBAs billing
insurance, drowning in session notes, authorisations and parent communication.
*Indian neighbour:* special educators, B.Ed special education, occupational therapy
graduates, psychology graduates, RCI-registered rehabilitation professionals.
*Route:* ABA practice VA, authorisation and session-note admin, parent-comms coordinator.
*This is the single most under-served market on the list.* The supply gap is documented in
their own trade press.

**3. Med spas and aesthetics practices**
~10,488 med spas in the US in 2023, up from 8,899 the year before, forecast ~11,553 by 2025,
with roughly 1,000 new med spas opening a year. Market $21.31bn in 2025 growing at ~14% CAGR;
155.8 million visits annually.
*Solo because:* a med spa is typically one nurse-injector or one owner-operator with a
front desk. Their entire acquisition engine is Instagram and before/after content.
*Indian neighbour:* BSc nursing and GNM nurses, cosmetology and aesthetics diploma holders,
dermatology clinic staff, BAMS/BHMS graduates who worked in skin clinics.
*Route:* **social media VA is the primary route here, not admin.** Also consult-booking and
follow-up sequences.
*Why it is strong:* the number of new openings per year is the ad. A business that opened
last month has no marketing person by definition.

**4. Solo and small law firms**
Roughly 40% of US law firms are one-lawyer practices; more than three-quarters have fewer
than six lawyers. Most common solo areas: bankruptcy, civil litigation, family law, estate
planning, immigration.
*Solo because:* by definition.
*Indian neighbour:* **this is the account's home turf.** LLB graduates, company secretaries,
paralegals, law students. LawSikho already has a US paralegal kit
(`USParalegal_LawSikho_MediaBuyer_29-31Aug`) - the VA framing is a second door into the same
market for people who will not clear a paralegal credential.
*Route:* legal VA, intake coordinator, e-filing and calendaring, immigration case-status VA.
Legal VA is named repeatedly as one of the highest-paying VA niches.

**5. Independent insurance agencies**
430,059 insurance brokers and agencies in the US in 2025; 35,000-40,000 independent P&C
agencies specifically; agency count growing ~1.6%/yr 2020-2025; independent agents placed
62% of all US P&C insurance and 87.7% of commercial lines.
*Solo because:* a huge share of these are one-producer shops.
*Indian neighbour:* LIC and general-insurance agents, insurance-company back-office staff,
BFSI graduates, actuarial-science dropouts, B.Com graduates who worked in claims.
*Route:* insurance VA - certificates of insurance, renewals chasing, quote intake, CRM
hygiene. Named as a high-paying niche.

**6. Real estate agents**
~1.49 million NAR Realtors as of late 2025, plus 500,000+ licensed non-member agents,
roughly 2 million licensees total. NAR reported 1,453,690 members as of 31 May 2025.
*Solo because:* an agent is an independent contractor running a one-person business.
*Indian neighbour:* Indian real-estate and property-consultancy staff, architecture and
interior-design graduates, civil engineering graduates, MBA marketing.
*Route:* real estate VA, **transaction coordinator** (a real, named, well-paid role),
listing coordinator, ISA / appointment setter, listing-social VA.
*Caveat:* this market is shrinking and squeezed - NAR projects membership could settle
around 1.2 million by end-2026. That cuts both ways: fewer clients, but far more pressure on
survivors to cut cost, which is the VA pitch. Flag it, do not hide it.

### Tier 2 - strong, with one soft edge

**7. Chiropractors**
65,850 chiropractors in the US as of 2025; **49% are solo practitioners** per the 2025 NBCE
Practice Analysis; over 28,652 employed as sole practitioners. Women are now 30% of the
workforce and nearing 50% of recent graduates.
*Indian neighbour:* physiotherapists (BPT/MPT), osteopathy and rehab diploma holders.
India's BPT output is large and starting salaries are Rs 20,000-40,000/month.
*Route:* chiropractic practice VA, recall and reactivation campaigns, practice SMM.
*Soft edge:* only ~0.9% annual growth. Big and solo, but not booming.

**8. Nurse practitioner-owned clinics and direct primary care**
461,000 licensed NPs in the US; NP jobs projected to grow **40% between 2024 and 2034**.
28 states plus DC now grant full practice authority. Over 2,300 direct primary care offices
in the US, on a membership model. Solo PMHNP telehealth practices are called out as the
fastest-growing NP-owned business model.
*Indian neighbour:* BSc nursing and GNM nurses - India has ~1,38,321 BSc Nursing seats
across ~5,310 institutions for 2024-25, and GNM seats around 1,30,182.
*Route:* medical VA, virtual scribe, prior-authorisation VA, patient intake, membership
billing.
*Why it is big:* 40% growth is the strongest single growth number on this list, and the
Indian nursing pipeline is the largest single supply pool on the India side.

**9. Virtual medical scribing and medical admin generally**
Not a profession, a route - but the demand data is the strongest here. Over 68% of US
healthcare providers using or considering virtual scribing (Deloitte, 2025). US medical
billing outsourcing projected to grow from $6.95bn in 2025 to $17.69bn by 2033 (12.56% CAGR).
Offshore hiring saves up to 70% vs a comparable US hire. Several scribe firms explicitly
recruit internationally.
*Indian neighbour:* the entire allied-health pipeline - nursing, pharmacy (India produces
roughly 4 lakh pharmacy graduates a year, with employability widely reported as poor),
BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BPT, life sciences, medical coding trainees.
*Route:* virtual medical scribe, medical billing/coding VA, prior-auth VA, patient
coordinator.
*Soft edge:* this is the most crowded and most agency-dominated route, and the most exposed
to ambient AI scribing. Sell the coordinator/authorisation end, not the transcription end.

**10. Veterinary practices**
56,756 veterinary services businesses in the US in 2025, up 2.0% from 2024. **93.9% of
surveyed practices are independently owned.**
*Indian neighbour:* BVSc graduates, animal husbandry and dairy science graduates, zoology
and life-science graduates, veterinary assistants.
*Route:* vet practice VA, appointment and recall management, client communication, practice
SMM. A neighbour who knows what a spay recovery or a titre test is writes a different email.
*Soft edge:* smaller absolute market, and consolidation is nibbling at the sole-proprietor end.

**11. Independent dental practices**
178,461 active US dental practices; 202,485 dentists; only 16% DSO-affiliated. About a third
of dentists remain solo, though that share is shrinking (15% among dentists in their first
decade vs 48% among 25+ year veterans).
*Indian neighbour:* **BDS graduates.** This is one of India's clearest surplus-credential
pools - large annual output, weak domestic private-practice economics.
*Route:* dental practice VA, insurance verification, recall and hygiene reactivation,
treatment-plan follow-up, practice SMM.
*Soft edge:* the solo share is declining. Still 178k practices and 84% non-DSO.

**12. Lactation consultants, doulas and the perinatal market**
IBCLC numbers rose from 13,300 in 2012 to 18,532 in 2022, roughly 40% growth, and are
growing 3-5%/yr against faster-growing demand. Insurance-coverage mandates for lactation
services are now in effect in 38 states, creating reimbursement for independent
practitioners. Lactation consulting services market $3.2bn in 2025 to $5.8bn by 2033.
*Indian neighbour:* nurses, midwives (ANM), dietitians and nutritionists, child-development
and home-science graduates, MSc Food & Nutrition.
*Route:* perinatal practice VA, insurance-billing VA, class and workshop coordinator,
practice SMM.
*Why it is interesting:* the reimbursement mandate is a genuine, dated, verifiable reason
these practices suddenly need billing help. That is a mechanism, not a vibe.
*Soft edge:* small absolute numbers. Good for one ad, not a wave.

**13. Health and wellness coaches**
More than 13,000 NBC-HWC credential holders; over 6,000 board-certified health coaches
actively practising as of early 2025. Market $20.1bn in 2025 to $37.96bn by 2034. BLS
projects 23% growth in health education and wellness roles through 2030.
*Indian neighbour:* the same nutrition/dietetics/home-science pool already used, plus
fitness certifications and yoga instructors.
*Route:* coaching-business VA, client onboarding, content repurposing, funnel and email VA.
*Soft edge:* overlaps heavily with the RDN ICP already built. Differentiate or skip.

**14. Independent RIAs and breakaway financial advisors**
Independent advisors now manage 27% of total US industry assets, up from 21% a decade ago.
Cerulli projects the number of independent RIAs to rise 12% by 2028. Close to 9% of
advisors, representing $3.1 trillion in assets, changed firms in 2025, mostly toward
independence.
*Indian neighbour:* **the strongest commerce-side neighbour on this list.** B.Com and
M.Com graduates, CA inter/dropouts, CFA level-1 holders, MBA finance, bank relationship
managers, mutual-fund distributors.
*Route:* financial-services VA - named as one of the highest-paying niches - CRM and
pipeline management, client-review prep, compliance-calendar admin, newsletter and LinkedIn.
*Why it is strong:* an advisor who breaks away from a wirehouse loses her entire support
staff on day one. That is the clearest "just became solo, needs help now" moment on the list.

**15. Cash-based physical therapy and outpatient rehab**
US PT clinics industry ~$53bn; occupational and physical therapy services projected $65.36bn
(2025) to $128.17bn (2032), 10.1% CAGR. Highly fragmented - the 50 largest competitors take
only 29% of revenue; typical provider revenue ~$871,000. Cash-based follow-up sessions run
$165-250.
*Indian neighbour:* **BPT and MPT graduates** - direct vocabulary match, large Indian output,
low domestic pay.
*Route:* rehab practice VA, plan-of-care admin, cash-pay package sales support, practice SMM.
*Soft edge:* no clean count of cash-based clinics specifically. Use the fragmentation stat.

**16. Speech-language pathologists in private practice / teletherapy**
ASHA has 247,000 members, certificate holders and affiliates. ASHA surveys show 14% of SLPs
deliver services from home.
*Indian neighbour:* Indian speech therapy and audiology graduates (BASLP), special
educators, child psychology graduates.
*Route:* teletherapy practice VA, scheduling across time zones, IEP-adjacent paperwork,
school-contract admin.
*Soft edge:* the private-practice split inside that 247,000 is not published, so the
headline number over-claims. Use the 14%-from-home figure instead, it is honest and it
tells the story better anyway.

### Tier 3 - real markets, weaker N test (list them, do not lead with them)

**17. Short-term rental hosts and Airbnb co-hosts.** Airbnb has 8M+ active listings managed
by 5M+ people; the Co-Host Network passed 15,000 co-hosts managing 100,000+ listings.
Co-hosting is a named, platform-blessed role. *Neighbour:* hotel management graduates (a
large, underemployed Indian pool), travel-agency staff, hospitality diploma holders. This one
actually has a decent N - IHM graduates genuinely know guest ops.

**18. Home services - HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, cleaning, lawn care.** US home
services market $650-750bn annually as of early 2025, growing 7-9%; over 5 million employed;
46% of contractors report difficulty finding skilled workers. *Neighbour:* weak - there is
no trained Indian neighbour who speaks HVAC. Sell this one on dispatch/CSR/booking skill, not
domain knowledge. *Route:* appointment-setting VA, dispatch coordinator, review-generation VA.

**19. Creator economy - course creators, coaches, podcasters, newsletter operators.**
45 million+ US professional creators; 46.7% of creators work full-time; online learning
market $203.81bn in 2025, US leading at $99.84bn. *Neighbour:* journalism and mass-comm
graduates, English literature graduates, teachers, video editors. *Route:* podcast VA, launch
manager, community manager, content-repurposing VA, funnel/tech VA. *Caveat:* 50% of creators
earn under $15,000 a year - most cannot afford anyone. Target the top decile only, and say so.

**20. Etsy / Amazon / Shopify sellers.** Etsy: 86.5M active buyers and 5.6M sellers at end
2025 (down from 8.1M after an inactive-account purge - use the 5.6M). 30% of US small
businesses sell on Amazon in 2026. *Neighbour:* fashion design, textile design and merch
graduates for Etsy; B.Com and supply-chain for Amazon ops. *Route:* e-commerce VA, listing
optimisation, Amazon PPC (an Amazon PPC specialist can command far more than a general VA).

**21. Estate planning specifically** - a popular solo practice area, but the count of solo
estate attorneys has fallen ~5% on consolidation. Keep inside the solo-law ICP, do not give
it its own ad.

## 3. The VA route taxonomy - what kinds of VA actually exist

The bootcamp currently names two routes: VA and social media VA. There are far more, and the
specialised ones pay materially better. Generalist VA work sits at $15-25/hr and is
described as highly competitive; if rates sit consistently above $25-30/hr the market is
paying for a specialisation. An illustrative spread: a general admin at $10/hr against an
Amazon PPC specialist at $50/hr.

### By function

| Route | What it actually is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **General admin VA** | Inbox, calendar, travel, data entry | The floor. Named as competitive and low-paid. Do not build a wave on it. |
| **Executive assistant (remote)** | Chief-of-staff-lite for one founder | Named among the roles with the most consistent demand and strongest rates. |
| **Online Business Manager (OBM)** | Runs operations, projects and the team, not tasks | The ceiling of this ladder. Experienced OBMs cited at $65-180/hr, or $1,500-7,500/month retainers. **This is the aspirational end of the bootcamp story.** |
| **Social media VA / manager** | Plans and schedules the weekly posts | Already the account's second route. |
| **Content repurposing VA** | One long asset into 20 short ones | Rising fast with short-form video. |
| **Short-form video editor** | Reels, Shorts, TikToks | Skill-gated, well paid, portfolio-provable. |
| **Email marketing VA** | Broadcasts, flows, list hygiene | Klaviyo/ConvertKit skill is nameable and testable. |
| **Funnel / tech / systems VA** | Zapier, Make, GoHighLevel, Kajabi, ClickFunnels | Called out as **the most accessible high-paying niche for someone with no prior specialist experience**, learnable in 3-6 months of focused study. Strong bootcamp fit. |
| **CRM / pipeline VA** | Keeping the sales database true | Named among consistently-demanded roles. |
| **Appointment setter / ISA** | Outbound calls and booked calls | Performance-paid, high ceiling, voice-dependent. |
| **Customer support VA** | Tickets, chat, refunds | Volume route, shift-dependent. |
| **Bookkeeping VA** | QuickBooks/Xero, reconciliations, AR chasing | Direct B.Com neighbour. |
| **Launch manager** | Runs a course or product launch end to end | Named among highest-paying niches. |
| **Community manager** | Runs the Circle/Slack/FB group | Underrated, retention-critical. |
| **Podcast VA** | Editing, show notes, guest booking, distribution | Clean deliverable, easy portfolio. |
| **Pinterest manager** | A real, distinct niche for ecommerce and bloggers | Narrow but real. |
| **SEO / content VA** | Briefs, on-page, internal linking | Direct content-writing bootcamp overlap. |
| **AI ops VA** | Prompt libraries, GPT workflows, ambient tools | Professionals experienced in AI-supported workflows named among the highest paid. **This is where the Women's AI bootcamp and this one join up.** |

### By vertical (the specialisations that pay)

Legal VA · medical VA / virtual scribe / prior-auth VA · dental VA · mental-health intake VA ·
real estate VA and **transaction coordinator** · insurance VA · financial-services VA ·
e-commerce and Amazon PPC VA · med-spa / aesthetics SMM · home-services dispatch VA ·
STR co-host · vet practice VA · ABA practice VA.

The named highest-paying set across sources: **legal VA, launch management, financial
services support, tech/systems VA, and medical admin.**

### Routes that are not "VA" at all

Worth testing as separate ads, because "VA" carries a low-status connotation for some
Indian professionals and these do not:

- **Transaction coordinator** (real estate) - a titled role, not an assistant.
- **Virtual medical scribe** - a titled clinical-adjacent role.
- **Intake coordinator** (legal, therapy) - a titled role.
- **Online Business Manager** - explicitly senior to VA.
- **Freelance social media manager** - already in use, already approved.
- **Bookkeeper** - a profession, not an assistant.
- **Airbnb co-host** - a platform-recognised title with its own marketplace.
- **Medical biller / prior authorisation specialist** - a titled role.
- **Community manager / launch manager** - titled roles in the creator economy.

Per the note already in the USRoles kit: an earlier kit invented role names and was rejected
with *"those jobs don't really seem real, I doubt anyone is hiring."* Every title in the two
lists above is a real, searchable job title with real job postings behind it. That is the bar.

## 6. Things to be careful about

- **Real estate is contracting.** NAR projects membership could fall to ~1.2 million by end
  2026 from ~1.45 million. Still 2 million licensees, but do not write "booming".
- **Solo dentistry is shrinking**, not growing - 15% solo among dentists in their first
  decade vs 48% among 25+ year veterans. The 178,461-practice number is the honest anchor,
  not the solo share.
- **The creator economy number is soft.** 50% of creators earn under $15,000/yr and online
  courses are only 4.4% of creator earnings. Do not build an ad implying course creators are
  a rich client pool.
- **Etsy's seller count fell** from 8.1M to 5.5-5.6M after an inactive-account purge. Use
  5.6M and never the older 8.1M.
- **Medical scribing is the most AI-exposed route** on this list. Sell prior-auth,
  coordination and billing rather than transcription.
- **The "$8-10bn virtual assistant market, 24% CAGR" figure is not us** - it measures
  AI/intelligent assistants. The USRoles kit already flagged this. It is still circulating;
  do not let it back in.
- **India-side graduate counts are the weakest numbers here.** Seat counts (1,38,321 BSc
  Nursing seats; ~1,30,182 GNM seats) are firmer than graduate counts. The Rs 20,000-40,000
  BPT starting salary and the 4-lakh pharmacy figure come from career-guidance and social
  sources, not official statistics - **verify before either appears on a landing page**, and
  neither belongs on an ad face under the existing money rule.
- **Rule 1 applies to every row.** None of these ads may say "your degree is worthless" or
  "clinics will not hire you". The frame is: the credential you already hold is the hiring
  criterion for a better-paid client abroad. That is what made the dietitian ad work.
