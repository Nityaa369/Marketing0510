# Corporate Finance, wave 3 - reader list for round-one approval (17 to 19 Oct 2026, Rs 10)

Drafted 29 Sep 2026 from the Aug (`Corporate finance bootcamp aug 15`) and Oct
(`CorpFinance_bootcamp_17-19Oct_CBO`) ad-level pulls, cost per Rs 10 buyer incl. GST, each ad
against its own campaign's average (Aug Rs 229, Oct Rs 249). Pages convert as well as in August
(12.6-13.2% of page visits); Instagram CTR fell 0.66% to 0.54%, so the need is new hooks, not
new pages.

Winning formula in both months: a question naming the reader's job and where it stops, then the
move into deal work. "A loan consultant whose every conversation ends at a loan?" / "Do corporate
finance work that goes further than arranging the loan." Definition (Oct, from the script):
corporate finance is helping a business raise the money it needs, as a loan or as investment,
and seeing the deal through to closing.

Hooks revised 29 Sep 2026 for the 20% brain-power test: one plain line, the work they already do,
then the step up. No clever angle.

## 1. Loan consultants and DSAs
Proven: Aug 0.85x on 396, Oct 0.73x on 75. Hook: "You arrange loans. Now help businesses raise investment."

## 2. Cost accountants (CMA)
Proven: Aug 0.88x on 281, Oct 0.78x on 208. Hook: "You know a company's costs. Now help it raise money."

## 3. Equity research analysts
Proven: Aug 0.94x on 169, Oct 0.89x on 121. Hook: "You analyse companies. Now help them raise money."

## 4. Credit analysts at NBFCs and banks
Near par, only ever run with a pay-led headline: Oct 1.20x on 78 ("Move from credit into a
better-paid corporate finance career"). Hook: "You judge loan files. Now help businesses raise money."

## 5. FP&A analysts
Near par, only ever run with a pay-led headline: Oct 1.09x on 35. Hook: "You build the forecast. Now help raise the money."

## 6. Chartered accountants who prepare project reports for bank loans
New cut of practising CAs (Aug 1.01x on 122 as "From Compliance To Deal Advisory"). Hook: "You write project reports. Now help clients raise money."

## 7. SME relationship managers at banks
Never run: built in the Oct Venn and Notify kits, never launched. Hook: "You sell your bank's loans. Now advise on all funding."
