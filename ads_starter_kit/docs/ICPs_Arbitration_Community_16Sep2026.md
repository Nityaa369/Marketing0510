## 0.6 What the 14 days actually teach (so `fit` is scored against the real offer)

Day 1 clause mastery, 2 Section 21 invocation, 3 pre-arbitration negotiation, 4 ODR and virtual

9 international commercial arbitration, 10 construction / FIDIC, 11 awards and challenges,
12 client opportunities, 13 visibility and empanelment, 14 capstone. Counsel-side. 6 live
webinars, 14 templates, certificate, Rs 200, refundable. The page addresses "lawyers, law
graduates and final-year students".

**It is not an arbitrator-training course.** That is why two candidates in §3 were struck.

## 1. The list

### A - Advocates, split by the practice they already run

1. **recovery-panel-advocates** - Advocates on a bank or NBFC panel who file cheque-bounce (s.138) and recovery matters at a fixed fee per matter. Most NBFC loan agreements carry an arbitration clause, so the lender's larger recoveries run as arbitrations, and the counsel who drafts the claim and argues it is paid per matter at a different scale. Ceiling: the panel pays for filings, volume, not for running the case. Awareness: problem aware.
2. **msme-dues-recovery-advocates** - Advocates who recover unpaid dues for small manufacturers and traders: legal notices, recovery suits, MSME Samadhaan filings. Under s.18 of the MSMED Act the Facilitation Council sends an unsettled delayed-payment claim to conciliation and then arbitration, so their own clients' matters already become arbitrations. Ceiling: billed per notice and per suit, and the hearing stage goes to someone else. Awareness: unaware that this is arbitration practice.
3. **partnership-family-business-advocates** - Advocates who draft partnership deeds and handle disputes between partners and family business owners. Partnership deeds routinely carry an arbitration clause, so when partners fall out, the matter they drafted goes to arbitration. Ceiling: they draft the deed and then refer the dispute to a senior. Awareness: problem aware.
4. **real-estate-rera-advocates** - Advocates handling RERA complaints, builder-buyer matters and developer contracts. Developer-contractor and joint-development agreements carry arbitration clauses, and the construction side of real estate is FIDIC-style contract work (day 10). Ceiling: flat-fee RERA complaints on the buyer side. Awareness: solution aware, they know the contracts.
5. **contract-drafting-lawyers** - Lawyers who draft and vet commercial contracts (vendor agreements, MSAs, NDAs, JVs) for clients or a firm. They write the arbitration clause, and when it is invoked the dispute goes to a disputes lawyer. Ceiling: paid for the draft, never for the dispute the draft decides. Warm: the Contract Drafting community already runs on this account. Awareness: product aware of clauses, not of the practice.
6. **ibc-nclt-practitioners** - Advocates filing Section 9 operational-creditor petitions before the NCLT. Where the debtor shows a pre-existing dispute the petition fails (Mobilox, 2017) and the claim has to be fought on its merits, usually under the contract's arbitration clause. Ceiling: their matter leaves the forum they practise in. Awareness: solution aware.
7. **adr-panel-mediators** - Advocates on a legal services authority or court-annexed mediation panel, paid an honorarium per matter. They already do ADR, in the room, with both sides. Ceiling: honorarium work with no route into the paid end of dispute resolution (day 13 empanelment). Awareness: solution aware.

### B - In-house

8. **infra-epc-in-house-legal** - Legal managers and legal officers at construction, EPC and infrastructure companies who manage contractor and client claims and brief outside counsel when a claim goes to arbitration. Ceiling: they brief the counsel; they do not become it. Construction is where the large fees sit (day 10 FIDIC). `mh02_office` "Manage Your Company's Arbitration In-House" is an APPROVED precedent for the in-house angle. Awareness: problem aware.

### C - Life situations with a trained discipline underneath

9. **judiciary-exam-aspirants** - Law graduates two to four years into judicial services preparation, studying CPC, Evidence and Contract every day. Arbitration practice runs on exactly that procedure law. Ceiling: an attempt cycle and an age limit, with no income while preparing. Offer a disputes practice that uses what they already studied, never "you failed". Awareness: unaware.
10. **llm-graduates-no-disputes-role** - LLM graduates in business, corporate or dispute resolution law, within two years of the degree, not working in disputes. Ceiling: the specialist degree did not come with a specialist role. Awareness: problem aware.
11. **gulf-indian-legal-consultants** - Indian-qualified lawyers working as legal consultants or legal executives in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. Gulf construction and infrastructure contracts are overwhelmingly FIDIC-based and arbitrated. Ceiling: a consultant title in a market where the disputes work goes to firm counsel. Awareness: solution aware.

## 3. Considered and struck

| Candidate | Why struck |
|---|---|
| Retired railway / CPWD / PWD engineers as arbitrators | The course is counsel-side, not arbitrator training. And the Supreme Court's Nov 2024 constitution bench on unilateral appointment (CORE v ECI-SPIC) narrowed the one-party panel route this ICP rested on. |
| Chartered accountants doing claims and damages work | Quantum experts, not counsel. The offer does not change their outcome (fit 2). |
| Consumer forum advocates | Consumers can keep a dispute in the forum despite an arbitration clause (Emaar MGF, 2018). The adjacency is weak. |
| Tax / GST advocates | Tax disputes are not arbitrable. |
| Small city / tier 2 advocates | Already run in July at n=2 and n=4. A situation with no discipline under it. |
| Company secretaries, law students who moot, junior drafting lawyers | Already in `SOP/ICP_INDEX.md` as GOLDEN / approved. Not new. |
| Insurance and maritime lawyers | Populations too small to reach on Meta at Rs 200. |

## 4. Caveats - read before any fact reaches a face

- Every legal fact above is **buying-pressure evidence for this list, not ad copy.** Before any
  of it goes on a face or page, check it against the Act or judgment: NBFC arbitration clause
  prevalence, s.18(3) MSMED Act, Mobilox (2017), Emaar MGF v Aftab Singh (2018), CORE (8 Nov
  2024), FIDIC prevalence in GCC contracts.
- **Rule 1 risk sits in 2, 6 and 8.** "The Council sends your client's case to arbitration" and
  "your s.9 petition fails" can drift into consequence framing. The value props below are
  written as the better job; the hooks must hold that line.
- **Reachability is scored for the face doing the selecting** (a broad lawyer audience and the
  practice named in the call-out), the way Patent named degrees, not for Meta detailed targeting,
  which cannot pick out an NBFC panel advocate.
- **Gulf (11) needs a payment check first.** Rs 200 through GrowthX from a UAE card, in INR,
  has never been tested on this funnel.
- `pain_score` is not fitted to live cost. It decides what to build, not what will convert.
