## What education ad benchmarks say (third-party, treat as directional)

From [webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/education-coaching-ad-creative-statistics) and [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ad-benchmarks-education-2026):
- **Career-outcome hooks beat generic promotion.** These sources report 40 to 60% higher CTR. Our own data says CTR does not predict cost, so judge on cost per buyer, never CTR.
- **Testimonial-first creative converts at 2 to 3 times the rate of credential-led copy.** UGC-style ads report 20 to 30% lower cost per lead.
- **Video and Reels report the lowest cost per lead** (USD 8 to 25), but fatigue within 2 to 4 weeks. Statics last 4 to 8 weeks.
- **The "skeptic" testimonial** ("I thought this was a scam, but...") meets the objection head on.
- **The "salary reveal" video does not fit our rules.** Income figures never go on our faces, and pay ladders stay page-only and sourced.

## What to use in our NCA ads next

1. **"Dual-qualify" as a headline frame.** For example: "Dual-qualify: India and Canada". This is SQE's cheapest-ad logic ("without pausing your practice") said in two words.
2. **"Try the route for Rs 10."** This is BARBRI's preview logic, with the price as a taste of the real thing.
3. **One objection per face,** answered in the email or chat body:
   - "I'd need a Canadian degree"
   - "My English test"
   - "I'm too far into my career"
   - "I can't leave my practice"
4. **A real learner on video (UGC),** once consent is confirmed. It is the strongest format in every benchmark, and the account's own videos ran 0.66x on Tech Law. A static can't do this; it needs a real person filmed.
5. **Keep the house rules:**
   - no scarcity
   - no salary on the face
   - no borrowed institutional authority or pass rates
   - judge on cost per buyer at 7 days and 20+ results
