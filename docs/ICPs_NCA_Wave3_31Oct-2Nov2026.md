## The learner's seat: what makes me click, then show up
I do not want to learn about the NCA. I want certainty about my own life. Six questions, in order:
1. Is this for someone like me? (the call-out names me)
2. Is it real, and can I actually do it? (the route, with sourced facts)
3. What do I walk out with? (my answer, my plan, one thing I wrote myself)
4. What does it cost me, and what do I keep? (Rs 10, refundable; from India; my practice stays)
5. Will it make my life bigger? (a second market, a second licence, never a replacement)
6. Why now? (the 2026 rules changed; one weekend) with no fear in it

**The one idea:** sell the personal outcome, not the topic. By Monday night I know what the NCA
will most likely ask of me, I have written one exam answer myself, and I hold my own 12-month plan.
Constant delivery sentence, only the outcome clause moves: "In our 3-day live bootcamp, we show
you how to clear the NCA exams from India, how to find Canadian legal work while you prepare, and
your 12-month plan to the Canadian bar."

## Honest limits (show him these)
- **Cells 1, 6 and 9 win on reach and intent, not on proven cheapness.** Litigators are at par,
  not cheap. Judge each on 7 full days and 20+ buyers.
- **Cells 3 and 4 share one evidence pool.** Separate them on creative and landing page, not on
  the cost number.
- **There is no 2 to 5 year cell.** The broad cell 1 and the 0 to 2 and 5+ cells bracket it.
- **"Know what the NCA will most likely ask of you" is the strongest claim we can make.** Never
  "you are eligible", never a guaranteed result. Camp content (the written answer, the 12-month
  plan, the Day 2 and 3 demos) is from the April 2024 script; the 2026 script has not been seen.
- Numbers ("0 to 2 years", "5+ years") live in targeting, not on faces: ID "15+ years" ran 1.90x
  and SQE "seniors 15+" ran 1.29x. Where Ruchika asks for one on a face, build a no-number twin.

## Moved to the bench, not struck
Women lawyers returning after a break (0.90x, n=8,586), lawyers running their own practice (0.80x
on DPDP), lawyers in the Gulf (0.88x, n=268), lawyers in tier 2 and 3 cities (never tried on a
foreign-qualification camp). Struck: law students in general (1.10x), freelancers (standing ban),
non-LLB professionals (the NCA assesses a law degree), judiciary aspirants mid-attempt. One test ad
at most, never a cell: LLM-comparers (1.37x on SQE), lawyers already in Canada (geo 2.10x).
Excluded on Ruchika's instruction: spouse or family moving, and company secretaries. For the
record, the spouse cell (nca02) is the best-proven NCA cell this year (0.93x, n=69).
