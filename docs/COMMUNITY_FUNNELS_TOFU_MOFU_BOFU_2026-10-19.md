## 2. What each stage means for a community camp

The business ladder is: free or Rs 10 bootcamp (3 days) → community camp (14 to 30 days, paid,
refundable) → paid course. The community camp is both a product with its own ad funnel and the
middle step of the ladder. Each ad stage has one job.

| | TOFU (cold) | MOFU (warm) | BOFU (hot) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Never touched us for this camp. Named reader (occupation or situation). | Clicked or viewed the page in 30 days, watched 50%+ of a video, engaged on IG/FB, or bought a related Rs 10 bootcamp. | Started checkout and did not pay (14 days), or viewed the page 2+ times. |
| **Pool per 90 days** | Lakhs | ~1.2 to 1.6 lakh page viewers per camp (Measured) | ~1,500 to 2,600 checkout drop-offs per camp (Measured: checkouts minus buyers) |
| **Their question** | "Is this for someone like me, and what would I become?" | "What exactly will I be able to do, and is it real?" | "Is it worth Rs X, and what if it is not for me?" |
| **Ad's job** | Name the reader, name the prize (the move and what they become). | Show the work: the documents, the sessions, the first thing they will make, proof from learners. | Remove the last doubt: full exchange, refundable, live only, what happens after paying. |
| **Best formats on record** | Calendar faces (Crim Lit 0.87x), cartoon (Legal AI 0.83x), sprint (CD 0.80x) | Curriculum grid / weekly calendar, document mock, learner proof, video walk-through | Plain offer card with button; FAQ-style "you asked" face |
| **Landing page** | 21-step page, first fold repeats the ad | Same page, deep-linked to the work section, or a "what you'll draft" page | Straight to the price card and pay button, with FAQ |
| **Judge on** | Cost per buyer, 7 days, 20+ buyers | Cost per buyer and view-to-checkout | Cost per buyer; frequency capped |
| **Exclude** | Buyers (all time), MOFU and BOFU pools | Buyers, BOFU pool | Buyers |

Rules that bite at MOFU and BOFU:
- **Dates on creatives:** Ramanuj, 13 Aug: dates go on the page, not the ad. A BOFU ad without
  "starts 21 Oct" is weaker. **Needs his call**: whether BOFU retargeting faces may carry the start
  date.
- **No invented urgency** ("seats left", countdowns). A real start date stated plainly is the
  only urgency allowed.
- **No hedge above the pay button; button states the full exchange** (e.g. "Join all 21 days for
  Rs 200, refundable"). The community button wording is not settled yet.

## 3. Per community

### 3.1 Contract Drafting (21 days, 21 Oct to 11 Nov)
- **Learner's seat:** "I studied law and I draft at work or not at all. I want to be paid to draft
  contracts for businesses, from wherever I am. Can I do it, who pays, how do I find them?"
- **TOFU prize:** become a contract drafting lawyer for startups and businesses. Reader pools:
  junior lawyers drafting under a senior, in-house and corporate lawyers, litigators who want
  desk work, law graduates not practising, women lawyers returning after a break (cd_women 1.07x),
  final-year law students (student pad 0.95x). Freelancers: banned audience and banned word.
- **MOFU proof:** 17 named documents in 21 days (NDA to joint development agreement); 9 live
  sessions; AI to find red flags; the client questions; the first article and LinkedIn profile.
  Strong warm pool: Data Privacy, Tech Law and US Corporate Law Rs 10 bootcamp buyers (Hypothesis).
- **BOFU:** Rs 200 refundable, daily reading and quiz on WhatsApp, live sessions in the evening,
  what arrives after payment.
- **Check:** checkout to buy is 56%, the lowest of all. Test-buy the page; check the pixel.

### 3.2 AI for Legal (14 days, 21 Oct to early Nov)
- **Learner's seat:** "I am a lawyer. Everyone says AI. I want to be the lawyer who uses it,
  not the one replaced by it, and I want it to bring me clients, not just save me time."
  (The fear stays in their head. It never goes on our face.)
- **TOFU prize:** run your own AI-powered practice, or lead Legal AI Ops. Not "draft faster":
  that is same-desk work (Rule 1). Reader pools: litigators (anticipatory bail, injunction, divorce
  are in the program), junior associates, solo practitioners, in-house counsel, law firm owners.
- **MOFU proof:** the things they build: a contract review agent, an NDA triage, an outreach
  system, a content agent, an app from their workflow. "Build" is the hook here, and it is true to
  the calendar.
- **BOFU:** price, refundable, the 8 live sessions, what they will have built by the last day.
- **Before MOFU and BOFU can run:** no Legal AI warm audience exists. Create one (page viewers
  30 days, checkout 14 days, buyers all time).

### 3.3 Criminal Litigation (9 webinars, 22 Oct to 14 Nov)
- **Learner's seat:** "I want my own criminal matters, not carrying files for a senior. Show me
  the drafting and the court moves that win a client's trust."
- **TOFU prize:** build your own criminal practice, including cyber and UPI fraud cases. Reader
  pools: junior advocates, newly enrolled advocates, civil litigators adding criminal work, law
  graduates preparing to start practice, district court advocates. Audience fatigue is real
  (frequency 7.5 to 14 in Sep): new readers, not a reskin.
- **MOFU proof:** the calendar itself is the cheapest face on record (0.87x, 943 buyers). Name the
  real documents: quashing petition, FIR complaint before the Magistrate, opposition to police
  custody, Section 63 BSA certificate, LOC.
- **BOFU:** price, refundable, 9 webinars, evening times.
- **Fix:** its problem is fatigue, so TOFU readers matter most here.

### 3.4 Independent Director (30 days)
- **Learner's seat:** "I have led teams for decades. I want a seat at a board table, with respect,
  as a second innings. Is it real, and what do I have to do?"
- **TOFU prize:** become a registered independent director (IICA databank, exam cleared, profile
  boards can find). Reader pools from the free camp that ran cheap: bankers (C4 Rs 57/lead),
  retired educators (V3 Rs 66/lead on 4,784), social sector (id19), civil servants, retired forces.
- **MOFU:** the free ID bootcamp's leads and attendees are the natural warm pool for the paid

  ladder of the four. (Inferred)
- **BOFU:** price, refundable, 11 webinars, the exam sessions.
- **Blockers:** the batch 86-87 calendar is half over; I need the next batch's dates and access to

## 4. Twenty ad sets per community: what it costs, and a proposed split

20 ad sets x up to 5 ads (Ramanuj's cap) = up to 100 ads and, under 1 ad = 1 page, up to 100
landing pages per community. Four communities = up to 400 pairs. (Calculated)

**Budget to judge them fairly:** 20 buyers per ad set per 7 days x par (~Rs 300 to 480) = about
Rs 6,000 to 9,600 per ad set per week, or **Rs 1.2L to 1.9L per community per week**. Today CD
spends about Rs 1.05L a week. (Calculated) Budgets are Ramanuj's call.

**Proposed split per community (Hypothesis, needs Ramanuj):**

| Stage | Ad sets | Audience | Why this many |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | 14 | One named reader per ad set, broad targeting, exclude warm + buyers | Fatigue needs new readers; one reader per ad set keeps the test clean |
| MOFU | 4 | Page viewers 30d; video viewers 50%; IG/FB engagers 60d; related Rs 10 bootcamp buyers | 1.2 to 1.6L pool supports 4 messages, not more |
| BOFU | 2 | Checkout drop-offs 14d; repeat page viewers 7d | ~600 to 900 people a month: two ad sets at most, low budget, frequency watched |

All 20 ad sets sit in one campaign per community, so no two of our campaigns bid against each
other (Ramanuj, 10 Aug). If MOFU and BOFU need their own campaign for budget control, they must
exclude each other and TOFU must exclude both.

**Order of work (from the rules):** this funnel doc → reader list per community, as text, approved
→ value props and hooks → one ad + page → the rest.
