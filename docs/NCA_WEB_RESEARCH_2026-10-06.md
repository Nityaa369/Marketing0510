# NCA and Canadian bar: what the web says, 6 Oct 2026

Collected for the NCA bootcamp (31 Oct to 2 Nov 2026, LawSikho). Official sources are marked
**[official]**. Everything else is a third-party guide or news report: use it for angles, and
re-check before putting a fact on a page.

## 1. The route (this is what the ads now show)

1. **NCA assessment.** The NCA (National Committee on Accreditation, run by the Federation of Law
   Societies of Canada) assesses the foreign law degree and assigns exams or courses.
2. **NCA exams.** Pass them to get the **Certificate of Qualification**. This puts you level with a
   Canadian common-law graduate.
3. **Provincial bar licensing.** In Ontario that means the Law Society of Ontario's barrister and
   solicitor exams plus 10 months of articling, or the 8-month Law Practice Program.
4. **Licensed:** you can practise in Canada.

Without step 2 there is no step 3, which is why the faces say "Clear the NCA exams, then pass the
bar licensing, then practise". The other way in is a Canadian JD. We deliberately do not say "the
only route".

## 2. What changed in 2026 (the "2026 notification")

- **From 1 March 2026, two new NCA requirements**
  ([CIC News](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/03/canada-adds-knowledge-criteria-language-testing-for-foreign-trained-lawyers-0373282.html),
  [NCA Tutor](https://www.nca-tutor.com/ncaexamblog/2026/1/14/preparing-for-march-1-2026-new-mandatory-requirements-you-need-to-know),
  [The NCA Hub](https://www.thencahub.com/blog/article-f1-nca-policy-changes-2026/)):
  1. **Mandatory language screening, now for everyone.** Earlier, an English-medium degree was
     enough; Indian LLBs used to be exempt this way.
     - The screening test is a proctored Versant English Placement Test (61 overall), with one
       attempt included in the assessment fee.
     - If you fail it, you need a full test: IELTS General 7 in each band, CELPIP 9 in each band,
       or PTE Core at set scores.
     - A valid test less than 2 years old can exempt you from the screening.
  2. **A stand-alone Indigenous Law and Peoples course** before the Certificate of Qualification.
     - It covers residential schools, UNDRIP, Aboriginal-Crown relations, the doctrines of
       Discovery and terra nullius, and systemic discrimination.
     - It is done as the CPLED online module or an approved law-school course. One guide says
       about 8 to 12 hours, self-paced; CIC calls the CPLED module six weeks.
- **Buzz:** one report says the rule landed with very little notice
  ([VisaHQ](https://www.visahq.com/news/2026-02-28/ca/canada-tightens-language-and-indigenous-law-requirements-for-foreign-trained-lawyers/)).
  NCA Tutor was already writing about it in mid-January, so **do not repeat "hours' notice"**.
- **Policy update effective 1 July 2025** **[official]**
  ([nca.legal](https://nca.legal/updates-to-nca-assessment-and-appeal-policy-2/)):
  - definitions were clarified;
  - appeals are now written only;
  - a new "National Requirement 2029" standard applies from 1 Jan 2029.
- **Ontario bar reform is pending, not decided.**
  - The LSO committee proposed in September 2025 to replace the barrister and solicitor exams with
    a mandatory skills-based course. Consultation closed 31 Jan 2026.
  - Bar groups pushed back, and Ontario's Attorney General said scrapping written exams "simply
    aren't acceptable".
  - No final decision was found
    ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/ontario-bar-exam-replaced-9.6987640),
    [Law Times](https://www.lawtimesnews.com/resources/professional-regulation/legal-orgs-tell-lso-they-support-reforms-to-licensing-process-but-want-to-keep-standardized-exams/393175),
    [LSO consultation](https://lso.ca/about-lso/initiatives/consultation-lawyer-licensing-process)).
  - **Never say "the bar exam is gone".** Say "bar licensing".
  - Other provinces (Alberta, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia, PEI) already use the PREP
    course.

## 3. Official costs and timelines [official]

From [nca.legal/costs-and-timelines](https://nca.legal/costs-and-timelines/):

- **Fees:**
  - Assessment: CAD 400 plus tax.
  - Each exam: CAD 500 plus tax.
  - Minimum total is about CAD 3,000 plus tax.
- **Exam schedule:** exams run **12 times a year (monthly)**. Results come 10 to 12 weeks after the
  sitting.
- **Timeline:**
  - Best case **10 months**, average **about two years**.
  - You must finish within **five years** of your assessment result.
- **Assessment speed:** gathering documents takes 2 to 6 weeks; the assessment itself takes 6 to 8
  weeks.

## 4. Indian LLB specifics (third-party guides)

From [The NCA Hub](https://www.thencahub.com/blog/article-d3-nca-indian-lawyers/),
[LawSection](https://lawsection.in/can-indian-lawyers-practice-in-canada/) and
[iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/how-to-get-pr-in-canada-from-india-a-guide-for-indian-lawyers/):

- **Usual subjects:** 5 core ones (Foundations of Canadian Law, Constitutional, Administrative,
  Criminal, Professional Responsibility), plus electives depending on the degree.
- **Exam format:**
  - Open book with hard-copy notes, up to 3 hours.
  - 50% pass mark, 3 attempts per subject.
  - **Written from India** with online proctoring (MonitorEDU).
- **No Canadian LLM is needed** to apply. This matches our script's "do not buy the LLM first"
  argument.
- **Traps Indian candidates fall into:** Criminal Law (IPC instinct misleads), Professional
  Responsibility (all new), Administrative and Constitutional review (Vavilov, not Wednesbury).
  - This is a strong page angle: our training is an asset, *and* we show you exactly where it
    differs.
  - On the face, keep the "Criminal Code reads like the IPC" line soft: similar roots, different
    rules.
- **Timeline to full licence:** "18 to 36 months" for most Indian lawyers (LawSection, not
  official).
- **Ontario pass rates (2023, Globe and Mail via search):**
  - Barrister exam: Canadian grads 88%, foreign-trained 58%.
  - Solicitor exam: Canadian grads 81%, foreign-trained 50%.
  - Page only, sourced. It argues for preparation, never as fear.
- **Ontario share:** "almost 30% of lawyers admitted in Ontario studied law outside Canada"
  (iPleaders, re-source before use).
- **Community cost talk** (Reddit, via search snippet): about CAD 500 per exam, plus materials, plus
  tutors at about CAD 500 per exam. This is the "it is expensive and confusing alone" pain our
  Rs 10 camp answers.
