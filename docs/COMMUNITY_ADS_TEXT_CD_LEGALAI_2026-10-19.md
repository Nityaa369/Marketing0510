## Critic's read (CLAUDE.md order: who, offer, prize, clarity, rules)
- **Who:** every call-out is an occupation or situation a lawyer would tick. No freelancers, no
  wishes. CD stays with law students, graduates and lawyers; Legal AI is lawyers only.
- **Offer:** CD11 (in-house) and AI07, AI10 (associate, in-house) carry the Rule 1 risk of "more
  work at the same desk" (Data Privacy, 12 Aug: "you need to offer a different career"). AI10 is
  the weakest; I recommend striking it unless Ramanuj wants the test.
- **Prize:** every TOFU head names the move and what they become. CD12 and AI12 are controls built
  on the cheapest past ads, rewritten so the biggest line names the prize, not the product.
- **MOFU and BOFU heads** show the work or the exchange by design, because the reader already
  knows the prize. Ramanuj's "biggest line names the prize" rule was set on cold ads; whether it
  applies to retargeting faces is **his call**.
- **Proof:** CD17 and AI17 need real learner testimonials with consent. Without them, swap in a
  second "where clients come from" face.

**Verdict: fix first.** Before any design:
1. Confirm the four claims in the table above, from the script.
2. Strike or keep AI10.
3. Supply two testimonials per camp, or approve the swap.
