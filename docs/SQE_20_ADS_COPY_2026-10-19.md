Format follows Virat's Contract Drafting example. Primary audience: litigators and lawyers who want to
move abroad. Rows 1 to 8 litigators, 9 to 14 moving abroad, 15 to 20 other lawyers.
`₹[PRICE]` is open: the files describe a free 7-day community, so Ramanuj decides free or a token fee.
Supporting copy is traceable to the community sessions: written exams in India (six Pearson VUE cities),
the SQE2 exemption route (Day 2), a mock SQE1 (Day 3), career pathways (Day 4), international profile
(Day 5), launch lab with a reference letter review (Day 6), live Q&A (Day 7).

## Flags before anything is built
1. `₹[PRICE]`: free or a token fee is Ramanuj's call. The CTA changes with it.
2. Row 13: "your Indian licence does not travel with you" is a threat-shaped opener. Use "You moved abroad" and
   let the second sentence carry the prize.
3. Row 12 and 14: thin audiences. Rows 9, 10 and 11 overlap; run one broad and one Gulf, not all three.
4. Row 10 and 7: DIFC and cross-border finance wording needs a source line on the page.
5. Rows 4 to 8 say "become X" for work the reader already does; the move abroad is carried by "solicitor of
   England and Wales", so check the blind read.
6. Six Indian cities, DIFC and arbitration facts come from the Day 1 and Day 2 decks. Re-check them on
   sqe.sra.org.uk before a landing page.
