## 1. Contract Drafting Community (CD 64 and 65), 21-day program

**Dates:** 21 Oct (Wed) to 11 Nov (Wed). Holidays 30 Oct, 6 Nov, Diwali 7 to 8 Nov.
**Format:** daily reading + daily quiz on WhatsApp, 9 live sessions (evenings, 5 to 8 PM).
**Price on record:** Rs 200 (LP rulebook). Brand on record: SkillArbitrage (`SA[Community]`).

**Documents taught (one a day):** parts of a contract, non-compete, e-commerce terms and
conditions, employment agreement (plus remote work monitoring policy), NDA and confidentiality,
cookie policy, co-founder agreement, DPDPA privacy policy, ESOP, SAFE agreement, term sheet, SaaS
agreement, representations and warranties, IP licensing, commercial lease, vendor service
agreement, joint development agreement. Extra: audit of D2C websites for silent obligations.

**Live sessions:**
1. Orientation + using AI to find missing clauses and red flags (influencer agreement)
2. Finding issues in terms and conditions using AI
3. Listing your first gig on Fiverr and starting a practice on it
4. Questions to ask a client before drafting (co-founder agreement) + roadmap
5. Networking with UK SMEs using a GDPR-compliant privacy policy
6. Apollo, direct pitching through Y Combinator
7. Psychological profiling of law firm partners and in-house counsel
8. LinkedIn profile: become a public contract authority
9. Publishing your first article

**What the reader becomes:** a contract drafting lawyer who drafts startup and business documents
(founders' agreements, SaaS, ESOP, SAFE, privacy policies) for paying clients in India and abroad,
and knows how to find those clients (Fiverr, UK SMEs, Y Combinator startups, LinkedIn).

**Watch-outs:** "freelancing career" is in the calendar title but freelance/freelancer is a banned
word on our copy and a banned audience. Fiverr is a third-party brand: never on the face.
**Past ads:** par Rs 480/buyer. Cheapest: `cd_sprint v1` Rs 382 (262 buyers), `cd_contracts_grid`
Rs 463. Women restart/remote Rs 511 to 516.

## 2. AI for Legal (batch 27 and 28), 14-day program (tentative)

**Dates:** 21 Oct (Wed) to closing on "4 Nov, Tuesday". **Calendar error:** 4 Nov 2026 is a
Wednesday; Day 13 is 2 Nov (Mon), so closing is probably 3 Nov (Tue). Confirm before any page.
**Format:** daily reading + quiz, 8 live sessions at 8 PM (7 PM Sat, 3 PM Sun).
**Price and brand:** not on record. The June campaign was `Sa_community_Legalai`: confirm brand.

**Live sessions:**
1. Orientation + AI lawyer toolkit (drafting an anticipatory bail application)
2. Smart litigation: drafting based on judicial psychology + LM Studio (local AI)
3. AI client outreach system: Apollo, Claude and an outreach app
4. 24x7 AI content and personal branding agent
5. Build your AI contract review agent with Claude Cowork
6. NDA triage on Claude Cowork
7. Build your Legal AI Ops function
8. Turn your AI workflow into an app

**Reading:** drafting in your senior's style, outreach via Y Combinator and RocketReach, an e-book
to show authority, contract negotiation playbook, deep research with every citation verified,
mutual consent divorce petition, D2C compliance gaps, temporary injunction, ethical brochure,
client delivery engine (training manual for juniors), AI client communication.

**What the reader becomes:** a lawyer who runs a practice on AI: drafts litigation and contract
work faster, has built their own review agents and apps, and brings in clients through automated
outreach and content. The strongest "new career" line is the **Legal AI Ops** role and "your own
practice that runs like a firm".

**Watch-outs:** Rule 1 risk. "Draft faster at your current job" is more work at the same desk.
The prize has to be the move (own practice, Legal AI Ops lead, the AI lawyer clients look for),
not speed. No AI-fear ("AI will replace lawyers").
**Past ads:** par Rs 396. Cheapest `cr03_legalai_toon` Rs 329 (421 buyers); toolbox Rs 424.

## 3. Criminal Litigation Community (CL 77 and 78), webinar schedule

**Dates:** 22 Oct (Thu) to 14 Nov (Sat). 9 webinars, 7 or 8 PM.
**Price and brand:** not on record. June campaign `PK_[21day_Community]_CriminalLitigation`.

**Webinars:**
1. FIR registration, letter to the SP, complaint before the Magistrate
2. Using AI to analyse a judge's persona and get favourable orders
3. Drafting a quashing petition
4. Handling a UPI fraud case, opportunities in criminal litigation, roadmap
5. CDR (call detail record) analysis + your pathway into criminal litigation
6. LinkedIn profile
7. Tendering a Section 63 BSA certificate, and getting a case dismissed without one
8. Opposing police custody (Section 187 BNSS)

**What the reader becomes:** a criminal litigator with their own matters: FIRs, quashing,
custody, bail-adjacent work, cyber and UPI fraud cases, digital evidence, LOC and passport cases.

**Watch-outs:** "get favourable orders" is an outcome promise: never on the face. Lawyers only
(LLB holders, law students for some cells).
**Past ads:** par Rs 309. Cheapest `crimlit_sep26_04_full-calendar` Rs 269 on 943 buyers (the
calendar face works for this camp), weekly calendar Rs 301. Nameplate and junior faces

## 4. Independent Director 30-day program (batch 86 and 87)

**Dates on this calendar:** 4 Oct (Sun) to 1 Nov (Sun). **This batch is already half over
(today 19 Oct).** Ads need the next batch's calendar and dates.
**Format:** 11 webinars. Brand: SkillArbitrage (`SkillArbitrage_V`).

**Webinars:** duties of an independent director and how they add value; reading balance sheets
and annual reports with AI; doubt clearing; signing up for and clearing the IICA exam and the
roadmap after it; personal brand from boardroom to TEDx; getting registered as an independent
director; two exam revision sessions; LinkedIn for independent directors; digital portfolio
masterclass; governance, ESG, AI and branding as boardroom strategy.

**What the reader becomes:** a registered independent director: in the IICA databank, exam
cleared, with a board profile and portfolio that boards can find.

**Watch-outs:** no board seat guarantee (only "eligible to apply"). Retired readers: "second
innings", never a step down (Ramanuj 28 Sep). No age or years on the face.
**Past ads:** community par Rs 336. Cheapest `Sortlist_test` Rs 208, `Orange` and `poster`
Rs 220. Free camp winners: `C4_banker` Rs 57/lead, `V3_educator_retired` Rs 66.
