# NCA enticing concepts: what stops the scroll, in plain English

7 Oct 2026. LawSikho, 31 Oct to 2 Nov 2026 bootcamp. Built from two research passes (earned-media craft of
Zomato, Swiggy, boAt, Tanmay Bhat; web voices of Indian lawyers on Canada) and the house rules.

## What travels in those brands, and what we can borrow

Most of their lift comes from puns, irony, rivalry and paid stars. Ramanuj's rules rule those out
(no riddles, no puns, one reader, no big budget). What survives:

1. **A format the reader already knows** (Zomato's political birthday hoarding, Swiggy's remade old ads). We use formats advocates know: cause list, group chat, timetable, visiting card.
2. **A peer voice, not a brand voice** (boAt mid-tier creators; our Data Protection email face).
3. **Specific mechanism** ("online, from India, every month").
4. **An invitation to forward or reply** (Swiggy "Voice of Hunger" took replies from the audience).

Not borrowed: puns, rival banter, celebrity casting, self-roast ads (hedge risk above a pay button).
No evidence anywhere that earned media converts a Rs 10 sign-up. Only live cost per result at 20+ results counts.

Footer on all: "Rs 10 live bootcamp, 3 days, refundable." NCA always glossed: "NCA: Canada's check on your Indian law degree."

## Held back, with reason

- **"NCA lawyers only get small practices"** is the one sceptic quote found (Legally India, about 5 years old, anonymous). It is the likely main objection, but we hold no evidence to answer it truthfully. Do not run an ad on it until we do.
- **"Canada PR admissions fell"** (Indian PRs down 24.8% Jan to May 2026, per Outlook India) is a fear frame and a market stat. Page only, and only as context.
- **Celebrity or rival banter** concepts: dropped.
