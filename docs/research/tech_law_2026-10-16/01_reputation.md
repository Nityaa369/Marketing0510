## What this means for ads

The Tech Law stack has no independent bad press of its own. The risk is the brand pattern a prospect
finds when they search "LawSikho review" before paying Rs 3,000 plus 18 EMIs. Ads cannot answer
these on the face (Rule 5: nothing hedges above a pay button; no "not for you if"); the landing page
and the camp must.

### The 5 strongest objections to answer

1. **"The refund is a trap."** Most repeated complaint across Voxya (2022, 2025), App Store (2025),
   Legally India and SkillArbitrage reviews. The 30-to-45-day refund needs every class attended and
   every assignment submitted, the Rs 3,000 booking fee is non-refundable, and a refund can bar
