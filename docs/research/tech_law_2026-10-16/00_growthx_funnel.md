## Readings

1. October is ahead: more paid seats already than all of August, at almost double the
   form-to-pay rate (58% vs 31%).
2. One funnel carries October: "Unused LLB: $2,000+ a month" has 48% of all paid seats. It puts
   an income figure on the face, which breaks the standing rule (no income figure on the face).
   Volume is not cost, and only Meta spend can show whether it is cheap. This is a call for
   Ramanuj, not a settled win.
3. Google form-fillers pay far less often than Meta ones in both camps (October Google funnels
   9% to 38%, apart from lp2 at 74%; Meta 71% to 94%). Google also shows high "Failed" counts
   (small towns 63: 91 failed of 831, about 11%), matching the 11.6% Google payment-failure
   rate in HISTORICAL_CONTEXT.md. Check the Google checkout and slugs before blaming creative.
4. "That someone can be you" (lp2) is the one Google funnel that pays like Meta (74%). Worth
   reading its page to see why.
5. Reader cells that pay well: comeback (79%), career break (72 to 79%), junior lawyers (75%),
   law firm tech team (78%), banking and NBFC lawyers (94% on 16). Litigator and SaaS skill
   cells on Google pay worst.
