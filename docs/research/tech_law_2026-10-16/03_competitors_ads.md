# Tech Law competitors and how they advertise (India), 16 Oct 2026

Our offer, for reference (from `docs/TECH_LAW_CAMP_FACTS_2026-10-16.md` section 5): a Rs 10, 3-day
live bootcamp (24 to 26 Oct 2026) that sells a 4-course stack (12-month tech law diploma, 6-month
US tech law and paralegal certificate, 6-month Indian data protection certificate, 8-month CIPP
readiness programme) at INR 57,000 (Day 2) or INR 58,600 (Day 3), with 18-month EMIs of about
INR 3,000.

### Where our stack sits on price (page use only, never on an ad face)

- Cheap tier (free to INR 5,000): free DPDP certificates, IALA (INR 1,999 to 2,999), Enhelion,
  Lawctopus Technology Laws (INR 4,900, stale), ASCL.
- Middle tier (INR 12,000 to 30,000): NLU Delhi x Mentblue (likely INR 12,000 to 15,000,
  unconfirmed), Lawctopus IPR and TMT (INR 19,999), DPDP plus ISO 27701 (INR 20,500 + GST).
- Our tier (INR 50,000 to 65,000): our stack at INR 57,000 to 58,600 with EMIs; DSCI DCDPO
  (INR 55,000 + GST = about INR 64,900 for non-members); IAPP training plus exam (about INR
  59,000 in 2020); a CIPP/E exam alone is USD 550.
- Implication: the only Indian sellers at our price are credential bodies (DSCI, IAPP). Against
  them our case is four courses, live classes and work samples, not the credential. Against the
  cheap tier our case cannot be price, so the Rs 10 camp has to show what the extra buys.
- Open question for the facts file: the stack is "CIPP readiness". Nothing in section 5 says the
  IAPP exam fee (USD 550) is included. Assume it is not, and never imply it is.

## 2. Ad and hook patterns seen

What competitors visibly do (from listings and paid articles, not from Meta creatives):

1. **Paid advertorials on legal news sites are the main visible channel.** NLU Delhi x Mentblue
   runs articles tagged "[Advertisement]" on LiveLaw and launch pieces on Bar & Bench
   ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/lawschool/diploma-certificate-courses/nlu-delhi-x-mentblue-launch-certificate-course-tmt-data-protection-fintech-546274)).
   IALA uses Bar & Bench too. Lawbhoomi and Lawctopus carry "Enrol by" listings that work as ads.
2. **Regulatory clock as the hook.** "As DPDP Rules take effect" (NLU Delhi x Mentblue). DPDP
   trainers sell readiness for 13 May 2027 and the November 2026 consent-manager window

## 3. Gaps we can own

Angles no competitor seen uses. Each still has to pass the CLAUDE.md critic order and trace to
the camp script.

1. **A foreign-client career, not an Indian compliance certificate.** Every Indian competitor sells
   DPDP or Indian cyber law. Nobody else seen bundles US tech law, paralegal work and
   GDPR/US/Canada/Asia privacy for Indian lawyers. Prize line: the move into tech law for foreign
   clients and what they become. (Check against Ramanuj on routing before we lean on "US".)
2. **Career, not regulator clock.** Competitors shout "DPDP Rules take effect" and "be ready by
   May 2027". Our rules ban the threat frame, which is an opening: frame the date as demand
   ("when businesses start buying this work"), as in the Data Protection wave 4 reference build.
3. **One named lawyer per ad.** Competitors address "law students, advocates, CA/CS, product
   teams" at once. Ramanuj's rule is lawyers only for Tech Law. A face that names one lawyer
   (for example a contracts or corporate lawyer whose files already touch software or data) has
   no rival in the feed.
4. **A paid live trial for Rs 10.** Others offer free workshops (Lawctopus) or straight sign-up
   (NLU Delhi, IALA). Rs 10, refundable, live only, is unique in this set. Use the BARBRI
   "preview" logic already logged in `docs/LEGAL_EDTECH_ADS_RESEARCH_2026-10-06.md`.
5. **Work samples over certificates.** Free sites made DPDP certificates worthless, and we cannot
   out-rank an NLU or DSCI on the certificate (ours is Medhavi Skills University with NSDC and
   Skill India logos). Lead with the work: assignments that become work samples, unlimited 1-on-1
   faculty calls (both in section 5). Keep the certificate off the face.
6. **CIPP readiness in rupees with EMIs.** Indian CIPP prep seen is either USD-priced (SGD 3,924
   CIPP/A course; USD 650 + GST in 2020) or exam-only (USD 550). An INR 3,000 a month route is
