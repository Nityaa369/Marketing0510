## 7. Critic flags for anyone building pages from this

1. **Harvey Legal Engineer** may be closed (Built In copy removed 11 May 2026). Say "Harvey has
   hired Legal Engineers in Bengaluru", not "is hiring", unless the live page confirms.
2. **GCC "25 to 30 percent above"** is traceable (TeamLease, Nov 2025) but it is all GCC roles
   against the national average, not legal roles. Word it that way on the page.
3. **Privacy pay (Rs 35 lakh to 80 lakh)** comes from a 132-person vendor survey of 7+ year
   professionals. Never imply a fresher or junior will earn it. Show the sample size.
4. **"60,000 privacy roles by May 2027"** is a vendor projection. If used, attribute it in the same
   sentence.
5. **Khaitan and AZB** items are departures. Do not list them as "building teams".
6. **Gaming** as an employer category needs checking against the 2025 gaming law before any page
   names it.
7. **Unused LLB and litigator readers** have the thinnest sourced evidence. Their pages currently
   lean on script stories only.
8. Facts file says DPDP deadlines "November 2026 and May 2027". That matches the Rules (consent
   managers at 12 months, most duties at 18 months). Frame as demand, not as a threat to the reader.
