06

Five words every tech contract uses

On Evening 1 you learn the five concepts behind most technology contracts. None of them needs coding.

SLAsA service level agreement sets the uptime a software provider promises, what counts as downtime, and the credits a customer gets when the promise is missed.
Support metricsHow fast a provider must answer and fix a problem, written as response and resolution times. Lawyers decide what happens when they slip.
APIsThe connection that lets one app talk to another. The contract decides who may use it, for what, and who is liable when it breaks.
Source code and object codeSource code is what programmers write. Object code is what the machine runs. Licences and ownership clauses turn on the difference.
White labellingSelling another company's product under your own brand. The agreement sets who owns what, the quality promised and who answers to the customer.

Questions lawyers ask us

I do not know anyone in this city. Can I still find work?The camp's outreach is online, to startups and lawyers in several countries. It does not depend on local contacts.
Do I have to give up the idea of practising in court here?No. Tech law can run beside a new court practice or replace it. You can decide that later, once you have seen the work.
As an enrolled advocate, can I approach startups at all?The rules restrict advertising and soliciting. The approach the camp teaches is to find a genuine issue in a public document and share a useful observation, not to ask for a brief. For anything specific, ask your State Bar Council.
My old practice was litigation. Is that enough to start?Litigation trains close reading and drafting, which are the base of contract review. The camp adds the tech side on Day 1, from SLAs and APIs to SaaS.
