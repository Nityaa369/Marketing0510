05

What tech law work looks like for an LLB holder who does not code

Picture an app that makes money from advertising. It needs a privacy policy, terms of use and agreements with ad partners. You read what user data it collects and draft the rules around it in plain English. If the app changes how it makes money, the documents change with it.

A subscription product needs clear terms on renewal, cancellation and refunds. A marketplace needs seller agreements and a rule on who answers when a third-party seller lets a buyer down. Not one line of code is involved.

A business that lives on commissions and affiliate links needs partner agreements that say who gets paid, when, and on what proof. You are reading money flows and promises, which is exactly what your degree taught. If a dispute comes later, it turns on those same promises.
