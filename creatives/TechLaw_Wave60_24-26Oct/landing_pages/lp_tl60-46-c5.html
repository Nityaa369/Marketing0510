05

What tech law work looks like for an LLB holder on Day 1

You begin with appointment: who is hired to build what. Then milestones, which break the project into stages so that payment follows progress. Then specifications, the written description of what the software must do. A loose specification leaves room for an argument later, so this clause matters.

Next comes consideration, how and when the developer is paid. Acceptance testing follows, the step where the client checks the work against the specifications before signing off. Each clause answers a question a founder would actually ask.

Finally you reach IP assignment and handover: who owns the code once it is paid for, and what must be delivered at the end. By now you are reading the deal as a business transaction, which is the real skill of tech law. The same habit carries over to service agreements, licences and the other contracts you meet later.
