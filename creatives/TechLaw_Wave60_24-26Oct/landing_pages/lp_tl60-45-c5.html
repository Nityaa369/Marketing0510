05

What tech law work looks like for a litigator who stays in court

After court, you open a startup's terms of use. You check six things: no liability for free services, liability capped at the fee for paid ones, clear termination, community standards, a link to a compliant privacy policy, and IP ownership.

You write a short note to the founder naming what is missing and why it matters if a dispute comes. Your court instinct for what a judge would ask helps you explain the risk in plain words. A founder can see a gap in their own document straight away.

Over time you could take on drafting of contracts and policies for the same founders, then the disputes that arise from them. Court remains your base, and the tech work grows beside it on your own timetable. Some weeks you will do more and some weeks less, depending on your cause list.
