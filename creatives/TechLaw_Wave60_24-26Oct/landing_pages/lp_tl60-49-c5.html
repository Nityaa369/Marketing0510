Three evenings, built to stack

The camp is designed so each evening rests on the one before. First you learn the vocabulary and draft a core contract. Then you see where the work comes from. Then you do the work and learn how to offer it.

That order matters because the problem is positioning, not effort. Your LLB already trained you to read and draft. What tech law work looks like for an LLB holder across the 3 evenings

Saturday, 6 to 9 PM IST, is about foundations. You learn five tech concepts that appear in contracts, then draft the key clauses of a software development agreement. You also see how lawyers reach startups abroad and in India, and network with US lawyers.

Sunday, 6 to 9 PM IST, is about where the work comes from. You map six ways tech companies make money and the legal work each creates. You get the six-month plan, the services e-commerce companies need, and ways to network with US, UK and Canadian lawyers.

Monday, 7 to 10 PM IST, is about doing and offering the work. You review a startup's terms and learn to pitch the fix, draft a refund policy and an influencer agreement, and learn to set up an Upwork profile for privacy work. The method we teach

1. SkillsLearn a few high-demand tasks first: contracts, website terms and policies. Not the whole field.
2. Track recordTurn each task into a work sample, a short article or a post that shows what you can do.
3. OutreachOffer useful help to startups and to lawyers abroad, deliver well, and ask for a testimonial.

The plan is about 2 hours a day: one hour learning, one hour outreach. No coding is needed. The work follows the business model.
