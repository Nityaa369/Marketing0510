How it works

The route, in 3 steps

NCA exams, from India

NCA is Canada's check on your Indian law degree. You write its exams online, from India. We show you what they cover and how to choose your first one.

2

Bar licensing

After the NCA, a province's law society licenses lawyers. Each province has its own steps. We show you the order, so nothing surprises you later.

Practise in Canada

With a Canadian qualification you can apply to practise. We show you what comes after the exams and where each path leads.

Why you can start from India

  1. Both systems are common law. Canada outside Quebec runs on common law, like India. The subjects will feel familiar, though you still have to learn Canadian law.Source: Justice Canada.
  2. The exams are online and monthly. You write them from home, in India. You choose the month.Source: nca.legal.
  3. The NCA decides your exams after it assesses your degree. Core subjects usually include Canadian constitutional law, administrative law, criminal law, professional responsibility and foundations of Canadian law. Your own list comes from the NCA.SCRIPT-CHECK: subjects and open book format from the April 2024 script; confirm for 2026
  4. The exams are open book. You are marked on applying concepts to a fact pattern, not on memory.SCRIPT-CHECK: confirm open book and 50% pass mark for 2026
