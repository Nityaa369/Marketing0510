1. The problem

Why you, and why now

You built a practice from nothing. Now your clients grow, do business abroad, sign English-law contracts, and for that work they go to someone else.

A practice owner who is also an English-law solicitor keeps that work, and can take on referral work from UK firms that cannot hire.

Your seniority is also your exemption evidence: years of practice, your own name on the letterhead, and the SRA's list of qualifying practice areas.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  2. No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  3. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  4. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  5. About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
  6. UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
  7. 57 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
    Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
  8. Awards under English law travel.Arbitral awards are enforceable in more than 170 countries under the New York Convention.
    Source: UN Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards, 1958
  9. You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
    Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  10. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
  11. Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
  12. The exemption application costs GBP 265.That fee covers one or both exemption requests. If SQE2 is waived you avoid an assessment fee of about GBP 3,000.
    Source: sra.org.uk, Apply for an individual SQE assessment exemption; SQE fees 2026
