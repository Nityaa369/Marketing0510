1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Domestic arbitration in India is a good practice. It is also a crowded one, and the cross-border matters, with the real fees, are run by English-law solicitors from London and Singapore.

That is structural. 78 percent of LCIA cases in 2024 were on English-law contracts. English law is the most common governing law in new ICC cases and applies in a quarter of SIAC cases. London is the world's preferred seat.

The pool of English-qualified arbitration lawyers is the bottleneck. You can be in it.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. 78 percent of LCIA arbitrations in 2024 were on English-law contracts.English law is also the single most common governing law in new ICC cases, and applies in a quarter of SIAC cases.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025; LCIA, ICC and SIAC annual casework reports 2024
  2. London is the world's first-choice arbitration seat.34 percent of practitioners name it their preferred seat, and it was the most popular ICC venue in 2024.
    Source: 2025 International Arbitration Survey, Queen Mary University of London and White and Case
  3. Awards under English law travel.Arbitral awards are enforceable in more than 170 countries under the New York Convention.
    Source: UN Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards, 1958
  4. About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
  5. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  6. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  7. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  8. You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
    Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  9. UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
  10. No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  11. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
  12. Firms shortlist on profile before the interview.Day 5 builds your international profile: the LinkedIn, the CV and the work samples that get a response from UK and Gulf firms.
