1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Criminal practice is relentless. Bail at ten, a trial at two, a remand at five, and clients who cannot always pay. Your skill is real, and it is locked to one court complex.

England and Wales has the opposite problem. The number of duty solicitors has fallen by a quarter since 2017, four in ten are over 55 and only six in a hundred are under 35. Whole counties have no junior criminal solicitor at all.

A qualified criminal advocate from India, with English-law qualification, walks into that gap with exactly the skills it needs.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. Criminal law is one of the SQE1 subjects.FLK2 covers criminal law and practice. Your bail and trial work is a head start, not a handicap.
    Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  2. The UK has fewer solicitors coming through.Duty solicitors fell 26 percent since 2017, 40 percent are now 55 or older, and only 6 percent are under 35. That is demand, and it is why firms look abroad.
    Source: The Law Society, criminal duty solicitors campaign; Law Gazette
  3. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  4. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  5. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  6. No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  7. You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
    Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  8. SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
    Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  9. 57 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
    Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
  10. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
  11. Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
  12. UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
