1. The problem

Why you, and why now

The first years at the bar are the leanest. You are learning the courts, earning little and watching seniors take the interesting work. Everyone tells you to wait your turn.

There is a second clock running. The SQE2 exemption needs two years of experience, and SQE1 needs about a year of part-time study. Start both now and the two clocks finish together.

By the time you are a three-year advocate, you could also be a solicitor of England and Wales, with a market most of your batch never considered.

Why this could be the most important investment you make this year

Rs 100 for seven live evenings is less than one court fee. It gives you a plan that uses the slow years instead of waiting them out.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  2. No UK degree is required.The route asks for a degree or an equivalent qualification in any subject, plus the SQE passes. Your Indian law degree counts.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  3. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  4. SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
    Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  5. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
  6. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  7. You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
    Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  8. 16,200 and more candidates sat the SQE in 2024 to 2025.You are joining a route that thousands of lawyers already take every year.
    Source: SRA, SQE assessment statistics 2024 to 2025
  9. About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
  10. 57 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
    Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
  11. Firms shortlist on profile before the interview.Day 5 builds your international profile: the LinkedIn, the CV and the work samples that get a response from UK and Gulf firms.
  12. Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
