1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Ten or fifteen years in, your name carries weight in your city. That is also the ceiling: your fees are set by what your local market can pay, and growth means more hours of the same work.

Most senior advocates assume a foreign qualification means stopping, moving and starting again. For an enrolled advocate with years of practice the route is the opposite. Your experience is the asset. The SRA can waive the practical exam, SQE2, on the strength of it.

That leaves one written exam, taken in India, around your practice.

Why this could be the most important investment you make this year

Seven live evenings at Rs 100 will tell you, with the SRA's own criteria, whether your practice already qualifies you for the exemption. If it does, the hardest part of the route is already behind you.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

1Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
2No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
3The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
4One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
Source: SRA
5About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
6UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
757 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
8You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
9The exemption application costs GBP 265.That fee covers one or both exemption requests. If SQE2 is waived you avoid an assessment fee of about GBP 3,000.
Source: sra.org.uk, Apply for an individual SQE assessment exemption; SQE fees 2026
10Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
11Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
12London is the world's first-choice arbitration seat.34 percent of practitioners name it their preferred seat, and it was the most popular ICC venue in 2024.
Source: 2025 International Arbitration Survey, Queen Mary University of London and White and Case
