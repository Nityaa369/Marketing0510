1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Most lawyers who want England plan it backwards: get there first, then figure out the qualification. That means unpaid years, visa pressure and a job below your level while you study.

The SQE lets you reverse the order. SQE1 is written in India. The SQE2 exemption, if granted, removes the only part that needs a UK trip. You can arrive already qualified, or close to it.

And England needs you. 57 percent of firms report difficulty hiring, and the solicitor pipeline outside London has thinned for a decade.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  2. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  3. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  4. No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  5. 57 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
    Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
  6. The UK has fewer solicitors coming through.Duty solicitors fell 26 percent since 2017, 40 percent are now 55 or older, and only 6 percent are under 35. That is demand, and it is why firms look abroad.
    Source: The Law Society, criminal duty solicitors campaign; Law Gazette
  7. UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
    Source: Law Society, International Data Insights Report 2025
  8. No UK degree is required.The route asks for a degree or an equivalent qualification in any subject, plus the SQE passes. Your Indian law degree counts.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  9. SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
    Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  10. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: Day 2 session, Your roadmap
  11. Firms shortlist on profile before the interview.Day 5 builds your international profile: the LinkedIn, the CV and the work samples that get a response from UK and Gulf firms.
    Source: Day 5 session
  12. Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
    Source: Day 6 Launch Lab
