1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Insolvency and banking work in India is sophisticated and it is domestic. The lender is Indian, the borrower is Indian, the tribunal is Indian. The international restructurings, the ones with foreign bondholders and English-law facility agreements, go to firms in London and Singapore.

Those agreements are governed by English law because that is what the finance market trusts. About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on it.

An NCLT practitioner who is also an English-law solicitor can sit on the international side of the same restructuring.
