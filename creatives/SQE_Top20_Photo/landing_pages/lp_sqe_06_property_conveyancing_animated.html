1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Conveyancing in India is volume work. Title searches, sale deeds, lease registrations, and a fee that has not moved in years. The work is careful and it is undervalued.

In England and Wales, property is a core solicitor practice area, and one of the areas where rural firms struggle hardest to recruit. The SRA lists property practice among the areas that can support an SQE2 exemption.

Your title work is both your exam preparation and your exemption evidence.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

1Land law and property practice are on the exam.FLK2 covers property practice and land law. Titles, deeds and leases are familiar ground.
Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
2Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
3One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
Source: SRA
4The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
5No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
6The UK has fewer solicitors coming through.Duty solicitors fell 26 percent since 2017, 40 percent are now 55 or older, and only 6 percent are under 35. That is demand, and it is why firms look abroad.
Source: The Law Society, criminal duty solicitors campaign; Law Gazette
757 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
8You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
9SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
10Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
11Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
12UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
