2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. Business law is the first paper.FLK1 covers business organisations, rules and procedures, plus contract and dispute resolution. Deal lawyers start ahead.
    Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  2. About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
    Source: Law Society, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
  3. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  4. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  5. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  6. In-house counsel qualify too.The SRA counts professional legal work experience, not only courtroom practice, toward the two years for the exemption application.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  7. UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
    Source: Law Society, International Data Insights Report 2025
  8. 78 percent of LCIA arbitrations in 2024 were on English-law contracts.English law is also the single most common governing law in new ICC cases, and applies in a quarter of SIAC cases.
    Source: Law Society report 2025; LCIA, ICC and SIAC 2024 casework
  9. No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  10. 57 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
    Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
  11. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: Day 2 session, Your roadmap
  12. Firms shortlist on profile before the interview.Day 5 builds your international profile: the LinkedIn, the CV and the work samples that get a response from UK and Gulf firms.
    Source: Day 5 session
