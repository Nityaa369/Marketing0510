1. The problem

Why you, and why now

Civil practice in India is slow money. A consumer complaint or a contract suit can take years, and your fee arrives at the end of it. You spend your best hours on adjournments.

The law you argue is English law by another name. The Indian Contract Act was drafted on English principles. Indian tort is English common law, uncodified. Negligence, nuisance and vicarious liability are the same cases.

Which means the exam that opens a global market tests what you already practise, and the practice itself is your exemption evidence.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

1You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
2One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
Source: SRA
3The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
4Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
5SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
6About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
778 percent of LCIA arbitrations in 2024 were on English-law contracts.English law is also the single most common governing law in new ICC cases, and applies in a quarter of SIAC cases.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025; LCIA, ICC and SIAC annual casework reports 2024
8Awards under English law travel.Arbitral awards are enforceable in more than 170 countries under the New York Convention.
Source: UN Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards, 1958
9No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
1057 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
11Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
12Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
