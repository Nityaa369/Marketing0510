1. The problem

Why you, and why now

You are good in court. You win more than you lose. And each year looks like the last one: the same corridors, the same adjournments, the same fee pressure from clients who pay late. Roughly 60,000 law graduates enter the Indian market every year, all chasing the same clients, and reputation in Indian practice takes ten to fifteen years to compound.

The problem is not your skill. It is the size of the market you are standing in. A lawyer in a market worth a few billion rupees and a lawyer in a market worth tens of billions of dollars are not playing the same game, even with identical ability.

An English-law qualification does not replace your practice. It adds a second market to it. Clients today ask whether you are qualified under English law, not where you are sitting.

Why this could be the most important investment you make this year

For Rs 100 you get seven live evenings that show you the whole route, from registration to admission, and whether the SQE2 exemption is realistic for you. That is the cheapest due diligence you will ever do on a qualification that can double the market you serve.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

1One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
Source: SRA
2The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
3Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
4You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
5About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
678 percent of LCIA arbitrations in 2024 were on English-law contracts.English law is also the single most common governing law in new ICC cases, and applies in a quarter of SIAC cases.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025; LCIA, ICC and SIAC annual casework reports 2024
7London is the world's first-choice arbitration seat.34 percent of practitioners name it their preferred seat, and it was the most popular ICC venue in 2024.
Source: 2025 International Arbitration Survey, Queen Mary University of London and White and Case
857 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
9No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
10SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
11Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
12Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
