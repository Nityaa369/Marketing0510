1. The problem

Why you, and why now

You draft well, you close on time and you are trusted by partners. Then a cross-border matter comes in and it goes to the London office, or to an English-qualified lateral hire.

That is not about ability. It is about qualification. Clients on international matters want a lawyer qualified under English law on the file.

An associate who adds that qualification, from inside the firm, is the one the partners send the cross-border work to.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

  1. About 40 percent of global business and financial transactions run on English law.Parties choose it because the case law is mature and the courts are trusted.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025, citing TheCityUK
  2. One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
    Source: SRA
  3. The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
    Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
  4. Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  5. In-house counsel qualify too.The SRA counts professional legal work experience, not only courtroom practice, toward the two years for the exemption application.
    Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
  6. Business law is the first paper.FLK1 covers business organisations, rules and procedures, plus contract and dispute resolution. Deal lawyers start ahead.
    Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
  7. 57 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
    Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
  8. UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
    Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
  9. No two years of UK work experience for you.Qualified lawyers do not need the two years of qualifying work experience that fresh graduates must complete.
    Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
  10. Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
    Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
  11. Firms shortlist on profile before the interview.Day 5 builds your international profile: the LinkedIn, the CV and the work samples that get a response from UK and Gulf firms.
  12. Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
