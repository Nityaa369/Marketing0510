1. The problem

Why you, and why now

In a smaller city the ceiling is low and visible. The clients are local, the fees are local, and the lawyers who grow are the ones who leave for Delhi or Mumbai.

There is a route that does not require moving. The SQE written exams are taken at test centres in six Indian cities. Preparation is reading, daily MCQs and live evening sessions. The exemption file is paper.

And the UK's own shortage is in its smaller towns, where firms cannot fill roles. A qualified lawyer who already knows small-city practice is exactly who they need.

2. Once you see it

Did you know? 12 reasons this is worth your attention

1The written exams are taken in India.SQE1 and the written part of SQE2 run at Pearson VUE test centres, including six Indian cities. Only the SQE2 oral sittings require a trip to England or Wales.
Source: sqe.sra.org.uk, test centre locations
2One exam, recognised worldwide.The SQE is the single national exam to qualify as a solicitor of England and Wales. It replaced the older routes and is open to qualified lawyers from other countries, including India.
Source: SRA
3No UK degree is required.The route asks for a degree or an equivalent qualification in any subject, plus the SQE passes. Your Indian law degree counts.
Source: SRA, Qualified lawyers
4The UK has fewer solicitors coming through.Duty solicitors fell 26 percent since 2017, 40 percent are now 55 or older, and only 6 percent are under 35. That is demand, and it is why firms look abroad.
Source: The Law Society, criminal duty solicitors campaign; Law Gazette
557 percent of UK law firms say they struggle to hire.56 percent of in-house teams report the same. A firm that cannot hire does not care where a qualified English-law lawyer sits.
Source: The Access Group, The 10 biggest challenges facing the legal profession
6Enrolled advocates can apply to skip SQE2.A qualified lawyer with at least two years of professional experience can apply to the SRA for an SQE2 exemption. If granted, it removes five half-days of assessment, the SQE2 fee and the UK trip. The SRA decides each case.
Source: sra.org.uk, SQE exemptions
7You already think in common law.The Indian Contract Act of 1872 was drafted on English principles, and Indian tort law is largely uncodified English common law. The same cases you cite are on the SQE1 syllabus.
Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872; SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
8SQE1 is 360 multiple-choice questions.Two papers, FLK1 and FLK2, closed book, on a computer. It rewards method, which is what a daily MCQ habit builds.
Source: SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification
9Most learners pass SQE1 within about a year of starting.A typical plan is nine to twelve months of part-time study, around work. The next realistic window for most of this group is SQE1 in July 2027.
Source: SRA, SQE assessment dates
10UK legal services earn about GBP 11 billion a year abroad.The UK is the second largest legal market in the world, behind only the United States.
Source: The Law Society of England and Wales, International Data Insights Report 2025
11Firms shortlist on profile before the interview.Day 5 builds your international profile: the LinkedIn, the CV and the work samples that get a response from UK and Gulf firms.
12Lawyers who moved first did it from their desks.Our members cleared SQE1 while in practice in India, then applied for the exemption with their own work samples and reference letters.
