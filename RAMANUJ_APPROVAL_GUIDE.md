# What Ramanuj approves

Distilled 6 Oct 2026 from `RAMANUJ_FEEDBACK.md` in the 5 Oct ads starter kit, which records his
verdicts from 7 Jul to 28 Sep 2026. Quotes are his words, typos included.

## The one test

A half-asleep stranger must get, in about 3 seconds: **who it is for, what better job they move
into, and that saying yes is easy** (Rs 10, live, refundable).

When an ad with a good audience fails, the problem is the offer, not the audience: *"Nothing
wrong with ICP / ICP is perfect / What you are offering to them / Not something they want"*
(12 Aug).

## 1. The audience (ICP)

| Approves | Rejects |
|---|---|
| A real occupation or life situation: loan consultants, single mothers, recently retired, court litigators | Wishes or broad groups: "women looking to learn AI skills" |
| A title people know, with the specialism in the duties | Invented US titles: *"those jobs don't really seem real, I doubt anyone is hiring"*, *"generalise the roles"* |
| Readers with a reason to buy now: *"willing to learn a new skill or are desperate in some way"* | Freelancers: *"this audience does not convert - always remember to exclude them"* |
| Lawyers for Tech Law | Non-lawyers for Tech Law: *"non-lawyer ICPs do not work for tech law"* (24 Sep) |
| Batches of new audiences: *"try to come up with 10 new ICPs first"* | A practising credential as the identity (VA to a dietitian reads as a demotion) |

## 2. The offer

He approves a **new, better career**. He rejects a **threat or more work at the same desk**.

Data Privacy, 12 Aug, nine audiences on one template:

| Approved | Rejected |
|---|---|
| "Tired of targets? Move into data privacy instead" | "every call you make to a borrower is now data processing": *"offer a different career to this person not threaten with a regulator"* |
| "Start a privacy analyst career from home" | "your payroll vendor is now a data processor, on paper or not": *"Not fine"* |
| "Become the DPO your hospital will need in 2027" | "the PAN and bank details you collect now need a data agreement": *"Same - not ok"* |

- A threat in the body overrides a good headline (the coaching ad, "Become the DPO…", was
  rejected for "targeted advertising to children is banned outright").
- Customer support (#7) passed with the rejected shape. It is an inconsistency, not a rule.
- Name the prize: *"where does it say build a career in corporate finance / join what / the
  prize is - get into corporate finance, earn more, get a career upgrade"* (21 Sep, 19 of 19
  rejected).
- Retired readers: *"it should feel like a second inning, not a step down"* (28 Sep).

## 3. The copy

| Approves | Rejects |
|---|---|
| Audience named first, big: *"the icp comes first"* | Hook before the audience: *"the flipping burger thing is not clear as a hook - who are you talking to and why"* |
| *"normal easy to understand english"* | *"too clever with the image and the hook"* |
| Full sentence or question: "HOW TO GET YOUR FIRST BOARD SEAT" | Fragments: "ONE PREPARATION. FIVE BOARD ROLES." (*"omg again cryptic"*) |
| Destination headline: "A global legal career, from home" | Riddles: *"sounds like a riddle"* |
| Desire: "WANT to break into the patent industry?" | Pain: "STRUGGLING to…" |
| Headline about the job | Headline about buying: "Read all three afternoons, then decide" (*"cryptic, we can drop it"*) |
| Few words | *"wayyy too much text"* |
| Open on Rs 10, then the value fast | Broken sentences: *"what does this sentence even mean?"*, *"how do you outsource a firm??"* |
| Income as a direction: "earn in dollars" | Figures that describe the viewer: *"KILLS AD PERFORMANCE"* |
| "You are in the right room if…" | "Not for you if…": *"i hate this never do this"* |

"Cryptic" has two causes: the hook comes before the reader is named (fix the order), or the
qualifier row leaves people out (widen it). If both are fine and the field is defined, it is not
clarity, it is the offer.

## 4. Design and pages

- Indian faces only. *"distinct colour pallete"* per ad. Light backgrounds. Varied looks.
- *"you write small texts in blind spots that is practically invisible"*: no small or grey type.
- No empty gaps. A visible buy button on every face.
- *"look through the design styles of past best performing ads and redo the designs."*
- *"But where are the landing pages"*: always ship the page with the ad.
- Value in the first fold, in the ad's words. Full argument: *"super short, deliver no value"*.
- Pages in a kit must differ: *"the lp is the same over and over again, making them
  disinterested."*
- One section names the reader's role. Facts from the script: *"corporate finance work is not
  valuation - please read the bootcamp script."*
- No hedge above the pay button: *"At least read what crap you are sending to me."*
- Media: max 5 ads per ad set; our campaigns must never bid against each other; judge on cost
  per result over a full window, never CTR or a part-day.

## 6. Caveats

1. Only his verdict counts. Ruchika's or an in-house label is not approval.
2. Gates passing is not approval: *"This ad sucks but the gates caught no real issues. It is a
   total fail."*
3. His approval is not a fact-check. "The desk with no seniors" was approved and overclaims.
4. Send the cheapest thing first: audience list, then value props, hooks, one pair, the wave.
